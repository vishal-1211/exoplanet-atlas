GJ 3138 b

GJ 3138 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 93 light-years away, orbiting GJ 3138. Discovered in 2017 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.2 days. It is 1.18 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.2 days, so about 299.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 92.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 93 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.18× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.78× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 903 K (630 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 118.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.02 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
