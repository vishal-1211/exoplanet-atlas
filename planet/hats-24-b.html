HATS-24 b

HATS-24 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,612 light-years away, orbiting HATS-24. Discovered in 2017 by transit. A year there lasts 1.3 days. It is 15.64 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.3 days, so about 270.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,612 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,612 years ago.
  • Its radius is 15.6× Earth's radius, or 1.4× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.63% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.26× Jupiter in mass (718.3× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,166 K (1,893 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 2,707 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.024 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
