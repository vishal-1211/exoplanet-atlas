HD 82943 b

HD 82943 b is a gas giant exoplanet 90 light-years away, orbiting HD 82943. Discovered in 2003 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.2 years. It is 13.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.2 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 90 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 90 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.5× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.09% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.68× Jupiter in mass (534.3× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 286 K (13 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits HD 82943, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,935 K.
  • HD 82943 has 2 known planets.
