K2-352 d

K2-352 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 574 light-years away, orbiting K2-352. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 14.9 days. It is 2.23 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 14.9 days, so about 24.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 574.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 574 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.23× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.6× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 79 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 727 K (454 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 66.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
