11 UMi b

11 UMi b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 409 light-years away, orbiting 11 UMi. Discovered in 2009 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.4 years. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.4 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 408.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 409 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0014% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 14.7× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 896 K (623 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 114.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits 11 UMi, a orange (K) star at 4,213 K.
