TOI-880.02

TOI-880.02 is a mini-neptune exoplanet 198 light-years away, orbiting TOI-880. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 2.6 days. It is 2.19 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.6 days, so about 141.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 197.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 198 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.19× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.43× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,085 K (812 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 301.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.035 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
