Kepler-763 c

Kepler-763 c is a super-earth exoplanet 3,139 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-763. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 4.1 days. It is 1.42 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.1 days, so about 89.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,139 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,139 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.42× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 938 K (665 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 182.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.047 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
