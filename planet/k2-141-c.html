K2-141 c

K2-141 c is a gas giant exoplanet 202 light-years away, orbiting K2-141. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 7.7 days. It is 7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.7 days, so about 47.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 201.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 202 years ago.
  • Its radius is 7× Earth's radius, or 0.62× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.89% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.13 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 695 K (422 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 39.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
