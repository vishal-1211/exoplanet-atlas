K2-209 b

K2-209 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 250 light-years away, orbiting K2-209. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 2.1 days. It is 0.87 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.1 days, so about 175.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 249.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 250 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.87× Earth's radius).
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.59× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 1,130 K (857 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 271.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.029 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
