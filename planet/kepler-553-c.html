Kepler-553 c

Kepler-553 c is a gas giant exoplanet 2,376 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-553. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 328.2 days. It is 11.58 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 328.2 days, so about 1.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,376 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,376 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.6× Earth's radius, or 1.03× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.38% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 6.7× Jupiter in mass (2,129× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 251 K (-22 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits Kepler-553, a orange (K) star at 5,191 K.
  • Kepler-553 has 2 known planets.
