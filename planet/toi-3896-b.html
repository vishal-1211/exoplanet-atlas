TOI-3896 b

TOI-3896 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 592 light-years away, orbiting TOI-3896. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 4.4 days. It is 3.15 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.4 days, so about 83.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 592.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 592 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.15× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 10.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 966 K (693 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 145 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.05 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
