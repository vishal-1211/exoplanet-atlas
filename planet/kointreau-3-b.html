KOINTREAU-3 b

KOINTREAU-3 b is a gas giant exoplanet 458 light-years away, orbiting ISO-Oph 96. Discovered in 2026 by imaging. A year there lasts 9,878 years. It is 13.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9,878 years, so a single orbit takes 9,878 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 458.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 458 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.1× Earth's radius, or 1.17× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.69% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.4× Jupiter in mass (1,081× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 9 K (-264 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 342 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
