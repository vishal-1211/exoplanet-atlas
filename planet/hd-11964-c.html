HD 11964 c

HD 11964 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 109 light-years away, orbiting HD 11964. Discovered in 2008 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 37.9 days. It is 5.38 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 37.9 days, so about 9.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 109.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 109 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.38× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 25× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 758 K (484 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 54.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.229 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
