OGLE-2006-BLG-284L A b

OGLE-2006-BLG-284L A b is a gas giant exoplanet 13,046 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2006-BLG-284L A. Discovered in 2020 by microlensing. A year there lasts 5.4 years. It is 14.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.4 years, so a single orbit takes 5.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 13,046 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 13,046 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.3× Earth's radius, or 1.28× Jupiter's.
  • It is 144× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.27 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • OGLE-2006-BLG-284L A is part of a system with 2 stars.
  • It was one of 234 planets discovered in 2020.
