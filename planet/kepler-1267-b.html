Kepler-1267 b

Kepler-1267 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 4,499 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1267. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 13 days. It is 3.01 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 13 days, so about 28 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 4,499 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 4,499 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.01× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 9.32× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 774 K (501 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 86.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.106 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
