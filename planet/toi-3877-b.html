TOI-3877 b

TOI-3877 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,610 light-years away, orbiting TOI-3877. Discovered in 2025 by transit. A year there lasts 4.1 days. It is 11.68 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.1 days, so about 88.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,610 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,610 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.7× Earth's radius, or 1.04× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.55% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 99.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.35 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,445 K (1,172 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 729 times as much starlight as Earth does.
