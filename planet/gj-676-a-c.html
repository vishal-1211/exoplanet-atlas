GJ 676 A c

GJ 676 A c is a super-jupiter exoplanet 52 light-years away, orbiting GJ 676 A. Discovered in 2016 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 38.1 years. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 38.1 years, so a single orbit takes 38.1 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 52.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 52 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.66% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 13.5× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 48 K (-225 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.1% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits GJ 676 A, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,734 K.
