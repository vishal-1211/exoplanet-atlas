TOI-201 d

TOI-201 d is a super-earth exoplanet 371 light-years away, orbiting TOI-201. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 5.8 days. It is 1.39 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.8 days, so about 62.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 371.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 371 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.39× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0094% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.8× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 3× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 210 kg.
  • Its average density is 11.9 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,237 K (964 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
