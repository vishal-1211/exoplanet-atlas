TOI-1634 c

TOI-1634 c is a not identified exoplanet 115 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1634. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 103 days.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 103 days, so about 3.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 115 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 115 years ago.
  • Its estimated temperature is 196 K (-78 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.33 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits TOI-1634, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,472 K.
  • TOI-1634 has 2 known planets.
  • TOI-1634 is part of a system with 2 stars.
  • It was one of 367 planets discovered in 2022.
