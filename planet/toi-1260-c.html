TOI-1260 c

TOI-1260 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 240 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1260. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 7.5 days. It is 2.76 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.5 days, so about 48.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 240 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 240 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.76× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.14% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 13.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 651 K (378 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 29.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.066 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
