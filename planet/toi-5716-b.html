TOI-5716 b

TOI-5716 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 129 light-years away, orbiting TOI-5716. Discovered in 2025 by transit. A year there lasts 6.8 days. It is 0.96 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.8 days, so about 54 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 128.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 129 years ago.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.16% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.84× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 400 K (127 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 3.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.04 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
