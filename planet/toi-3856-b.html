TOI-3856 b

TOI-3856 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,026 light-years away, orbiting TOI-3856. Discovered in 2025 by transit. A year there lasts 2 days. It is 13.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2 days, so about 178.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,026 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,026 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.6× Earth's radius, or 1.21× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.39% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.54× Jupiter in mass (171.6× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.38 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,544 K (1,271 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 949 times as much starlight as Earth does.
