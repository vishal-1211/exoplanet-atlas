Kepler-1544 b

Kepler-1544 b is a super-earth exoplanet 1,093 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1544. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 168.8 days. It is 1.78 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 168.8 days, so about 2.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,093 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,093 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.78× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 3.82× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 269 K (-4 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits where starlight is in a range that could allow liquid water on a rocky planet. That does not mean it is habitable: its atmosphere and surface are unknown.
  • It orbits Kepler-1544, a orange (K) star at 4,886 K.
