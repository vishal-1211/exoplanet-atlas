Kepler-34 b

Kepler-34 b is a gas giant exoplanet 5,873 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-34. Discovered in 2011 by transit. A year there lasts 288.8 days. It is 8.56 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 288.8 days, so about 1.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 5,873 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 5,873 years ago.
  • Its radius is 8.56× Earth's radius, or 0.76× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.46% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 69.9× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.61 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 294 K (21 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits Kepler-34, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,913 K.
