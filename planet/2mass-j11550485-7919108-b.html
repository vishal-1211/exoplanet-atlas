2MASS J11550485-7919108 b

2MASS J11550485-7919108 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 329 light-years away, orbiting 2MASS J11550485-7919108. Discovered in 2024 by imaging. A year there lasts 18,540 years. It is 12.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 18,540 years, so a single orbit takes 18,540 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 329 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 329 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.1× Earth's radius, or 1.08× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 3.41% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 20× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,506 K (2,233 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 587 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
