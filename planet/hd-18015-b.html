HD 18015 b

HD 18015 b is a gas giant exoplanet 404 light-years away, orbiting HD 18015. Discovered in 2018 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 6.2 years. It is 13.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.2 years, so a single orbit takes 6.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 403.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 404 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.1× Earth's radius, or 1.17× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.3× Jupiter in mass (1,049× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 243 K (-30 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 18015, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,603 K.
  • It was one of 308 planets discovered in 2018.
