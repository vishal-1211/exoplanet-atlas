Kepler-978 b

Kepler-978 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 2,226 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-978. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 49.6 days. It is 2.99 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 49.6 days, so about 7.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,226 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,226 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.99× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 9.22× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 546 K (273 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 21 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.273 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
