TOI-4458 b

TOI-4458 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,648 light-years away, orbiting TOI-4458. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 3.9 days. It is 12.41 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.9 days, so about 92.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,648 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,648 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.4× Earth's radius, or 1.11× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.34% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.74× Jupiter in mass (552.1× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,759 K (1,486 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,595 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.052 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
