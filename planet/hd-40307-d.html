HD 40307 d

HD 40307 d is a gas giant exoplanet 42 light-years away, orbiting HD 40307. Discovered in 2009 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 20.4 days. It is 12.76 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 20.4 days, so about 17.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 42.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 42 years ago.
  • It weighs 5.59× Jupiter in mass (1,775× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 541 K (268 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 13.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.132 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits HD 40307, a orange (K) star at 4,956 K.
  • HD 40307 has 5 known planets.
