KIC 3526061 b

KIC 3526061 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 1,309 light-years away, orbiting KIC 3526061. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 9.7 years. It is 12.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.7 years, so a single orbit takes 9.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 1,309 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,309 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.1× Earth's radius, or 1.08× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 18.1× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 249 K (-25 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.85). It is 12 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits KIC 3526061, a orange (K) star at 4,829 K.
