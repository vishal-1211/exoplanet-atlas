TOI-1768.01

TOI-1768.01 is a mini-neptune exoplanet 514 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1768. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 7.3 days. It is 3.45 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.3 days, so about 49.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 514 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 514 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.45× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,566 K (1,293 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 240.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.076 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
