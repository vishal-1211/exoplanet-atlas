HD 35843 c

HD 35843 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 227 light-years away, orbiting HD 35843. Discovered in 2025 by transit. A year there lasts 47 days. It is 2.54 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 47 days, so about 7.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 227 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 227 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.54× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11.3× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 479 K (206 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 11.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.25 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
