PSR B1257+12 c

PSR B1257+12 c is a super-earth exoplanet 1,957 light-years away, orbiting PSR B1257+12. Discovered in 1992 by pulsar timing. A year there lasts 66.5 days. It is 1.91 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • Among the very first confirmed planets outside our Solar System (1992), found around a pulsar, the leftover core of an exploded star.
  • One year here lasts 66.5 days, so about 5.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,957 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,957 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.91× Earth's radius.
  • It is 4.3× Earth's mass.
  • It orbits at 0.36 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • PSR B1257+12 has 3 known planets.
  • It was discovered in 1992, when only a handful of planets outside our Solar System were known.
