TOI-4898 b

TOI-4898 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 322 light-years away, orbiting TOI-4898. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 2.8 days. It is 3.72 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.8 days, so about 132.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 321.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 322 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.72× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.28% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 13.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 667 K (394 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 33 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.033 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
