PSR B0329+54 b

PSR B0329+54 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 1,647 light-years away, orbiting PSR B0329+54. Discovered in 2017 by pulsar timing. A year there lasts 27.8 years. It is 1.22 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 27.8 years, so a single orbit takes 27.8 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 1,647 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,647 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.22× Earth's radius.
  • It is 1.97× Earth's mass.
  • It was one of 152 planets discovered in 2017.
