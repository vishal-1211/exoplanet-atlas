HD 116029 c

HD 116029 c is a gas giant exoplanet 402 light-years away, orbiting HD 116029. Discovered in 2016 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.5 years. It is 13.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.5 years, so a single orbit takes 2.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 402 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 402 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.7× Earth's radius, or 1.22× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.27× Jupiter in mass (403.6× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.86 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its estimated temperature is 361 K (87 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
