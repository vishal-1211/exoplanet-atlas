ome Ser b

ome Ser b is a gas giant exoplanet 250 light-years away, orbiting ome Ser. Discovered in 2013 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 278.6 days. It is 14 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 278.6 days, so about 1.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 250.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 250 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14× Earth's radius, or 1.25× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.74× Jupiter in mass (233.9× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 825 K (552 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 77.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits ome Ser, a orange (K) star at 4,724 K.
