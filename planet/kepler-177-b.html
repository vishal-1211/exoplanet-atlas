Kepler-177 b

Kepler-177 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 4,682 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-177. Discovered in 2013 by transit. A year there lasts 36.9 days. It is 3.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 36.9 days, so about 9.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 4,682 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 4,682 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.5× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.84× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.75 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 549 K (276 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 30.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
