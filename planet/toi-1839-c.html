TOI-1839 c

TOI-1839 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 379 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1839. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 4 days. It is 2.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4 days, so about 90.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 378.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 379 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.5× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,121 K (848 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 263 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.047 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
