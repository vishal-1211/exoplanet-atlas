psi1 Dra B b

psi1 Dra B b is a gas giant exoplanet 74 light-years away, orbiting psi1 Dra B. Discovered in 2015 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 8.5 years. It is 13.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.5 years, so a single orbit takes 8.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 74.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 74 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.5× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.97% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.53× Jupiter in mass (486.3× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 159 K (-114 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits psi1 Dra B, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,212 K.
  • psi1 Dra B is part of a system with 3 stars.
