Kepler-103 b

Kepler-103 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,614 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-103. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 16 days. It is 3.49 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 16 days, so about 22.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,614 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,614 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.49× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11.7× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 973 K (700 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 150.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.133 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
