Kepler-563 b

Kepler-563 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,843 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-563. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 22.2 days. It is 3.09 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 22.2 days, so about 16.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,843 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,843 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.09× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.14% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 9.75× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 546 K (273 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 18.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.142 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
