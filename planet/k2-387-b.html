K2-387 b

K2-387 b is a gas giant exoplanet 869 light-years away, orbiting K2-387. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 28.7 days. It is 7.32 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 28.7 days, so about 12.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 868.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 869 years ago.
  • Its radius is 7.32× Earth's radius, or 0.65× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.31% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 42.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 338 K (65 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.152 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
