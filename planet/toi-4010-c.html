TOI-4010 c

TOI-4010 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 579 light-years away, orbiting TOI-4010. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 5.4 days. It is 5.93 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.4 days, so about 67.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 578.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 579 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.93× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.43% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 20.3× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.54 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 907 K (634 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 112 times as much starlight as Earth does.
