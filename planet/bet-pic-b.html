bet Pic b

bet Pic b is a gas giant exoplanet 64 light-years away, orbiting bet Pic. Discovered in 2008 by imaging. A year there lasts 23.8 years. It is 18.49 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • Photographed directly around the young star Beta Pictoris, which is wrapped in a huge disc of dust and debris.
  • One year here lasts 23.8 years, so a single orbit takes 23.8 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 64.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 64 years ago.
  • Its radius is 18.5× Earth's radius, or 1.65× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.2% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 8.7× Jupiter in mass (2,765× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,612 K (1,339 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives only 8.8% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits bet Pic, a white (A) star at 8,039 K.
