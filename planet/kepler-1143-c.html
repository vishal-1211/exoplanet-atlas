Kepler-1143 c

Kepler-1143 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 1,811 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1143. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 210.6 days. It is 3.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 210.6 days, so about 1.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,811 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,811 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.6× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.18% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 12.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 234 K (-39 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits Kepler-1143, a orange (K) star at 5,053 K.
  • Kepler-1143 has 2 known planets.
