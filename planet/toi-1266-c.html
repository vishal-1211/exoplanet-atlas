TOI-1266 c

TOI-1266 c is a super-earth exoplanet 117 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1266. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 18.8 days. It is 1.98 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 18.8 days, so about 19.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 117.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 117 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.98× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.18% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 3.17× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 0.81× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 57 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 346 K (73 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
