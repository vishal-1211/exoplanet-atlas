TOI-2322 b

TOI-2322 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 195 light-years away, orbiting TOI-2322. Discovered in 2025 by transit. A year there lasts 11.3 days. It is 0.99 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11.3 days, so about 32.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 195.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 195 years ago.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.31× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 2.3× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 164 kg.
  • Its average density is 13 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 603 K (330 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
