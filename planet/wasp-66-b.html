WASP-66 b

WASP-66 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,632 light-years away, orbiting WASP-66. Discovered in 2012 by transit. A year there lasts 4.1 days. It is 15.69 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.1 days, so about 89.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,632 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,632 years ago.
  • Its radius is 15.7× Earth's radius, or 1.4× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.67% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.35× Jupiter in mass (746.9× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,570 K (1,297 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,435 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.055 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
