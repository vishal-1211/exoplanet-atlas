TOI-2158 c

TOI-2158 c is a gas giant exoplanet 647 light-years away, orbiting TOI-2158. Discovered in 2026 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.1 years. It is 13.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.1 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 647.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 647 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.5× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.88% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.62× Jupiter in mass (514.9× Earth).
  • Its estimated temperature is 296 K (23 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.54). It is 3 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits TOI-2158, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,673 K.
