HD 102329 c

HD 102329 c is a gas giant exoplanet 708 light-years away, orbiting HD 102329. Discovered in 2016 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.1 years. It is 13.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.1 years, so a single orbit takes 3.1 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 708.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 708 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.5× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.52× Jupiter in mass (483.1× Earth).
  • Its estimated temperature is 385 K (112 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 3.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 102329, a orange (K) star at 4,830 K.
