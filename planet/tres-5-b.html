TrES-5 b

TrES-5 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,175 light-years away, orbiting TrES-5. Discovered in 2011 by transit. A year there lasts 1.5 days. It is 13.38 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.5 days, so about 246.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,175 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,175 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.4× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.99% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.79× Jupiter in mass (568.9× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,480 K (1,207 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 443 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.025 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
