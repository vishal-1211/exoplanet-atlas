Kepler-387 b

Kepler-387 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 2,613 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-387. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 6.8 days. It is 1.03 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.8 days, so about 53.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,613 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,613 years ago.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0081% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.08× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 994 K (721 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 304.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.068 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
