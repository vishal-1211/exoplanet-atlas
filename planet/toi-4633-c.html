TOI-4633 c

TOI-4633 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 311 light-years away, orbiting TOI-4633. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 271.9 days. It is 3.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 271.9 days, so about 1.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 310.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 311 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.2× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 123× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 269 K (-5 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives 1.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits TOI-4633, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,800 K.
