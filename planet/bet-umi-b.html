bet UMi b

bet UMi b is a gas giant exoplanet 126 light-years away, orbiting bet UMi. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.4 years. It is 12.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.4 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 126.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 126 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.7× Earth's radius, or 1.13× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.00092% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 6.1× Jupiter in mass (1,939× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,041 K (767 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 274 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits bet UMi, a orange (K) star at 4,126 K.
