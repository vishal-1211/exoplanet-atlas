K2-360 c

K2-360 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 757 light-years away, orbiting EPIC 201595106. Discovered in 2024 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 9.8 days. It is 4.02 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.8 days, so about 37.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 757 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 757 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.02× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.14% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 15.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 912 K (639 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 115 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.088 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
