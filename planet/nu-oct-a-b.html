nu Oct A b

nu Oct A b is a gas giant exoplanet 69 light-years away, orbiting nu Oct A. Discovered in 2016 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.1 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.1 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 69.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 69 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.19× Jupiter in mass (696× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 477 K (204 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 8.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits nu Oct A, a orange (K) star at 4,910 K.
