GJ 163 c

GJ 163 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 49 light-years away, orbiting GJ 163. Discovered in 2013 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 25.6 days. It is 2.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 25.6 days, so about 14.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 49.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 49 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.5× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.31% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 305 K (32 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits at 0.125 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits GJ 163, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,500 K.
