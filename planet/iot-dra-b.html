iot Dra b

iot Dra b is a gas giant exoplanet 102 light-years away, orbiting iot Dra. Discovered in 2002 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.4 years. It is 12.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.4 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 102.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 102 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.4× Earth's radius, or 1.11× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0093% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 11.8× Jupiter in mass (3,757× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 619 K (346 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 25 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.7). It is 6 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
