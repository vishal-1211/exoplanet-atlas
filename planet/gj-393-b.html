GJ 393 b

GJ 393 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 23 light-years away, orbiting GJ 393. Discovered in 2021 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 7 days. It is 1.17 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7 days, so about 52 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 22.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 23 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.17× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.71× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 485 K (212 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 9.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.054 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
