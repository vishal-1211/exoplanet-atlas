TOI-1230.01

TOI-1230.01 is a mini-neptune exoplanet 467 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1230. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 25.1 days. It is 2.39 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 25.1 days, so about 14.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 466.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 467 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.39× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.29× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 77 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,047 K (773 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 199.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
