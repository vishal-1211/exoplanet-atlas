NGTS-14 A b

NGTS-14 A b is a neptune-like exoplanet 1,051 light-years away, orbiting NGTS-14 A. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 3.5 days. It is 4.98 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.5 days, so about 103.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,051 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,051 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.98× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.29% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 29.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,143 K (870 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 191.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.04 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
