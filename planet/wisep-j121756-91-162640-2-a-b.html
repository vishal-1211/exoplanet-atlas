WISEP J121756.91+162640.2 A b

WISEP J121756.91+162640.2 A b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 33 light-years away, orbiting WISEP J121756.91+162640.2 A. Discovered in 2012 by imaging. A year there lasts 132.9 years. It is 10.47 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 132.9 years, so a single orbit takes 132.9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 32.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 33 years ago.
  • Its radius is 10.5× Earth's radius, or 0.93× Jupiter's.
  • At 22× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 450 K (177 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits WISEP J121756.91+162640.2 A, a ultracool dwarf star at 575 K.
  • It was one of 144 planets discovered in 2012.
