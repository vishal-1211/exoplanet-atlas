COCONUTS-2 b

COCONUTS-2 b is a gas giant exoplanet 36 light-years away, orbiting COCONUTS-2 A. Discovered in 2021 by imaging. A year there lasts 1,100,616 years. It is 12.58 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1,100,616 years, so a single orbit takes 1,100,616 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 35.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 36 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.6× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 9.9% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 7.5× Jupiter in mass (2,384× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 493 K (220 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 7,506 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
