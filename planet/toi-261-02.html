TOI-261.02

TOI-261.02 is a mini-neptune exoplanet 373 light-years away, orbiting TOI-261. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 13 days. It is 3.35 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 13 days, so about 28 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 373.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 373 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.35× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,008 K (734 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 243.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.111 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
