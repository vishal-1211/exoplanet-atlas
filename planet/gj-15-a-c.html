GJ 15 A c

GJ 15 A c is a gas giant exoplanet 12 light-years away, orbiting GJ 15 A. Discovered in 2018 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 20.8 years. It is 6.67 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 20.8 years, so a single orbit takes 20.8 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 11.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 12 years ago. That makes it the 14th closest of the 6,451 known planets.
  • Its radius is 6.67× Earth's radius, or 0.6× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.58% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 36× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 46 K (-227 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.08% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits GJ 15 A, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,607 K.
