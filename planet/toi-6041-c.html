TOI-6041 c

TOI-6041 c is a gas giant exoplanet 236 light-years away, orbiting TOI-6041. Discovered in 2025 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 88 days. It is 10.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 88 days, so about 4.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 236.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 236 years ago.
  • Its radius is 10.5× Earth's radius, or 0.94× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.2% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 77.9× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 404 K (130 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 4.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.372 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
