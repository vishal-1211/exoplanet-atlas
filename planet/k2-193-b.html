K2-193 b

K2-193 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 1,344 light-years away, orbiting K2-193. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 14.8 days. It is 3.95 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 14.8 days, so about 24.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,344 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,344 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.95× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.14% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 14.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 739 K (466 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 59.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.118 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
