KMT-2025-BLG-2272L b

KMT-2025-BLG-2272L b is a gas giant exoplanet 21,592 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2025-BLG-2272L. Discovered in 2026 by microlensing. A year there lasts 9.7 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.7 years, so a single orbit takes 9.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 21,592 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 21,592 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 2.47× Jupiter in mass (785× Earth).
  • It was one of 355 planets discovered in 2026.
