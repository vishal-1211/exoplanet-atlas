HD 16175 b

HD 16175 b is a gas giant exoplanet 195 light-years away, orbiting HD 16175. Discovered in 2009 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.7 years. It is 12.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.7 years, so a single orbit takes 2.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 195.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 195 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.8× Earth's radius, or 1.14× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.46% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 5.9× Jupiter in mass (1,875× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 260 K (-13 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.6). It is 4 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits HD 16175, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,981 K.
