Kepler-158 d

Kepler-158 d is a sub-earth exoplanet 1,028 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-158. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 15.5 hours. It is 0.43 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 15.5 hours.
  • It is 1,028 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,028 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.43× Earth's radius).
  • It is the 6th smallest planet in the catalogue by radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0035% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • Its mass is only 0.05× Earth's, lighter than Mars.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 0.26× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 18 kg.
  • Its estimated temperature is 1,713 K (1,439 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
