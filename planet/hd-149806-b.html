HD 149806 b

HD 149806 b is a gas giant exoplanet 66 light-years away, orbiting HD 149806. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 45.2 years. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 45.2 years, so a single orbit takes 45.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 66.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 66 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.6% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 12.6× Jupiter in mass (4,004× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 69 K (-204 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.37% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 149806, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,352 K.
