Kepler-381 d

Kepler-381 d is an earth-sized exoplanet 872 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-381. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 8.3 days. It is 1.07 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.3 days, so about 44.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 871.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 872 years ago.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0020% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.23× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,387 K (1,114 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 436.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.084 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
