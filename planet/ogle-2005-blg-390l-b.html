OGLE-2005-BLG-390L b

OGLE-2005-BLG-390L b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 21,526 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2005-BLG-390L. Discovered in 2005 by microlensing. A year there lasts 9 years. It is 2.21 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9 years, so a single orbit takes 9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 21,526 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 21,526 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.21× Earth's radius.
  • It is 5.5× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 79 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 50 K (-223 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It was one of 36 planets discovered in 2005.
