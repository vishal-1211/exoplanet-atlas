K2-290 c

K2-290 c is a gas giant exoplanet 890 light-years away, orbiting K2-290. Discovered in 2019 by transit. A year there lasts 48.4 days. It is 11.28 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 48.4 days, so about 7.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 890.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 890 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.3× Earth's radius, or 1.01× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.47% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.77× Jupiter in mass (246× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.95 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 676 K (403 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 28.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
