KMT-2016-BLG-1836L b

KMT-2016-BLG-1836L b is a gas giant exoplanet 23,157 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2016-BLG-1836L. Discovered in 2019 by microlensing. A year there lasts 9.4 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.4 years, so a single orbit takes 9.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 23,157 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 23,157 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 2.2× Jupiter in mass (700× Earth).
  • It was one of 194 planets discovered in 2019.
