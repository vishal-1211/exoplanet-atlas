Kepler-432 c

Kepler-432 c is a gas giant exoplanet 2,766 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-432. Discovered in 2015 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.1 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.1 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 2,766 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,766 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.09% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.43× Jupiter in mass (772.3× Earth).
  • Its estimated temperature is 447 K (174 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 6.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits Kepler-432, a orange (K) star at 4,995 K.
