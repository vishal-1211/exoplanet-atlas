K2-233 d

K2-233 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 220 light-years away, orbiting K2-233. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 24.4 days. It is 2.36 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 24.4 days, so about 15 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 220.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 220 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.36× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.09% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 10.3× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.8× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 129 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 525 K (252 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 12.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
