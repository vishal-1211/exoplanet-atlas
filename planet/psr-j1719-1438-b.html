PSR J1719-1438 b

PSR J1719-1438 b is a gas giant exoplanet 3,914 light-years away, orbiting PSR J1719-1438. Discovered in 2011 by pulsar timing. A year there lasts 2.2 hours. It is 13.66 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 2.2 hours.
  • It is 3,914 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,914 years ago.
  • It weighs 1.2× Jupiter in mass (382.8× Earth).
  • It orbits at 0.0044 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
  • It orbits PSR J1719-1438, a orange (K) star at 4,500 K.
  • It was one of 141 planets discovered in 2011.
