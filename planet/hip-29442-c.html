HIP 29442 c

HIP 29442 c is a super-earth exoplanet 222 light-years away, orbiting TOI-469. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.5 days. It is 1.55 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.5 days, so about 103.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 222.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 222 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.55× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.5× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.9× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 131 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,123 K (850 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 451 times as much starlight as Earth does.
