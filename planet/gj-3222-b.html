GJ 3222 b

GJ 3222 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 59 light-years away, orbiting GJ 3222. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 10.7 days. It is 3.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 10.7 days, so about 34.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 59.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 59 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.4× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.11% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 811 K (538 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 67.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.091 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
