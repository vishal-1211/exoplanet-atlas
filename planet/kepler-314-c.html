Kepler-314 c

Kepler-314 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 877 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-314. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 6 days. It is 2.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6 days, so about 61.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 877 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 877 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.9× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.75× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 890 K (617 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 150 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.064 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
