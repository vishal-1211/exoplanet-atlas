KMT-2016-BLG-2397L b

KMT-2016-BLG-2397L b is a gas giant exoplanet 15,916 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2016-BLG-2397L. Discovered in 2020 by microlensing. A year there lasts 8.7 years. It is 13.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.7 years, so a single orbit takes 8.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 15,916 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 15,916 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.2× Earth's radius, or 1.18× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 2.63× Jupiter in mass (836× Earth).
  • It was one of 234 planets discovered in 2020.
