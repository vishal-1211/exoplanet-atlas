Kepler-1513 b

Kepler-1513 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,139 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1513. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 160.9 days. It is 8.59 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 160.9 days, so about 2.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,139 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,139 years ago.
  • Its radius is 8.59× Earth's radius, or 0.77× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.69% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 48.3× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.42 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 346 K (73 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
