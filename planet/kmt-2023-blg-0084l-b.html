KMT-2023-BLG-0084L b

KMT-2023-BLG-0084L b is a gas giant exoplanet 13,046 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2023-BLG-0084L. Discovered in 2026 by microlensing. A year there lasts 8.2 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.2 years, so a single orbit takes 8.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 13,046 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 13,046 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 2.5× Jupiter in mass (794.6× Earth).
  • It was one of 355 planets discovered in 2026.
