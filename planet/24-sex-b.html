24 Sex b

24 Sex b is a gas giant exoplanet 235 light-years away, orbiting 24 Sex. Discovered in 2010 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.2 years. It is 13.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.2 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 235.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 235 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.4× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.99× Jupiter in mass (632.5× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 471 K (198 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 8.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits 24 Sex, a orange (K) star at 5,098 K.
