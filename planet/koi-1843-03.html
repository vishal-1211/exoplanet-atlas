KOI-1843.03

KOI-1843.03 is a sub-earth exoplanet 438 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-974. Discovered in 2013 by transit. A year there lasts 4.2 hours. It is 0.61 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 4.2 hours.
  • It is 438.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 438 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.61× Earth's radius).
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 21.5× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 1,505 kg.
  • Its average density is 194.2 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,654 K (1,381 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
