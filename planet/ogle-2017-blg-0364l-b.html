OGLE-2017-BLG-0364L b

OGLE-2017-BLG-0364L b is a gas giant exoplanet 25,636 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2017-BLG-0364L. Discovered in 2024 by microlensing. A year there lasts 10.5 years. It is 10.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 10.5 years, so a single orbit takes 10.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 25,636 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 25,636 years ago.
  • Its radius is 10.3× Earth's radius, or 0.92× Jupiter's.
  • It is 75.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.38 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • It was one of 260 planets discovered in 2024.
