Kepler-740 b

Kepler-740 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 4,703 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-740. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 3.6 days. It is 4.57 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.6 days, so about 101.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 4,703 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 4,703 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.57× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.23% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 18.9× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,131 K (858 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 432.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.046 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
