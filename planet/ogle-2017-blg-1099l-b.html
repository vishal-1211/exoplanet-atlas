OGLE-2017-BLG-1099L b

OGLE-2017-BLG-1099L b is a gas giant exoplanet 23,646 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2017-BLG-1099L. Discovered in 2021 by microlensing. A year there lasts 6.7 years. It is 13.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.7 years, so a single orbit takes 6.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 23,646 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 23,646 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.1× Earth's radius, or 1.17× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 3.02× Jupiter in mass (959.8× Earth).
  • It was one of 564 planets discovered in 2021.
