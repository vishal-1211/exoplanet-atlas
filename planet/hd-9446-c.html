HD 9446 c

HD 9446 c is a gas giant exoplanet 164 light-years away, orbiting HD 9446. Discovered in 2009 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 192.9 days. It is 13.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 192.9 days, so about 1.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 164.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 164 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.4× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.51% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.82× Jupiter in mass (578.4× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 345 K (72 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 9446, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,793 K.
