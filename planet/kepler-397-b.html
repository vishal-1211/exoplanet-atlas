Kepler-397 b

Kepler-397 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 3,160 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-397. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 22.3 days. It is 2.46 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 22.3 days, so about 16.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,160 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,160 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.46× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.09% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.62× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 539 K (266 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 20 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.144 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
