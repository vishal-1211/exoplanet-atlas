Kepler-197 b

Kepler-197 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 1,078 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-197. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 5.6 days. It is 1.02 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.6 days, so about 65.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,078 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,078 years ago.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0070% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.04× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be close to Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 70 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,142 K (869 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 431.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
