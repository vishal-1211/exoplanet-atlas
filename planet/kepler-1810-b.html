Kepler-1810 b

Kepler-1810 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 3,294 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1810. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 101.1 days. It is 3.92 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 101.1 days, so about 3.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,294 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,294 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.92× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.12% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 14.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 380 K (107 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 4.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits Kepler-1810, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,492 K.
