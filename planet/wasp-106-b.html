WASP-106 b

WASP-106 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,149 light-years away, orbiting WASP-106. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 9.3 days. It is 12.11 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.3 days, so about 39.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,149 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,149 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.1× Earth's radius, or 1.08× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.57% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.93× Jupiter in mass (613.4× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,140 K (867 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 327.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.09 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
