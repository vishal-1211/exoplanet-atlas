HD 20794 f

HD 20794 f is a mini-neptune exoplanet 20 light-years away, orbiting HD 20794. Discovered in 2025 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.8 years. It is 2.28 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.8 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 19.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 20 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.28× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.82× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 215 K (-59 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 20794, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,368 K.
  • HD 20794 has 4 known planets.
