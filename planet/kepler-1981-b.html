Kepler-1981 b

Kepler-1981 b is a gas giant exoplanet 2,151 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1981. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 1.2 years. It is 9.23 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.2 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 2,151 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,151 years ago.
  • Its radius is 9.23× Earth's radius, or 0.82× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 62.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 390 K (117 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 5.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits Kepler-1981, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,874 K.
