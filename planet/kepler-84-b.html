Kepler-84 b

Kepler-84 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 3,340 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-84. Discovered in 2012 by transit. A year there lasts 8.7 days. It is 2.23 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.7 days, so about 41.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,340 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,340 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.23× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 40× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 8× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 563 kg.
  • Its average density is 19.9 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 985 K (712 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
