G 264-012 b

G 264-012 b is a super-earth exoplanet 52 light-years away, orbiting G 264-012. Discovered in 2021 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.3 days. It is 1.39 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.3 days, so about 158.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 52.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 52 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.39× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.17% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 587 K (314 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 20.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.023 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
