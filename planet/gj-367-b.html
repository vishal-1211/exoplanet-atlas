GJ 367 b

GJ 367 b is a sub-earth exoplanet 31 light-years away, orbiting GJ 367. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 7.7 hours. It is 0.74 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 7.7 hours.
  • It is 30.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 31 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.74× Earth's radius).
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.5× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be close to Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 65 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,329 K (1,056 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 579 times as much starlight as Earth does.
