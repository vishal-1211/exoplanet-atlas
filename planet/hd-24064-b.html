HD 24064 b

HD 24064 b is a gas giant exoplanet 979 light-years away, orbiting HD 24064. Discovered in 2015 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.5 years. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.5 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 979.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 979 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.00079% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 12.9× Jupiter in mass (4,097× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,088 K (815 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 211.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 24064, a orange (K) star at 4,052 K.
