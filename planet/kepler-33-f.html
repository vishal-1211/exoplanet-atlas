Kepler-33 f

Kepler-33 f is a neptune-like exoplanet 3,944 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-33. Discovered in 2011 by transit. A year there lasts 41 days. It is 4.46 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 41 days, so about 8.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,944 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,944 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.46× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 9.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.6 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 691 K (418 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 53.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
