HD 74156 c

HD 74156 c is a gas giant exoplanet 188 light-years away, orbiting HD 74156. Discovered in 2003 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 6.7 years. It is 12.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.7 years, so a single orbit takes 6.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 187.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 188 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.5× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.57% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 8.66× Jupiter in mass (2,754× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 188 K (-86 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 74156, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,068 K.
  • HD 74156 has 2 known planets.
