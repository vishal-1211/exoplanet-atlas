Kepler-407 c

Kepler-407 c is a gas giant exoplanet 1,104 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-407. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 8.2 years. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.2 years, so a single orbit takes 8.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 1,104 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,104 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.24% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 12.6× Jupiter in mass (4,000× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 147 K (-126 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 8.5% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits Kepler-407, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,476 K.
