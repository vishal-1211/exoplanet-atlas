XO-2 S c

XO-2 S c is a gas giant exoplanet 494 light-years away, orbiting XO-2 S. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 120.1 days. It is 13.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 120.1 days, so about 3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 493.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 494 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.6× Earth's radius, or 1.21× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.49% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.38× Jupiter in mass (438.6× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.96 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 377 K (104 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 3.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
