HD 221287 b

HD 221287 b is a gas giant exoplanet 182 light-years away, orbiting HD 221287. Discovered in 2007 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.2 years. It is 13.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.2 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 182.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 182 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.1× Earth's radius, or 1.17× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.11% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.09× Jupiter in mass (982× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 290 K (17 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits HD 221287, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,304 K.
  • It was one of 52 planets discovered in 2007.
