K2-183 b

K2-183 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 1,073 light-years away, orbiting K2-183. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 11.3 hours. It is 1.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 11.3 hours.
  • It is 1,073 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,073 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.1× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.37× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 2,291 K (2,017 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 4,588 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.012 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
