HD 110067 f

HD 110067 f is a mini-neptune exoplanet 105 light-years away, orbiting HD 110067. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 41.1 days. It is 2.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 41.1 days, so about 8.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 104.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 105 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.6× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.09% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.04× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 489 K (216 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 9.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.216 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
