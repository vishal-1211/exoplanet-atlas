Qatar-7 b

Qatar-7 b is a gas giant exoplanet 2,303 light-years away, orbiting Qatar-7. Discovered in 2019 by transit. A year there lasts 2 days. It is 19.06 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2 days, so about 179.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,303 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,303 years ago.
  • Its radius is 19.1× Earth's radius, or 1.7× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.24% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.88× Jupiter in mass (597.5× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.48 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,053 K (1,780 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 2,886 times as much starlight as Earth does.
