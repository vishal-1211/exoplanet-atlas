tau Gem b

tau Gem b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 367 light-years away, orbiting tau Gem. Discovered in 2013 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 305.5 days. It is 12.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 305.5 days, so about 1.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 367 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 367 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.1× Earth's radius, or 1.08× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0017% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 20.6× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,013 K (740 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 241.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits tau Gem, a orange (K) star at 4,388 K.
