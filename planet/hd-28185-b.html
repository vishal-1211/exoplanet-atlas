HD 28185 b

HD 28185 b is a gas giant exoplanet 128 light-years away, orbiting HD 28185. Discovered in 2001 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 385.9 days. It is 12.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 385.9 days, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 128.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 128 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.8× Earth's radius, or 1.14× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.25% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 5.85× Jupiter in mass (1,859× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 272 K (-1 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 28185, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,602 K.
  • HD 28185 has 2 known planets.
