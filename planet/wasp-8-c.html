WASP-8 c

WASP-8 c is a gas giant exoplanet 293 light-years away, orbiting WASP-8. Discovered in 2013 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 11.8 years. It is 12.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11.8 years, so a single orbit takes 11.8 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 293.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 293 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.5× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.42% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 9.45× Jupiter in mass (3,003× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 117 K (-156 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 3.1% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits WASP-8, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,690 K.
