TOI-559 b

TOI-559 b is a gas giant exoplanet 761 light-years away, orbiting TOI-559. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 7 days. It is 12.23 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7 days, so about 52.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 760.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 761 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.2× Earth's radius, or 1.09× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.82% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 6.01× Jupiter in mass (1,910× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,180 K (907 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 404.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.072 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
