Kepler-49 b

Kepler-49 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,015 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-49. Discovered in 2012 by transit. A year there lasts 7.2 days. It is 2.58 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.2 days, so about 50.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,015 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,015 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.58× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 9.77× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 509 K (236 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 21.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.059 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
