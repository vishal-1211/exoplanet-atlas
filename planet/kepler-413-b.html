Kepler-413 b

Kepler-413 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 2,764 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-413. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 66.3 days. It is 4.35 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 66.3 days, so about 5.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,764 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,764 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.35× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.26% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 67× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 335 K (62 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.355 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
