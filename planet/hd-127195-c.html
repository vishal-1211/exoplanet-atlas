HD 127195 c

HD 127195 c is a gas giant exoplanet 276 light-years away, orbiting HD 127195. Discovered in 2026 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.3 years. It is 14.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.3 years, so a single orbit takes 2.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 276.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 276 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.1× Earth's radius, or 1.26× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.65× Jupiter in mass (206.6× Earth).
  • Its estimated temperature is 354 K (81 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 127195, a orange (K) star at 4,981 K.
