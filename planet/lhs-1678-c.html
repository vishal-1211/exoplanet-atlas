LHS 1678 c

LHS 1678 c is an earth-sized exoplanet 65 light-years away, orbiting LHS 1678. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 3.7 days. It is 0.94 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.7 days, so about 98.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 64.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 65 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.94× Earth's radius).
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.4× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.6× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 111 kg.
  • Its average density is 9.3 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 511 K (238 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
