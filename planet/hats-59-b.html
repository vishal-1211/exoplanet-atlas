HATS-59 b

HATS-59 b is a gas giant exoplanet 2,097 light-years away, orbiting HATS-59. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 5.4 days. It is 12.62 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.4 days, so about 67.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,097 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,097 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.6× Earth's radius, or 1.13× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.24% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.81× Jupiter in mass (256.2× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.7 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,128 K (855 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 269 times as much starlight as Earth does.
