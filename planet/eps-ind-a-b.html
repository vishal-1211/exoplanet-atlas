eps Ind A b

eps Ind A b is a gas giant exoplanet 12 light-years away, orbiting eps Ind A. Discovered in 2019 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 69.7 years. It is 12.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 69.7 years, so a single orbit takes 69.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 11.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 12 years ago. That makes it the 19th closest of the 6,445 known planets.
  • Its radius is 12.7× Earth's radius, or 1.13× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.66% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 6.5× Jupiter in mass (2,066× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 275 K (2 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives only 0.1% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits eps Ind A, a orange (K) star at 4,700 K.
