WASP-81 b

WASP-81 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,300 light-years away, orbiting WASP-81. Discovered in 2017 by transit. A year there lasts 2.7 days. It is 16.02 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.7 days, so about 134.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,300 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,300 years ago.
  • Its radius is 16× Earth's radius, or 1.43× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.31% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.73× Jupiter in mass (231.7× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.31 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,623 K (1,350 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,034 times as much starlight as Earth does.
