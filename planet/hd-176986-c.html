HD 176986 c

HD 176986 c is a gas giant exoplanet 91 light-years away, orbiting HD 176986. Discovered in 2017 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 16.8 days. It is 12.67 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 16.8 days, so about 21.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 90.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 91 years ago.
  • It weighs 6.6× Jupiter in mass (2,098× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 558 K (285 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 22.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.119 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits HD 176986, a orange (K) star at 4,931 K.
  • HD 176986 has 3 known planets.
