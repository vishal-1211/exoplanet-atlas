HD 10180 g

HD 10180 g is a gas giant exoplanet 127 light-years away, orbiting HD 10180. Discovered in 2010 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.7 years. It is 12.41 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.7 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 127.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 127 years ago.
  • It weighs 10.6× Jupiter in mass (3,375× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 251 K (-22 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 10180, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,911 K.
  • HD 10180 has 6 known planets.
  • It was one of 93 planets discovered in 2010.
