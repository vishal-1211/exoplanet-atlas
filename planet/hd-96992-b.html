HD 96992 b

HD 96992 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,292 light-years away, orbiting HD 96992. Discovered in 2021 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.4 years. It is 13.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.4 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 1,292 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,292 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.7× Earth's radius, or 1.22× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.14× Jupiter in mass (362.3× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.78 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 558 K (285 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 19.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
