2MASS J22501512+2325342 b

2MASS J22501512+2325342 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 187 light-years away, orbiting 2MASS J22501512+2325342. Discovered in 2018 by imaging. A year there lasts 17,913 years. It is 11.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 17,913 years, so a single orbit takes 17,913 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 186.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 187 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.9× Earth's radius, or 1.06× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 6.2% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 30× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,600 K (1,327 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 518 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
