HD 215152 c

HD 215152 c is a gas giant exoplanet 70 light-years away, orbiting HD 215152. Discovered in 2018 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 7.3 days. It is 13.53 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.3 days, so about 50.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 70.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 70 years ago.
  • It weighs 1.47× Jupiter in mass (468.8× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 783 K (510 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 66.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.067 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
  • It orbits HD 215152, a orange (K) star at 4,935 K.
