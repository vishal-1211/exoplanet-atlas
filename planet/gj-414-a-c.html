GJ 414 A c

GJ 414 A c is a gas giant exoplanet 39 light-years away, orbiting GJ 414 A. Discovered in 2021 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.1 years. It is 8.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.1 years, so a single orbit takes 2.1 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 38.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 39 years ago.
  • Its radius is 8.4× Earth's radius, or 0.75× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.28% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 53.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.5 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 125 K (-148 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 6.1% of the starlight Earth gets.
