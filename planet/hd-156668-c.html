HD 156668 c

HD 156668 c is a gas giant exoplanet 79 light-years away, orbiting HD 156668. Discovered in 2021 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.2 years. It is 6.17 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.2 years, so a single orbit takes 2.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 79.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 79 years ago.
  • Its radius is 6.17× Earth's radius, or 0.55× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.53% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 31.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 166 K (-107 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 156668, a orange (K) star at 4,904 K.
  • HD 156668 has 2 known planets.
