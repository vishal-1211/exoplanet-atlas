Kepler-631 b

Kepler-631 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 2,485 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-631. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 18 days. It is 2.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 18 days, so about 20.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,485 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,485 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.9× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.75× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 828 K (555 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 94.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.14 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
