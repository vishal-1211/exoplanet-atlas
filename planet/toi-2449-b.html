TOI-2449 b

TOI-2449 b is a gas giant exoplanet 511 light-years away, orbiting TOI-2449. Discovered in 2025 by transit. A year there lasts 106.1 days. It is 11.24 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 106.1 days, so about 3.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 511.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 511 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.2× Earth's radius, or 1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.93% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.7× Jupiter in mass (222× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.86 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 400 K (127 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 362.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
