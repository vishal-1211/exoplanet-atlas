HR 2562 b

HR 2562 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 111 light-years away, orbiting HR 2562. Discovered in 2016 by imaging. A year there lasts 91.7 years. It is 12.44 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 91.7 years, so a single orbit takes 91.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 110.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 111 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.4× Earth's radius, or 1.11× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.73% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 22× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,255 K (982 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives only 0.69% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HR 2562, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,597 K.
