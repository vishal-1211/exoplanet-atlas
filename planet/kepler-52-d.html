Kepler-52 d

Kepler-52 d is a super-earth exoplanet 1,049 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-52. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 36.4 days. It is 1.95 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 36.4 days, so about 10 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,049 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,049 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.95× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 88.9× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 23.4× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 1,636 kg.
  • Its average density is 66 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 332 K (59 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
