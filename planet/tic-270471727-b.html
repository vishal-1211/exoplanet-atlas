TIC 270471727 b

TIC 270471727 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 594 light-years away, orbiting TIC 270471727. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 10.5 days. It is 2.75 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 10.5 days, so about 34.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 593.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 594 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.75× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,147 K (874 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 284 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.09 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
