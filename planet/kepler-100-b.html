Kepler-100 b

Kepler-100 b is a super-earth exoplanet 994 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-100. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 6.9 days. It is 1.34 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.9 days, so about 53 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 993.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 994 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.34× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0066% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.01× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 2.2× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 156 kg.
  • Its average density is 9.2 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,155 K (882 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
