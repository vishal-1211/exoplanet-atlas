K2-210 b

K2-210 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 576 light-years away, orbiting K2-210. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 13.7 hours. It is 0.86 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 13.7 hours.
  • It is 575.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 576 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.86× Earth's radius).
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0078% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.57× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 2,190 K (1,917 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 3,835 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.013 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
