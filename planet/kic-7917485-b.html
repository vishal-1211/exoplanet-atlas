KIC 7917485 b

KIC 7917485 b is a gas giant exoplanet 4,510 light-years away, orbiting KIC 7917485. Discovered in 2016 by pulsation timing variations. A year there lasts 2.3 years. It is 12.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.3 years, so a single orbit takes 2.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 4,510 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 4,510 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.4× Earth's radius, or 1.11× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.32% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 11.8× Jupiter in mass (3,750× Earth).
  • Its estimated temperature is 337 K (64 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits KIC 7917485, a yellow-white (F) star at 7,067 K.
