TOI-6677 b

TOI-6677 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 1,111 light-years away, orbiting TOI-6677. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 5.5 days. It is 5.84 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.5 days, so about 67 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,111 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,111 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.84× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.12% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 28.7× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 334 K (61 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.069 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
