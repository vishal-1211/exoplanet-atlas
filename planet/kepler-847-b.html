Kepler-847 b

Kepler-847 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 1,674 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-847. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 2.3 days. It is 1.18 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.3 days, so about 155.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,674 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,674 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.18× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.76× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.3× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 88 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,054 K (781 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 292.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
