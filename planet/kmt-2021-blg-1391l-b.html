KMT-2021-BLG-1391L b

KMT-2021-BLG-1391L b is a super-earth exoplanet 18,656 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2021-BLG-1391L. Discovered in 2022 by microlensing. A year there lasts 5.7 years. It is 1.97 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.7 years, so a single orbit takes 5.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 18,656 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 18,656 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.97× Earth's radius.
  • It is 4.55× Earth's mass.
  • It was one of 367 planets discovered in 2022.
