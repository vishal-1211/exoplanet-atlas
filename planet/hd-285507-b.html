HD 285507 b

HD 285507 b is a gas giant exoplanet 147 light-years away, orbiting HD 285507. Discovered in 2013 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 6.1 days. It is 13.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.1 days, so about 60 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 146.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 147 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.8× Earth's radius, or 1.23× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 3.35% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.98× Jupiter in mass (311.5× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.65 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 736 K (463 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 53 times as much starlight as Earth does.
