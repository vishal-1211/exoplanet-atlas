TOI-178 g

TOI-178 g is a mini-neptune exoplanet 204 light-years away, orbiting TOI-178. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 20.7 days. It is 2.94 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 20.7 days, so about 17.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 204.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 204 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.94× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.17% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.96 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 470 K (197 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 19.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
