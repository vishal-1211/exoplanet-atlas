CoRoT-7 d

CoRoT-7 d is a neptune-like exoplanet 522 light-years away, orbiting CoRoT-7. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 9 days. It is 4.31 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9 days, so about 40.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 521.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 522 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.31× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.22% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 17.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 810 K (537 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 71.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.082 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
