Kepler-712 c

Kepler-712 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 2,846 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-712. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 226.9 days. It is 4.85 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 226.9 days, so about 1.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,846 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,846 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.85× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.32% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 21× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 236 K (-37 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits Kepler-712, a orange (K) star at 5,148 K.
  • Kepler-712 has 2 known planets.
