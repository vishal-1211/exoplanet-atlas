KOI-1833 d

KOI-1833 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 947 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-968. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 7.7 days. It is 2.58 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.7 days, so about 47.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 946.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 947 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.58× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.13% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 613 K (340 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 27.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.063 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
