KIC 10001893 b

KIC 10001893 b is a not identified exoplanet 5,456 light-years away, orbiting KIC 10001893. Discovered in 2014 by orbital brightness modulation. A year there lasts 5.3 hours.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 5.3 hours.
  • It is 5,456 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 5,456 years ago.
  • Its estimated temperature is 7,559 K (7,285 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 543,900 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.0055 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
  • It orbits KIC 10001893, a blue-white (B) star at 27,500 K.
  • KIC 10001893 has 3 known planets.
