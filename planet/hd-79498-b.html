HD 79498 b

HD 79498 b is a gas giant exoplanet 160 light-years away, orbiting HD 79498. Discovered in 2011 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 4.9 years. It is 13.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.9 years, so a single orbit takes 4.9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 159.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 160 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.6× Earth's radius, or 1.21× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.41% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.34× Jupiter in mass (425.9× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 165 K (-109 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.57). It is 4 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits HD 79498, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,748 K.
