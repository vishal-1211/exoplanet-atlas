NGTS-3 A b

NGTS-3 A b is a gas giant exoplanet 2,427 light-years away, orbiting NGTS-3 A. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 1.7 days. It is 16.59 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.7 days, so about 218 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,427 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,427 years ago.
  • Its radius is 16.6× Earth's radius, or 1.48× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.67% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.38× Jupiter in mass (756.4× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.91 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,434 K (1,161 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,001 times as much starlight as Earth does.
