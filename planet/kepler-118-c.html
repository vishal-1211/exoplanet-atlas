Kepler-118 c

Kepler-118 c is a gas giant exoplanet 1,873 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-118. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 20.2 days. It is 7.68 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 20.2 days, so about 18.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,873 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,873 years ago.
  • Its radius is 7.68× Earth's radius, or 0.69× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.41% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 45.7× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 564 K (291 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 21.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.141 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
