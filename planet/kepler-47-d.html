Kepler-47 d

Kepler-47 d is a gas giant exoplanet 3,343 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-47. Discovered in 2019 by transit. A year there lasts 187.4 days. It is 7.04 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 187.4 days, so about 1.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,343 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,343 years ago.
  • Its radius is 7.04× Earth's radius, or 0.63× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.47% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 19× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.3 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 314 K (41 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 1.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
