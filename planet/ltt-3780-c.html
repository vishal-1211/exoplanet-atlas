LTT 3780 c

LTT 3780 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 72 light-years away, orbiting LTT 3780. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 12.3 days. It is 2.39 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 12.3 days, so about 29.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 71.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 72 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.39× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.33% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.04× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.4× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 99 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 359 K (86 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
