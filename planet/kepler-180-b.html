Kepler-180 b

Kepler-180 b is a super-earth exoplanet 2,269 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-180. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 13.8 days. It is 1.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 13.8 days, so about 26.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,269 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,269 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.5× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.86× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 781 K (508 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 72.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.109 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
