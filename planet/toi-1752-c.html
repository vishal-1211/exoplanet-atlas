TOI-1752 c

TOI-1752 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 336 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1752. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 32.7 days. It is 2.29 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 32.7 days, so about 11.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 336 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 336 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.29× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.16% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.86× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 291 K (18 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 1.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.161 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
