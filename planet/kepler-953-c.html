Kepler-953 c

Kepler-953 c is an earth-sized exoplanet 844 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-953. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 9.1 days. It is 1.19 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.1 days, so about 40.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 844.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 844 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.19× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.81× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 873 K (600 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 121 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.086 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
