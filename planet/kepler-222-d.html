Kepler-222 d

Kepler-222 d is a neptune-like exoplanet 2,475 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-222. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 28.1 days. It is 3.69 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 28.1 days, so about 13 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,475 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,475 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.69× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 13.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 522 K (249 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 15.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.18 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
