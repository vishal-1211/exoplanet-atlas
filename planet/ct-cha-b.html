CT Cha b

CT Cha b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 622 light-years away, orbiting CT Cha. Discovered in 2007 by imaging. It is 24.66 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • It is 622 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 622 years ago.
  • Its radius is 24.7× Earth's radius, or 2.2× Jupiter's.
  • It is the 8th largest planet in the catalogue by radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.81% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 17× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,600 K (2,327 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 440 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
