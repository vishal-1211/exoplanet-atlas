TOI-561 d

TOI-561 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 280 light-years away, orbiting TOI-561. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 25.7 days. It is 2.62 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 25.7 days, so about 14.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 279.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 280 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.62× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 13.3× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 600 K (327 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 21.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.159 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
