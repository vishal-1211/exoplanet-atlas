HD 41004 B b

HD 41004 B b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 136 light-years away, orbiting HD 41004 B. Discovered in 2003 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.3 days. It is 12.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.3 days, so about 275 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 135.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 136 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.1× Earth's radius, or 1.08× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.13% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 18.4× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its estimated temperature is 1,877 K (1,604 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 2,070 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.017 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
