KOI-1783.01

KOI-1783.01 is a gas giant exoplanet 2,824 light-years away, orbiting KOI-1783. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 134.5 days. It is 8.86 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 134.5 days, so about 2.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,824 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,824 years ago.
  • Its radius is 8.86× Earth's radius, or 0.79× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.5% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 71× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.56 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 412 K (139 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 5.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
