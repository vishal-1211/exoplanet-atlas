Pr0211 c

Pr0211 c is a gas giant exoplanet 596 light-years away, orbiting Pr0211. Discovered in 2016 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 13.3 years. It is 12.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 13.3 years, so a single orbit takes 13.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 595.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 596 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.6× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.95% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 7.79× Jupiter in mass (2,476× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 99 K (-174 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 1.5% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.71). It is 6 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
