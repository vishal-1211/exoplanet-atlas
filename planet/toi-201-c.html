TOI-201 c

TOI-201 c is a super-jupiter exoplanet 371 light-years away, orbiting TOI-201. Discovered in 2025 by transit timing variations. A year there lasts 7.9 years. It is 10.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.9 years, so a single orbit takes 7.9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 371.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 371 years ago.
  • Its radius is 10.4× Earth's radius, or 0.93× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.53% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 15.7× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 170 K (-104 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.65). It is 5 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits TOI-201, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,423 K.
