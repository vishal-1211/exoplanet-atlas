HD 206893 b

HD 206893 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 133 light-years away, orbiting HD 206893. Discovered in 2021 by imaging. A year there lasts 30.4 years. It is 14.01 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 30.4 years, so a single orbit takes 30.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 132.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 133 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14× Earth's radius, or 1.25× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.96% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 19.5× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,429 K (1,156 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives only 2.5% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 206893, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,500 K.
