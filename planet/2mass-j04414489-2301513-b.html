2MASS J04414489+2301513 b

2MASS J04414489+2301513 b is a gas giant exoplanet 393 light-years away, orbiting 2MASS J04414489+2301513. Discovered in 2010 by imaging. A year there lasts 421.5 years. It is 12.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 421.5 years, so a single orbit takes 421.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 392.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 393 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.6× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 24.8% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 7.5× Jupiter in mass (2,384× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 18 K (-256 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits 2MASS J04414489+2301513, a red dwarf (M) star at 2,936 K.
