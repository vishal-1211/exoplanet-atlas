HD 219077 b

HD 219077 b is a gas giant exoplanet 95 light-years away, orbiting HD 219077. Discovered in 2012 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 15 years. It is 12.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 15 years, so a single orbit takes 15 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 95.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 95 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.5× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.36% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 9.62× Jupiter in mass (3,058× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 147 K (-126 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 7.5% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.77). It is 8 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
