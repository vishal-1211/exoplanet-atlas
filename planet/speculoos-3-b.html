SPECULOOS-3 b

SPECULOOS-3 b is an earth-sized exoplanet, orbiting SPECULOOS-3. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 17.3 hours. It is 0.98 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 17.3 hours.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.53% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.89× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be close to Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 66 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 553 K (280 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 15.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.0073 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
