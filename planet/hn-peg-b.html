HN Peg b

HN Peg b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 59 light-years away, orbiting HN Peg. Discovered in 2006 by imaging. A year there lasts 20,680 years. It is 11.78 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 20,680 years, so a single orbit takes 20,680 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 59.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 59 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.8× Earth's radius, or 1.05× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 22× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 11 K (-262 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 773 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
