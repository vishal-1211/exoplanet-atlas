HD 33844 c

HD 33844 c is a gas giant exoplanet 345 light-years away, orbiting HD 33844. Discovered in 2015 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.5 years. It is 13.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.5 years, so a single orbit takes 2.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 345 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 345 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.5× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.75× Jupiter in mass (556.2× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 360 K (87 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 33844, a orange (K) star at 4,861 K.
