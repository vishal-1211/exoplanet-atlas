OGLE-2017-BLG-1522L b

OGLE-2017-BLG-1522L b is a gas giant exoplanet 24,429 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2017-BLG-1522L. Discovered in 2018 by microlensing. A year there lasts 2.1 years. It is 14 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.1 years, so a single orbit takes 2.1 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 24,429 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 24,429 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14× Earth's radius, or 1.25× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 0.76× Jupiter in mass (240× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.48 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • It was one of 308 planets discovered in 2018.
