HD 5891 b

HD 5891 b is a gas giant exoplanet 925 light-years away, orbiting HD 5891. Discovered in 2011 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 177.1 days. It is 12.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 177.1 days, so about 2.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 924.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 925 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.6× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 7.63× Jupiter in mass (2,425× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 919 K (646 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 96.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 5891, a orange (K) star at 4,673 K.
