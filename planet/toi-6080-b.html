TOI-6080 b

TOI-6080 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 614 light-years away, orbiting TOI-6080. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 8.1 days. It is 3.32 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.1 days, so about 45.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 613.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 614 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.32× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 919 K (646 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 168.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.079 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
