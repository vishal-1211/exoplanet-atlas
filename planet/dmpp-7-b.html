DMPP-7 b

DMPP-7 b is a gas giant exoplanet 331 light-years away, orbiting DMPP-7. Discovered in 2026 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 4.9 days. It is 9.78 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.9 days, so about 74.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 330.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 331 years ago.
  • Its radius is 9.78× Earth's radius, or 0.87× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.36% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 69× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,456 K (1,183 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 749.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.061 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
