LTT 3780 b

LTT 3780 b is a super-earth exoplanet 72 light-years away, orbiting LTT 3780. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 18.4 hours. It is 1.33 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 18.4 hours.
  • It is 71.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 72 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.32× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.46× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.4× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 98 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 903 K (630 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 111 times as much starlight as Earth does.
