Kepler-82 c

Kepler-82 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 2,950 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-82. Discovered in 2012 by transit. A year there lasts 51.5 days. It is 5.34 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 51.5 days, so about 7.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,950 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,950 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.34× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.3% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 13.9× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.5 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 450 K (177 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
