Kepler-30 c

Kepler-30 c is a gas giant exoplanet 2,982 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-30. Discovered in 2012 by transit. A year there lasts 60.3 days. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 60.3 days, so about 6.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,982 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,982 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.41% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.01× Jupiter in mass (640× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 411 K (138 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 6.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.3 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
