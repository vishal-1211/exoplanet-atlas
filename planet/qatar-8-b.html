Qatar-8 b

Qatar-8 b is a gas giant exoplanet 918 light-years away, orbiting Qatar-8. Discovered in 2019 by transit. A year there lasts 3.7 days. It is 14.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.7 days, so about 98.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 918.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 918 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.4× Earth's radius, or 1.29× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 117.9× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.22 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,457 K (1,184 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 698.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
