TOI-500 e

TOI-500 e is a neptune-like exoplanet 155 light-years away, orbiting TOI-500. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 61.3 days. It is 3.99 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 61.3 days, so about 6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 154.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 155 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.99× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.29% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 15.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 336 K (63 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.275 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
