Kepler-396 b

Kepler-396 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 730 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-396. Discovered in 2013 by transit. A year there lasts 43 days. It is 3.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 43 days, so about 8.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 729.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 730 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.5× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.09% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 75.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 496 K (223 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 13.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.241 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
