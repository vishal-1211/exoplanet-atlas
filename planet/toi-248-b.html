TOI-248 b

TOI-248 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 248 light-years away, orbiting TOI-248. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 6 days. It is 2.37 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6 days, so about 61 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 247.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 248 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.37× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.21× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 77 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,447 K (1,174 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 728.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
