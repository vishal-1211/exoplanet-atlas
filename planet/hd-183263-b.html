HD 183263 b

HD 183263 b is a gas giant exoplanet 177 light-years away, orbiting HD 183263. Discovered in 2004 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.7 years. It is 13.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.7 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 176.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 177 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.1× Earth's radius, or 1.17× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.95% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.47× Jupiter in mass (1,103× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 263 K (-1 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 183263, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,948 K.
  • HD 183263 has 2 known planets.
