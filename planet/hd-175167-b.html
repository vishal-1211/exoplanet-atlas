HD 175167 b

HD 175167 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 232 light-years away, orbiting HD 175167. Discovered in 2009 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.2 years. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.2 years, so a single orbit takes 3.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 231.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 232 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.41% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 14.8× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 232 K (-41 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.51). It is 3 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits HD 175167, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,635 K.
