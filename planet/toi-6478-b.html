TOI-6478 b

TOI-6478 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 126 light-years away, orbiting TOI-6478. Discovered in 2025 by transit. A year there lasts 34 days. It is 4.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 34 days, so about 10.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 125.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 126 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.6× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 3.24% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 19.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 204 K (-69 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.03% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 0.114 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
