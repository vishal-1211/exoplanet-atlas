HD 114783 b

HD 114783 b is a gas giant exoplanet 69 light-years away, orbiting HD 114783. Discovered in 2001 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.4 years. It is 13.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.4 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 68.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 69 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.7× Earth's radius, or 1.22× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.23% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.1× Jupiter in mass (349.6× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.75 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 211 K (-62 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 114783, a orange (K) star at 5,135 K.
