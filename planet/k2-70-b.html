K2-70 b

K2-70 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 760 light-years away, orbiting K2-70. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 14 days. It is 2.89 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 14 days, so about 26.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 759.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 760 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.89× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.7× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 784 K (511 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 62 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.111 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
