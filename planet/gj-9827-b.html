GJ 9827 b

GJ 9827 b is a super-earth exoplanet 97 light-years away, orbiting GJ 9827. Discovered in 2017 by transit. A year there lasts 1.2 days. It is 1.44 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.2 days, so about 302.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 96.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 97 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.44× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.28× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 2.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 144 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,035 K (762 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 318 times as much starlight as Earth does.
