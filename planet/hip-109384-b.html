HIP 109384 b

HIP 109384 b is a gas giant exoplanet 192 light-years away, orbiting HIP 109384. Discovered in 2016 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.4 years. It is 13.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.4 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 192.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 192 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.5× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.21% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.56× Jupiter in mass (495.8× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 214 K (-59 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.55). It is 3 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits HIP 109384, a orange (K) star at 5,180 K.
