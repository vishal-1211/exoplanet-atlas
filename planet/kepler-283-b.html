Kepler-283 b

Kepler-283 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,527 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-283. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 11 days. It is 2.13 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11 days, so about 33.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,527 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,527 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.13× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.12% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.18× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 488 K (215 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 16.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.082 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
