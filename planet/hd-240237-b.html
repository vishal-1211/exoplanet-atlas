HD 240237 b

HD 240237 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 3,044 light-years away, orbiting HD 240237. Discovered in 2011 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2 years. It is 12.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2 years, so a single orbit takes 2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 3,044 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,044 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.2× Earth's radius, or 1.09× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.00025% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 15.9× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,153 K (880 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 83.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 240237, a orange (K) star at 3,926 K.
