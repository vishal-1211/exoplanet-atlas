HD 168863 b

HD 168863 b is a gas giant exoplanet 130 light-years away, orbiting HD 168863. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 40.4 years. It is 12.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 40.4 years, so a single orbit takes 40.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 130.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 130 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.7× Earth's radius, or 1.13× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.24% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 6.78× Jupiter in mass (2,155× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 63 K (-210 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.26% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 168863, a orange (K) star at 4,905 K.
