Kepler-367 b

Kepler-367 b is a super-earth exoplanet 613 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-367. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 37.8 days. It is 1.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 37.8 days, so about 9.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 613.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 613 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.3× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.24× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 401 K (128 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.201 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
