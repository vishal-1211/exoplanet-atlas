OGLE-2014-BLG-1722L c

OGLE-2014-BLG-1722L c is a gas giant exoplanet 20,874 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2014-BLG-1722L. Discovered in 2018 by microlensing. A year there lasts 3.5 years. It is 11 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.5 years, so a single orbit takes 3.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 20,874 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 20,874 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11× Earth's radius, or 0.98× Jupiter's.
  • It is 83.7× Earth's mass.
  • OGLE-2014-BLG-1722L has 2 known planets.
  • It was one of 308 planets discovered in 2018.
