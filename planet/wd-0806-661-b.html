WD 0806-661 b

WD 0806-661 b is a gas giant exoplanet 63 light-years away, orbiting WD 0806-661. Discovered in 2011 by imaging. A year there lasts 160,706 years. It is 12.11 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 160,706 years, so a single orbit takes 160,706 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 62.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 63 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.1× Earth's radius, or 1.08× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 7× Jupiter in mass (2,225× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 357 K (84 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits at 2,500 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
  • It orbits WD 0806-661, a blue-white (B) star at 10,393 K.
  • It was one of 141 planets discovered in 2011.
