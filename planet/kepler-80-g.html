Kepler-80 g

Kepler-80 g is an earth-sized exoplanet 1,205 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-80. Discovered in 2017 by transit. A year there lasts 14.6 days. It is 1.13 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 14.6 days, so about 24.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,205 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,205 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.13× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • Its mass is only 0.07× Earth's, lighter than Mars.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 0.05× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 4 kg.
  • Its average density is 0.25 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 418 K (145 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
