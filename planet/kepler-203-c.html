Kepler-203 c

Kepler-203 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 2,294 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-203. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 5.4 days. It is 2.47 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.4 days, so about 68 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,294 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,294 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.47× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.36× Jupiter in mass (749.9× Earth).
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 122.9× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 8,604 kg.
  • Its average density is 274.2 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,096 K (823 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
