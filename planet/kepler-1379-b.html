Kepler-1379 b

Kepler-1379 b is a super-earth exoplanet 3,168 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1379. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 21.2 hours. It is 1.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 21.2 hours.
  • It is 3,168 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,168 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.3× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.24× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,564 K (1,291 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,600 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.017 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
