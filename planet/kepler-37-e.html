Kepler-37 e

Kepler-37 e is a sub-earth exoplanet 208 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-37. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 51.2 days. It is 0.37 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 51.2 days, so about 7.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 208.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 208 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.37× Earth's radius).
  • It is the 3rd smallest planet in the catalogue by radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0019% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.1× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 59.2× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 4,142 kg.
  • Its average density is 881.1 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
