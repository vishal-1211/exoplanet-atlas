HD 31527 b

HD 31527 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 125 light-years away, orbiting HD 31527. Discovered in 2019 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 16.6 days. It is 3.22 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 16.6 days, so about 22.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 125.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 125 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.22× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 10.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 832 K (559 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 76.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.125 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
