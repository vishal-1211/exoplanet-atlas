LHS 1678 b

LHS 1678 b is a sub-earth exoplanet 65 light-years away, orbiting LHS 1678. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 20.6 hours. It is 0.69 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 20.6 hours.
  • It is 64.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 65 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.69× Earth's radius).
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.35× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 0.75× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 52 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 830 K (557 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 94.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
