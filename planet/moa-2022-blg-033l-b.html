MOA-2022-BLG-033L b

MOA-2022-BLG-033L b is a neptune-like exoplanet 6,001 light-years away, orbiting MOA-2022-BLG-033L. Discovered in 2025 by microlensing. A year there lasts 4.4 years. It is 3.52 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.4 years, so a single orbit takes 4.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 6,001 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 6,001 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.52× Earth's radius.
  • It is 12.2× Earth's mass.
  • It was one of 246 planets discovered in 2025.
