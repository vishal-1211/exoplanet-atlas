mu2 Sco b

mu2 Sco b is a super-jupiter exoplanet, orbiting mu2 Sco. Discovered in 2022 by imaging. A year there lasts 1,251 years. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1,251 years, so a single orbit takes 1,251 times longer than Earth's.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 14.4× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 159 K (-114 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 242 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.56). It is 4 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits mu2 Sco, a blue-white (B) star at 21,700 K.
