HD 15906 c

HD 15906 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 149 light-years away, orbiting HD 15906. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 21.6 days. It is 2.93 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 21.6 days, so about 16.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 148.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 149 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.93× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.12% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.91× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 532 K (259 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 13.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.141 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
