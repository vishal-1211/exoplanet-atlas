HIP 109600 b

HIP 109600 b is a gas giant exoplanet 216 light-years away, orbiting HIP 109600. Discovered in 2016 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 232.1 days. It is 13.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 232.1 days, so about 1.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 216.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 216 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.2× Earth's radius, or 1.18× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.55% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.68× Jupiter in mass (851.8× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 313 K (40 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 1.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HIP 109600, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,530 K.
