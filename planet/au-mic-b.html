AU Mic b

AU Mic b is a neptune-like exoplanet 32 light-years away, orbiting AU Mic. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 8.5 days. It is 4.79 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.5 days, so about 43.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 31.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 32 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.79× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.26% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.99× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.45 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 555 K (282 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 21.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
