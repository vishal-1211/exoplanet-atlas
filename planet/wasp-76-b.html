WASP-76 b

WASP-76 b is a gas giant exoplanet 634 light-years away, orbiting WASP-76. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 1.8 days. It is 20.78 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • Its night side may have iron rain: iron vaporised on the scorching day side is carried by winds to the cooler night side and condenses.
  • One year here lasts 1.8 days, so about 201.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 634.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 634 years ago.
  • Its radius is 20.8× Earth's radius, or 1.85× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.17% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.89× Jupiter in mass (284.1× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.17 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,228 K (1,955 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 4,104 times as much starlight as Earth does.
