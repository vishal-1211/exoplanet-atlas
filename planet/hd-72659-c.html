HD 72659 c

HD 72659 c is a super-jupiter exoplanet 169 light-years away, orbiting HD 72659. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 97.2 years. It is 12.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 97.2 years, so a single orbit takes 97.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 169.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 169 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.1× Earth's radius, or 1.08× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.66% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 19.4× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 72 K (-201 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.51% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 72659, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,929 K.
