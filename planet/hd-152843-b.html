HD 152843 b

HD 152843 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 352 light-years away, orbiting HD 152843. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 11.6 days. It is 3.05 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11.6 days, so about 31.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 351.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 352 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.05× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 9.82× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,003 K (730 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 265.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.105 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
