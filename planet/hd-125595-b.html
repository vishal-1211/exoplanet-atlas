HD 125595 b

HD 125595 b is a gas giant exoplanet 92 light-years away, orbiting HD 125595. Discovered in 2011 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 9.7 days. It is 12.38 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.7 days, so about 37.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 92 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 92 years ago.
  • It weighs 11.1× Jupiter in mass (3,531× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 688 K (415 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 37.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.08 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
  • It orbits HD 125595, a orange (K) star at 4,691 K.
