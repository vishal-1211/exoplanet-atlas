HD 192310 b

HD 192310 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 29 light-years away, orbiting HD 192310. Discovered in 2011 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 74.7 days. It is 4.27 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 74.7 days, so about 4.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 28.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 29 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.27× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.21% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 16.9× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 355 K (82 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 3.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.32 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
