HD 191939 f

HD 191939 f is a gas giant exoplanet 175 light-years away, orbiting HD 191939. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 7.9 years. It is 13.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.9 years, so a single orbit takes 7.9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 174.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 175 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.2× Earth's radius, or 1.18× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.65% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.88× Jupiter in mass (915.3× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 125 K (-148 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 4.7% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 191939, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,348 K.
