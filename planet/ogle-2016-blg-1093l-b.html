OGLE-2016-BLG-1093L b

OGLE-2016-BLG-1093L b is a gas giant exoplanet 26,484 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2016-BLG-1093L. Discovered in 2022 by microlensing. A year there lasts 4.6 years. It is 14 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.6 years, so a single orbit takes 4.6 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 26,484 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 26,484 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14× Earth's radius, or 1.25× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 0.71× Jupiter in mass (225.7× Earth).
  • It was one of 367 planets discovered in 2022.
