Kepler-165 c

Kepler-165 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,829 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-165. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 15.3 days. It is 2.23 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 15.3 days, so about 23.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,829 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,829 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.23× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.6× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 79 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 581 K (308 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 25.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
