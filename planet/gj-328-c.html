GJ 328 c

GJ 328 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 67 light-years away, orbiting GJ 328. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 241.8 days. It is 4.91 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 241.8 days, so about 1.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 66.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 67 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.91× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.51% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 21.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 184 K (-89 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits GJ 328, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,897 K.
  • GJ 328 has 2 known planets.
