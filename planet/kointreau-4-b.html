KOINTREAU-4 b

KOINTREAU-4 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 474 light-years away, orbiting 2MASS J16262785-2625152. Discovered in 2026 by imaging. It is 12.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • It is 474.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 474 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.2× Earth's radius, or 1.09× Jupiter's.
  • At 15.3× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • It orbits at 182 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
  • It orbits 2MASS J16262785-2625152, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,252 K.
  • It was one of 355 planets discovered in 2026.
