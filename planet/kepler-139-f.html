Kepler-139 f

Kepler-139 f is a gas giant exoplanet 1,275 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-139. Discovered in 2025 by transit timing variations. A year there lasts 355 days. It is 6.67 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 355 days, so about 1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,275 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,275 years ago.
  • Its radius is 6.67× Earth's radius, or 0.6× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.34% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 36× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.67 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its estimated temperature is 277 K (4 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits Kepler-139, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,630 K.
