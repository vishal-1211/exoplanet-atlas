KMT-2021-BLG-2609L b

KMT-2021-BLG-2609L b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 24,396 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2021-BLG-2609L. Discovered in 2024 by microlensing. A year there lasts 7.8 years. It is 3.17 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.8 years, so a single orbit takes 7.8 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 24,396 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 24,396 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.17× Earth's radius.
  • It is 10.2× Earth's mass.
  • It was one of 260 planets discovered in 2024.
