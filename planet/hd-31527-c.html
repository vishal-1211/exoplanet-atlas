HD 31527 c

HD 31527 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 125 light-years away, orbiting HD 31527. Discovered in 2019 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 51.2 days. It is 3.85 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 51.2 days, so about 7.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 125.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 125 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.85× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.11% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 14.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 571 K (298 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 16.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.266 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
