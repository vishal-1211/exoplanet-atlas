Kepler-128 b

Kepler-128 b is a super-earth exoplanet 1,293 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-128. Discovered in 2013 by transit. A year there lasts 15 days. It is 1.42 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 15 days, so about 24.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,293 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,293 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.42× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0062% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 3.79× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.9× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 131 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 968 K (695 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 227.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
