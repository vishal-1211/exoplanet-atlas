GJ 687 c

GJ 687 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 15 light-years away, orbiting GJ 687. Discovered in 2020 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2 years. It is 4.14 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 14.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 15 years ago. That makes it the 35th closest of the 6,445 known planets.
  • Its radius is 4.14× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.82% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 16× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 99 K (-175 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 1.6% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits GJ 687, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,413 K.
