K2-255 b

K2-255 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 625 light-years away, orbiting K2-255. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 2 days. It is 2.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2 days, so about 186 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 624.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 625 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.9× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.75× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,034 K (761 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 215.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.027 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
