Kepler-607 c

Kepler-607 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,693 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-607. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 39.7 days. It is 2.76 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 39.7 days, so about 9.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,693 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,693 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.76× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.05× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 473 K (200 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 8.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.215 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
