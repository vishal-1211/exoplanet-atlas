TOI-2498 b

TOI-2498 b is a gas giant exoplanet 898 light-years away, orbiting TOI-2498. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 3.7 days. It is 6.06 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.7 days, so about 97.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 897.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 898 years ago.
  • Its radius is 6.06× Earth's radius, or 0.54× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.19% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 34.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.86 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,443 K (1,170 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 790.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
