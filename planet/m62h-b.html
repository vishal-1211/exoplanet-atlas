M62H b

M62H b is a gas giant exoplanet, orbiting M62H. Discovered in 2024 by pulsar timing. A year there lasts 3.2 hours. It is 13.23 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 3.2 hours.
  • It weighs 2.47× Jupiter in mass (785× Earth).
  • It orbits at 0.0057 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
  • It was one of 260 planets discovered in 2024.
