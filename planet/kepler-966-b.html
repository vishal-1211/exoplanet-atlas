Kepler-966 b

Kepler-966 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 3,352 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-966. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 99.7 days. It is 4.13 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 99.7 days, so about 3.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,352 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,352 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.13× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.14% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 16× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 391 K (118 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 11 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits Kepler-966, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,840 K.
