HAT-P-47 b

HAT-P-47 b is a gas giant exoplanet 920 light-years away, orbiting HAT-P-47. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 4.7 days. It is 14.72 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.7 days, so about 77.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 920.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 920 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.7× Earth's radius, or 1.31× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.79% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 65.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.11 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,605 K (1,332 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 379.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
