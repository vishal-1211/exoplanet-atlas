Kepler-62 f

Kepler-62 f is a super-earth exoplanet 981 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-62. Discovered in 2013 by transit. A year there lasts 267.3 days. It is 1.41 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 267.3 days, so about 1.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 981.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 981 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.41× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 35× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 17.6× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 1,232 kg.
  • Its average density is 68.8 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 208 K (-65 °C), below the freezing point of water.
