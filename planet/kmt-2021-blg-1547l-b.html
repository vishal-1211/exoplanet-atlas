KMT-2021-BLG-1547L b

KMT-2021-BLG-1547L b is a gas giant exoplanet 16,536 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2021-BLG-1547L. Discovered in 2023 by microlensing. A year there lasts 11.3 years. It is 13.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11.3 years, so a single orbit takes 11.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 16,536 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 16,536 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.6× Earth's radius, or 1.21× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 1.47× Jupiter in mass (467.2× Earth).
  • It was one of 323 planets discovered in 2023.
