K2-131 b

K2-131 b is a super-earth exoplanet 497 light-years away, orbiting K2-131. Discovered in 2017 by transit. A year there lasts 8.9 hours. It is 1.69 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 8.9 hours.
  • It is 497.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 497 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.69× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 7.9× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 2.8× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 194 kg.
  • Its average density is 9 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,223 K (1,950 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
