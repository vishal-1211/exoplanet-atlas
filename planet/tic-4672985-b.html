TIC 4672985 b

TIC 4672985 b is a gas giant exoplanet 826 light-years away, orbiting TIC 4672985. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 69 days. It is 11.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 69 days, so about 5.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 826.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 826 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.5× Earth's radius, or 1.03× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.84% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 12.7× Jupiter in mass (4,049× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 517 K (244 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 11.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.33 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
