rho CrB e

rho CrB e is a super-earth exoplanet 57 light-years away, orbiting rho CrB. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 12.9 days. It is 1.75 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 12.9 days, so about 28.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 57 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 57 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.75× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 3.71× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.2× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 85 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,005 K (732 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 151.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
