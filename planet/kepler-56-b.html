Kepler-56 b

Kepler-56 b is a gas giant exoplanet 2,978 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-56. Discovered in 2012 by transit. A year there lasts 10.5 days. It is 6.51 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 10.5 days, so about 34.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,978 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,978 years ago.
  • Its radius is 6.51× Earth's radius, or 0.58× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 22.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.44 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,378 K (1,105 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 819.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
