HR 858 d

HR 858 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 104 light-years away, orbiting HR 858. Discovered in 2019 by transit. A year there lasts 11.2 days. It is 2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11.2 days, so about 32.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 104.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 104 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 7.1× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.8× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 124 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,061 K (788 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 217 times as much starlight as Earth does.
