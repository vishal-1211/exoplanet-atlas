K2-138 c

K2-138 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 661 light-years away, orbiting K2-138. Discovered in 2017 by transit. A year there lasts 3.6 days. It is 2.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.6 days, so about 102.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 660.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 661 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.3× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.31× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.2× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 84 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,012 K (739 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 279 times as much starlight as Earth does.
