UZ For c

UZ For c is a gas giant exoplanet 781 light-years away, orbiting UZ For. Discovered in 2011 by eclipse timing variations. A year there lasts 5.2 years. It is 12.58 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.2 years, so a single orbit takes 5.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 781.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 781 years ago.
  • It weighs 7.7× Jupiter in mass (2,447× Earth).
  • It orbits UZ For, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,287 K.
  • UZ For has 2 known planets.
  • UZ For is part of a system with 2 stars.
  • It was one of 141 planets discovered in 2011.
