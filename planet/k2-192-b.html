K2-192 b

K2-192 b is a super-earth exoplanet 723 light-years away, orbiting K2-192. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 4.2 days. It is 1.52 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.2 days, so about 87.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 723.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 723 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.52× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.92× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 939 K (666 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 181.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.049 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
