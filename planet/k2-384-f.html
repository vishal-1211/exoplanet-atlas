K2-384 f

K2-384 f is a mini-neptune exoplanet 270 light-years away, orbiting K2-384. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 13.6 days. It is 2.22 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 13.6 days, so about 26.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 269.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 270 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.22× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.34% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.57× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 79 kg.
  • Its estimated temperature is 371 K (98 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 3.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
