TOI-1776 b

TOI-1776 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 146 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1776. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 2.8 days. It is 1.22 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.8 days, so about 130.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 145.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 146 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.22× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.4× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be close to Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 66 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,269 K (996 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 560.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
