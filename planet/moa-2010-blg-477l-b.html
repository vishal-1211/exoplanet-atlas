MOA-2010-BLG-477L b

MOA-2010-BLG-477L b is a gas giant exoplanet 7,502 light-years away, orbiting MOA-2010-BLG-477L. Discovered in 2012 by microlensing. A year there lasts 3.5 years. It is 13.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.5 years, so a single orbit takes 3.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 7,502 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 7,502 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.6× Earth's radius, or 1.21× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 1.51× Jupiter in mass (480× Earth).
  • It orbits MOA-2010-BLG-477L, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,950 K.
  • It was one of 144 planets discovered in 2012.
