HATS-59 c

HATS-59 c is a gas giant exoplanet 2,097 light-years away, orbiting HATS-59. Discovered in 2018 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.9 years. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.9 years, so a single orbit takes 3.9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 2,097 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,097 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.18% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 12.7× Jupiter in mass (4,036× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 176 K (-97 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HATS-59, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,670 K.
  • HATS-59 has 2 known planets.
