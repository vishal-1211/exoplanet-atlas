HD 86226 b

HD 86226 b is a gas giant exoplanet 149 light-years away, orbiting HD 86226. Discovered in 2009 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 4.5 years. It is 14.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.5 years, so a single orbit takes 4.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 149 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 149 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.3× Earth's radius, or 1.28× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.55% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 143× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 176 K (-97 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 86226, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,863 K.
  • HD 86226 has 2 known planets.
