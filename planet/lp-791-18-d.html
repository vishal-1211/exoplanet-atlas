LP 791-18 d

LP 791-18 d is an earth-sized exoplanet 86 light-years away, orbiting LP 791-18. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 2.8 days. It is 1.03 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.8 days, so about 132.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 86.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 86 years ago.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.27% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.91× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 0.85× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 60 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 396 K (122 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 5.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
