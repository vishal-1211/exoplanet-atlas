KMT-2018-BLG-1743L b

KMT-2018-BLG-1743L b is a gas giant exoplanet 21,145 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2018-BLG-1743L. Discovered in 2021 by microlensing. A year there lasts 4 years. It is 10.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4 years, so a single orbit takes 4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 21,145 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 21,145 years ago.
  • Its radius is 10.5× Earth's radius, or 0.94× Jupiter's.
  • It is 77.9× Earth's mass.
  • It was one of 564 planets discovered in 2021.
