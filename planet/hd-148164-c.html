HD 148164 c

HD 148164 c is a gas giant exoplanet 252 light-years away, orbiting HD 148164. Discovered in 2018 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 13.9 years. It is 12.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 13.9 years, so a single orbit takes 13.9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 251.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 252 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.8× Earth's radius, or 1.14× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.77% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 5.16× Jupiter in mass (1,640× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 136 K (-137 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 5.7% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 148164, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,032 K.
