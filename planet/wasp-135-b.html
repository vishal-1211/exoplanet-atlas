WASP-135 b

WASP-135 b is a gas giant exoplanet 973 light-years away, orbiting WASP-135. Discovered in 2015 by transit. A year there lasts 1.4 days. It is 14.57 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.4 days, so about 260.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 972.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 973 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.6× Earth's radius, or 1.3× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.93% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.9× Jupiter in mass (603.9× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,404 K (1,131 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 917.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.024 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
