TOI-1448 b

TOI-1448 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 239 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1448. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 8.1 days. It is 2.75 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.1 days, so about 45 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 239.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 239 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.75× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.44% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 19.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 426 K (153 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 5.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.057 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
