PDS 70 c

PDS 70 c is a gas giant exoplanet 369 light-years away, orbiting PDS 70. Discovered in 2019 by imaging. A year there lasts 202.7 years. It is 22.19 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 202.7 years, so a single orbit takes 202.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 368.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 369 years ago.
  • Its radius is 22.2× Earth's radius, or 1.98× Jupiter's.
  • It is the 15th largest planet in the catalogue by radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.6% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 7.5× Jupiter in mass (2,384× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,054 K (781 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives only 0.03% of the starlight Earth gets.
