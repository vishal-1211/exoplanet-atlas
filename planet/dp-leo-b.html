DP Leo b

DP Leo b is a gas giant exoplanet 997 light-years away, orbiting DP Leo. Discovered in 2009 by eclipse timing variations. A year there lasts 28 years. It is 12.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 28 years, so a single orbit takes 28 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 997 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 997 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.7× Earth's radius, or 1.13× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 6.28× Jupiter in mass (1,996× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 24 K (-249 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.01% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits DP Leo, a blue-white (B) star at 13,500 K.
  • DP Leo is part of a system with 2 stars.
