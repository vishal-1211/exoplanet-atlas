LkCa 15 b

LkCa 15 b is a not identified exoplanet 516 light-years away, orbiting LkCa 15. Discovered in 2015 by imaging.

Interesting facts

  • It is 515.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 516 years ago.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 67 K (-206 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.3% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits LkCa 15, a orange (K) star at 4,194 K.
  • LkCa 15 has 2 known planets.
  • It was one of 155 planets discovered in 2015.
  • NASA flags this planet's status as debated in the scientific literature, so its details may change.
