OGLE-2012-BLG-0406L b

OGLE-2012-BLG-0406L b is a gas giant exoplanet 16,210 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2012-BLG-0406L. Discovered in 2013 by microlensing. A year there lasts 9.7 years. It is 13.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.7 years, so a single orbit takes 9.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 16,210 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 16,210 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.2× Earth's radius, or 1.18× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 2.73× Jupiter in mass (868× Earth).
  • It was one of 128 planets discovered in 2013.
