HD 207832 b

HD 207832 b is a gas giant exoplanet 192 light-years away, orbiting HD 207832. Discovered in 2012 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 160.1 days. It is 14.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 160.1 days, so about 2.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 192.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 192 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.2× Earth's radius, or 1.27× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.13% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.56× Jupiter in mass (178× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.34 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 340 K (67 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
