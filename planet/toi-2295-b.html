TOI-2295 b

TOI-2295 b is a gas giant exoplanet 412 light-years away, orbiting TOI-2295. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 30 days. It is 16.48 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 30 days, so about 12.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 411.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 412 years ago.
  • Its radius is 16.5× Earth's radius, or 1.47× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.87× Jupiter in mass (278.1× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.34 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 747 K (474 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 42.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
