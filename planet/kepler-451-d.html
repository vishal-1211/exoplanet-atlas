Kepler-451 d

Kepler-451 d is a gas giant exoplanet 1,293 light-years away, orbiting 2MASS J19383260+4603591. Discovered in 2022 by eclipse timing variations. A year there lasts 43 days. It is 13.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 43 days, so about 8.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,293 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,293 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.5× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 37.1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.76× Jupiter in mass (559.4× Earth).
  • Its estimated temperature is 1,481 K (1,208 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 801.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.188 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
