GJ 1002 c

GJ 1002 c is an earth-sized exoplanet 16 light-years away, orbiting GJ 1002. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 21.2 days. It is 1.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 21.2 days, so about 17.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 15.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 16 years ago. That makes it the 41st closest of the 6,451 known planets.
  • Its radius is 1.1× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.54% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.36× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 182 K (-91 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.074 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
