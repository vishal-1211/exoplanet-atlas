K2-187 b

K2-187 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 1,079 light-years away, orbiting K2-187. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 18.6 hours. It is 1.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 18.6 hours.
  • It is 1,079 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,079 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.2× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.87× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,815 K (1,542 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 2,367 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.016 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
