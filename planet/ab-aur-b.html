AB Aur b

AB Aur b is a gas giant exoplanet 529 light-years away, orbiting AB Aur. Discovered in 2022 by imaging. A year there lasts 587.3 years. It is 12.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 587.3 years, so a single orbit takes 587.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 528.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 529 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.5× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 9× Jupiter in mass (2,860× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 84 K (-190 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.67% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 94 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
