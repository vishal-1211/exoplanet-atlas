HAT-P-13 b

HAT-P-13 b is a gas giant exoplanet 805 light-years away, orbiting HAT-P-13. Discovered in 2009 by transit. A year there lasts 2.9 days. It is 14.26 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.9 days, so about 125.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 805 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 805 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.3× Earth's radius, or 1.27× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.55% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.85× Jupiter in mass (270.5× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.51 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,740 K (1,467 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,979 times as much starlight as Earth does.
