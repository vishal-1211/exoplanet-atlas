K2-60 b

K2-60 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,604 light-years away, orbiting K2-60. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 3 days. It is 7.66 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3 days, so about 121.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,604 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,604 years ago.
  • Its radius is 7.66× Earth's radius, or 0.68× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.39% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 135.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,400 K (1,127 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,212 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.045 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
