HD 160691 c

HD 160691 c is a gas giant exoplanet 51 light-years away, orbiting HD 160691. Discovered in 2004 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 10.8 years. It is 12.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 10.8 years, so a single orbit takes 10.8 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 50.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 51 years ago.
  • It weighs 4.4× Jupiter in mass (1,398× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 157 K (-116 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 160691, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,773 K.
  • HD 160691 has 4 known planets.
  • It was one of 27 planets discovered in 2004.
