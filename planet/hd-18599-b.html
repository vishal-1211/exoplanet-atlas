HD 18599 b

HD 18599 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 126 light-years away, orbiting HD 18599. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 4.1 days. It is 2.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.1 days, so about 88.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 125.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 126 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.6× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 24.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 863 K (590 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 145 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.048 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
