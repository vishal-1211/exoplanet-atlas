BD-06 1339 b

BD-06 1339 b is a gas giant exoplanet 66 light-years away, orbiting BD-06 1339. Discovered in 2013 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.9 days. It is 12.85 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.9 days, so about 94.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 66.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 66 years ago.
  • It weighs 4.79× Jupiter in mass (1,523× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 835 K (562 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 51.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.043 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
  • It orbits BD-06 1339, a orange (K) star at 4,324 K.
