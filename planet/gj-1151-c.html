GJ 1151 c

GJ 1151 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 26 light-years away, orbiting GJ 1151. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 389.7 days. It is 3.25 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 389.7 days, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 26.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 26 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.25× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.79% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 10.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 88 K (-185 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 1% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits GJ 1151, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,280 K.
