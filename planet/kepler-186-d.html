Kepler-186 d

Kepler-186 d is a super-earth exoplanet 579 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-186. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 13.3 days. It is 1.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 13.3 days, so about 27.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 579.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 579 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.4× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.54× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 379 K (106 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 7.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.078 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
