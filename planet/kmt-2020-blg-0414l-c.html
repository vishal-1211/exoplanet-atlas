KMT-2020-BLG-0414L c

KMT-2020-BLG-0414L c is a super-jupiter exoplanet 3,979 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2020-BLG-0414L. Discovered in 2021 by microlensing. A year there lasts 150.4 years. It is 11.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 150.4 years, so a single orbit takes 150.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 3,979 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,979 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.9× Earth's radius, or 1.06× Jupiter's.
  • At 27× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • KMT-2020-BLG-0414L has 2 known planets.
  • It was one of 564 planets discovered in 2021.
