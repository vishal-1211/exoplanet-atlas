HD 125271 b

HD 125271 b is a gas giant exoplanet 128 light-years away, orbiting HD 125271. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 40.8 years. It is 12.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 40.8 years, so a single orbit takes 40.8 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 128.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 128 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.4× Earth's radius, or 1.11× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.28% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 10.9× Jupiter in mass (3,466× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 60 K (-213 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.21% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 125271, a orange (K) star at 4,704 K.
