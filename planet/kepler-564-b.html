Kepler-564 b

Kepler-564 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 2,815 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-564. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 3.8 days. It is 4.63 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.8 days, so about 97.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,815 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,815 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.63× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.21% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 19.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,129 K (856 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 389.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.048 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
