HD 163607 c

HD 163607 c is a gas giant exoplanet 221 light-years away, orbiting HD 163607. Discovered in 2012 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.5 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.5 years, so a single orbit takes 3.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 221.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 221 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.48% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.2× Jupiter in mass (699.5× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 229 K (-45 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 163607, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,522 K.
  • HD 163607 has 2 known planets.
