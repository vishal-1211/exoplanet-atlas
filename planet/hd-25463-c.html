HD 25463 c

HD 25463 c is a super-earth exoplanet 149 light-years away, orbiting HD 25463. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 3 days. It is 1.36 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3 days, so about 120 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 148.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 149 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.36× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0078% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.3× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 2.3× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 162 kg.
  • Its average density is 9.3 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,586 K (1,312 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
