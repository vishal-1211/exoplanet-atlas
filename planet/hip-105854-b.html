HIP 105854 b

HIP 105854 b is a gas giant exoplanet 256 light-years away, orbiting HIP 105854. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 184.2 days. It is 12.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 184.2 days, so about 2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 256.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 256 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.6× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 8.2× Jupiter in mass (2,606× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 822 K (549 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 71.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HIP 105854, a orange (K) star at 4,780 K.
