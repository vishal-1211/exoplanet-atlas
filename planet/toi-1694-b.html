TOI-1694 b

TOI-1694 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 407 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1694. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 3.8 days. It is 5.27 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.8 days, so about 96.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 406.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 407 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.27× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.35% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 28.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,037 K (764 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 195.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.044 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
