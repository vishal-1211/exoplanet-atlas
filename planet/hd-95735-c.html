HD 95735 c

HD 95735 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 19 light-years away, orbiting GJ 411. Discovered in 2021 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 8.1 years. It is 3.76 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.1 years, so a single orbit takes 8.1 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 18.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 19 years ago. That makes it the 50th closest of the 6,451 known planets.
  • Its radius is 3.76× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.87% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 13.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 63 K (-210 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.23% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits GJ 411, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,719 K.
