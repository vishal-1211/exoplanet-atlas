HIP 86221 b

HIP 86221 b is a gas giant exoplanet 96 light-years away, orbiting HIP 86221. Discovered in 2021 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.2 days. It is 14 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.2 days, so about 164.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 96.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 96 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14× Earth's radius, or 1.25× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 0.71× Jupiter in mass (225.7× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.45 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • It orbits at 0.031 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
  • It was one of 564 planets discovered in 2021.
