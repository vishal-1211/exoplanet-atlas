Kepler-975 c

Kepler-975 c is a gas giant exoplanet 1,390 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-975. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 5.1 days. It is 10.69 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.1 days, so about 72.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,390 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,390 years ago.
  • Its radius is 10.7× Earth's radius, or 0.95× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.34% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 80.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,168 K (895 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 439.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.057 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
