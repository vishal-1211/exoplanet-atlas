Kepler-405 b

Kepler-405 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 3,474 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-405. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 10.6 days. It is 2.08 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 10.6 days, so about 34.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,474 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,474 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.08× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.98× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.2× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 81 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 785 K (512 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 55.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
