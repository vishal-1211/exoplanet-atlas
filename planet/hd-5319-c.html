HD 5319 c

HD 5319 c is a gas giant exoplanet 396 light-years away, orbiting HD 5319. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.4 years. It is 13.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.4 years, so a single orbit takes 2.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 396 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 396 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.8× Earth's radius, or 1.23× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.05× Jupiter in mass (334.7× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 341 K (68 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 5319, a orange (K) star at 4,871 K.
