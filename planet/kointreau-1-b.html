KOINTREAU-1 b

KOINTREAU-1 b is a gas giant exoplanet 517 light-years away, orbiting XEST 17-036. Discovered in 2026 by imaging. A year there lasts 35,546 years. It is 12.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 35,546 years, so a single orbit takes 35,546 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 517.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 517 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.4× Earth's radius, or 1.11× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 10.6× Jupiter in mass (3,369× Earth).
  • It orbits at 690 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
  • It orbits XEST 17-036, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,551 K.
  • It was one of 355 planets discovered in 2026.
