bet Pic d

bet Pic d is a gas giant exoplanet 64 light-years away, orbiting bet Pic. Discovered in 2026 by imaging. A year there lasts 90.9 years. It is 14.12 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 90.9 years, so a single orbit takes 90.9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 64.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 64 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.1× Earth's radius, or 1.26× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.7% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.4× Jupiter in mass (762.8× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 600 K (327 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives only 1.3% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits bet Pic, a white (A) star at 8,039 K.
