Kepler-454 d

Kepler-454 d is a gas giant exoplanet 753 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-454. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 11.2 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11.2 years, so a single orbit takes 11.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 753 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 753 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.31% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.31× Jupiter in mass (734.2× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 125 K (-148 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 4.3% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits Kepler-454, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,687 K.
