Kepler-827 b

Kepler-827 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 6,675 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-827. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 51.9 days. It is 5.63 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 51.9 days, so about 7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 6,675 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 6,675 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.63× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.32% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 27× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 574 K (301 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 23.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.274 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
