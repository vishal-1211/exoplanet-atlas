GJ 3293 b

GJ 3293 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 66 light-years away, orbiting GJ 3293. Discovered in 2015 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 30.6 days. It is 5.19 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 30.6 days, so about 11.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 65.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 66 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.19× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.38% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 23.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 281 K (7 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits at 0.143 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits GJ 3293, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,466 K.
