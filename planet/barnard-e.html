Barnard e

Barnard e is a sub-earth exoplanet 6 light-years away, orbiting Barnard's star. Discovered in 2025 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 6.7 days. It is 0.64 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.7 days, so about 54.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 6 years ago. That makes it the 3rd closest of the 6,445 known planets.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.64× Earth's radius).
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.19× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 340 K (67 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.038 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
