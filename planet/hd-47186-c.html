HD 47186 c

HD 47186 c is a gas giant exoplanet 122 light-years away, orbiting HD 47186. Discovered in 2008 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.7 years. It is 13 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.7 years, so a single orbit takes 3.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 122.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 122 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13× Earth's radius, or 1.16× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.22% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 111.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 183 K (-90 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 47186, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,657 K.
  • HD 47186 has 2 known planets.
