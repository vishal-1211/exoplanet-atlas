KOI-351 g

KOI-351 g is a gas giant exoplanet 2,767 light-years away, orbiting KOI-351. Discovered in 2013 by transit. A year there lasts 210.7 days. It is 7.72 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 210.7 days, so about 1.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,767 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,767 years ago.
  • Its radius is 7.72× Earth's radius, or 0.69× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.36% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 15× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.18 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 342 K (69 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 3.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
