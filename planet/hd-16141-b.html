HD 16141 b

HD 16141 b is a gas giant exoplanet 123 light-years away, orbiting HD 16141. Discovered in 2000 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 75.5 days. It is 10.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 75.5 days, so about 4.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 123.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 123 years ago.
  • Its radius is 10.9× Earth's radius, or 0.97× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.45% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 82.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.35 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 563 K (290 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 17.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
