Kepler-150 f

Kepler-150 f is a neptune-like exoplanet 2,906 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-150. Discovered in 2017 by transit. A year there lasts 1.7 years. It is 3.64 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.7 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 2,906 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,906 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.64× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.14% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 12.9× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 228 K (-45 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits Kepler-150, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,528 K.
  • Kepler-150 has 5 known planets.
