TIC 139270665 b

TIC 139270665 b is a gas giant exoplanet 619 light-years away, orbiting TIC 139270665. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 23.6 days. It is 7.23 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 23.6 days, so about 15.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 619.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 619 years ago.
  • Its radius is 7.23× Earth's radius, or 0.64× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.42% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 147.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 703 K (430 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 40.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.163 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
