TOI-1749 d

TOI-1749 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 325 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1749. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 9 days. It is 2.52 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9 days, so about 40.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 324.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 325 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.52× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.18% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 15× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 533 K (260 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 14.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.071 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
