HD 28192 b

HD 28192 b is a gas giant exoplanet 162 light-years away, orbiting HD 28192. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 14.2 days. It is 12.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 14.2 days, so about 25.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 162.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 162 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.2× Earth's radius, or 1.09× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.09% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 99.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 860 K (587 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 91.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.118 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
