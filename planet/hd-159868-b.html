HD 159868 b

HD 159868 b is a gas giant exoplanet 182 light-years away, orbiting HD 159868. Discovered in 2006 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.2 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.2 years, so a single orbit takes 3.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 182.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 182 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.33% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.22× Jupiter in mass (704.9× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 256 K (-17 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 159868, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,534 K.
  • HD 159868 has 2 known planets.
