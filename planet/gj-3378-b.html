GJ 3378 b

GJ 3378 b is a super-earth exoplanet 25 light-years away, orbiting GJ 3378. Discovered in 2026 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 21.4 days. It is 1.32 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 21.4 days, so about 17 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 25.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 25 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.32× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.19% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.3× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 272 K (-1 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.097 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
