K2-18 c

K2-18 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 124 light-years away, orbiting K2-18. Discovered in 2017 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 9 days. It is 2.65 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9 days, so about 40.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 124 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 124 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.65× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.35% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 7.51× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 363 K (90 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.06 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
