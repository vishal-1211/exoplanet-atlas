EPIC 201595106 b

EPIC 201595106 b is a super-earth exoplanet 757 light-years away, orbiting EPIC 201595106. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 21.1 hours. It is 1.57 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 21.1 hours.
  • It is 757 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 757 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.56× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 7.67× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 3.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 219 kg.
  • Its average density is 11 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,038 K (1,765 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
