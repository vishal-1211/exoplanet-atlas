HD 202206 b

HD 202206 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 150 light-years away, orbiting HD 202206. Discovered in 2026 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 256.3 days. It is 12.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 256.3 days, so about 1.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 149.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 150 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.1× Earth's radius, or 1.08× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.14% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 21.5× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 315 K (42 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 1.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 202206, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,764 K.
