K2-268 f

K2-268 f is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,069 light-years away, orbiting K2-268. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 26.3 days. It is 2.23 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 26.3 days, so about 13.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,069 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,069 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.23× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.6× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 79 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 492 K (219 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 14 times as much starlight as Earth does.
