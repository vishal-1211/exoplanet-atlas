HAT-P-44 c

HAT-P-44 c is a gas giant exoplanet 1,135 light-years away, orbiting HAT-P-44. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.4 years. It is 13 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.4 years, so a single orbit takes 2.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 1,135 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,135 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13× Earth's radius, or 1.16× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.57% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 4× Jupiter in mass (1,271× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 188 K (-85 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HAT-P-44, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,295 K.
  • HAT-P-44 has 2 known planets.
