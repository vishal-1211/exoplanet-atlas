OGLE-2006-BLG-109L c

OGLE-2006-BLG-109L c is a gas giant exoplanet 4,925 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2006-BLG-109L. Discovered in 2008 by microlensing. A year there lasts 13.5 years. It is 11.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 13.5 years, so a single orbit takes 13.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 4,925 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 4,925 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.1× Earth's radius, or 0.99× Jupiter's.
  • It is 86× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 59 K (-214 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • OGLE-2006-BLG-109L has 2 known planets.
  • It was one of 62 planets discovered in 2008.
