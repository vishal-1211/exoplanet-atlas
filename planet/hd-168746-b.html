HD 168746 b

HD 168746 b is a gas giant exoplanet 136 light-years away, orbiting HD 168746. Discovered in 2002 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 6.4 days. It is 11.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.4 days, so about 57 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 135.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 136 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.1× Earth's radius, or 0.99× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.87% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 85.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 900 K (627 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 224.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.07 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
