HD 221416 b

HD 221416 b is a gas giant exoplanet 312 light-years away, orbiting HD 221416. Discovered in 2019 by transit. A year there lasts 14.3 days. It is 9.17 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 14.3 days, so about 25.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 311.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 312 years ago.
  • Its radius is 9.17× Earth's radius, or 0.82× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 60.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.43 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,315 K (1,042 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 495.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
