HD 134606 b

HD 134606 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 87 light-years away, orbiting HD 134606. Discovered in 2024 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 12.1 days. It is 2.97 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 12.1 days, so about 30.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 87.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 87 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.97× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 9.09× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 895 K (621 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 106.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.105 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
