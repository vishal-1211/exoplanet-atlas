HIP 67851 c

HIP 67851 c is a gas giant exoplanet 209 light-years away, orbiting HIP 67851. Discovered in 2015 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 8.6 years. It is 12.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.6 years, so a single orbit takes 8.6 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 209.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 209 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.7× Earth's radius, or 1.13× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 6.94× Jupiter in mass (2,205× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 255 K (-18 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HIP 67851, a orange (K) star at 4,840 K.
  • HIP 67851 has 2 known planets.
