Kepler-385 b

Kepler-385 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 4,673 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-385. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 10 days. It is 2.73 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 10 days, so about 36.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 4,673 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 4,673 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.73× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 12.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 951 K (678 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 143.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.097 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
