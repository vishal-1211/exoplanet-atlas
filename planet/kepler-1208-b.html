Kepler-1208 b

Kepler-1208 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 2,691 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1208. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 11.1 days. It is 2.32 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11.1 days, so about 32.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,691 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,691 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.32× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.99× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 665 K (392 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 37.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.09 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
