KOI-7913 b

KOI-7913 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 885 light-years away, orbiting KOI-7913 A. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 24.3 days. It is 2.34 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 24.3 days, so about 15 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 884.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 885 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.34× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.08× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 432 K (159 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 8.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.146 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
