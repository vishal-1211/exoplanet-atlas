Kepler-1031 b

Kepler-1031 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 2,227 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1031. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 1.2 days. It is 0.87 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.2 days, so about 297.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,227 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,227 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.87× Earth's radius).
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0051% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.59× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,024 K (1,751 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 3,974 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.022 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
