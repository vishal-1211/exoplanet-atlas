HAT-P-38 b

HAT-P-38 b is a gas giant exoplanet 832 light-years away, orbiting HAT-P-38. Discovered in 2012 by transit. A year there lasts 4.6 days. It is 9.25 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.6 days, so about 78.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 832.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 832 years ago.
  • Its radius is 9.25× Earth's radius, or 0.82× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.84% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 84.9× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.59 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,082 K (809 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 243 times as much starlight as Earth does.
