HD 220842 b

HD 220842 b is a gas giant exoplanet 211 light-years away, orbiting HD 220842. Discovered in 2016 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 218.5 days. It is 13.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 218.5 days, so about 1.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 210.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 211 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.1× Earth's radius, or 1.17× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.72% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.18× Jupiter in mass (1,011× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 398 K (124 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 4.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 220842, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,960 K.
