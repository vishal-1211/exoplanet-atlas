Luhman 16 b

Luhman 16 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet, orbiting Luhman 16 A. Discovered in 2017 by imaging. A year there lasts 26.5 years. It is 11.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 26.5 years, so a single orbit takes 26.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • Its radius is 11.9× Earth's radius, or 1.06× Jupiter's.
  • At 29.4× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It was one of 152 planets discovered in 2017.
