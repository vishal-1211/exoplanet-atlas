WASP-79 b

WASP-79 b is a gas giant exoplanet 805 light-years away, orbiting WASP-79. Discovered in 2012 by transit. A year there lasts 3.7 days. It is 17.15 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.7 days, so about 99.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 804.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 805 years ago.
  • Its radius is 17.1× Earth's radius, or 1.53× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.85× Jupiter in mass (270.2× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.3 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,716 K (1,443 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,646 times as much starlight as Earth does.
