WASP-94 B b

WASP-94 B b is a gas giant exoplanet 681 light-years away, orbiting WASP-94 B. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2 days. It is 14.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2 days, so about 181.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 680.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 681 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.1× Earth's radius, or 1.26× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.91% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.62× Jupiter in mass (196.4× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.39 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,849 K (1,576 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 2,000 times as much starlight as Earth does.
