EPIC 249893012 c

EPIC 249893012 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 1,048 light-years away, orbiting EPIC 249893012. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 15.6 days. It is 3.67 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 15.6 days, so about 23.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,048 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,048 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.67× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 14.7× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 990 K (717 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 160 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.13 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
