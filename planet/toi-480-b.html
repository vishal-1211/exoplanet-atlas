TOI-480 b

TOI-480 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 178 light-years away, orbiting TOI-480. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 6.9 days. It is 2.85 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.9 days, so about 53.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 177.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 178 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.85× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 20.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,199 K (926 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 531.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.077 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
