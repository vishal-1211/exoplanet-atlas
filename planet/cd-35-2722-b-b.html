CD-35 2722 B b

CD-35 2722 B b is a gas giant exoplanet, orbiting CD-35 2722 B. Discovered in 2026 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 171.1 days. It is 13.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 171.1 days, so about 2.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • Its radius is 13.9× Earth's radius, or 1.24× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 0.92× Jupiter in mass (292.4× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.6 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 77 K (-196 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.6% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 0.2 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits CD-35 2722 B, a ultracool dwarf star at 1,800 K.
