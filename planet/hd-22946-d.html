HD 22946 d

HD 22946 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 205 light-years away, orbiting HD 22946. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 47.4 days. It is 2.61 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 47.4 days, so about 7.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 204.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 205 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.61× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 26.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 8.3 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 546 K (273 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 18.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
