WASP-41 b

WASP-41 b is a gas giant exoplanet 533 light-years away, orbiting WASP-41. Discovered in 2011 by transit. A year there lasts 3.1 days. It is 12.33 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.1 days, so about 119.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 532.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 533 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.85% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.85× Jupiter in mass (270.2× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.79 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,244 K (971 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 344.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
