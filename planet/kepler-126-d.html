Kepler-126 d

Kepler-126 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 774 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-126. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 100.3 days. It is 2.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 100.3 days, so about 3.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 774.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 774 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.5× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 54.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 19.3 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 475 K (202 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 14.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
