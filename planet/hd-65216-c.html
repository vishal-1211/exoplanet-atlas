HD 65216 c

HD 65216 c is a gas giant exoplanet 115 light-years away, orbiting HD 65216. Discovered in 2013 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 19.2 years. It is 13.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 19.2 years, so a single orbit takes 19.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 114.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 115 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.1× Earth's radius, or 1.17× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.86% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.32× Jupiter in mass (1,055× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 97 K (-176 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 1.5% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 65216, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,645 K.
