DMPP-2 c

DMPP-2 c is a gas giant exoplanet 450 light-years away, orbiting DMPP-2. Discovered in 2026 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.2 days. It is 8.26 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.2 days, so about 115.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 449.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 450 years ago.
  • Its radius is 8.26× Earth's radius, or 0.74× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.18% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 51.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.51 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,911 K (1,638 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives 553.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
