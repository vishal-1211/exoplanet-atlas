Kepler-1388 e

Kepler-1388 e is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,480 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1388. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 37.6 days. It is 2.46 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 37.6 days, so about 9.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,480 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,480 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.46× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.14% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.62× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 299 K (26 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.186 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
