K2-413 b

K2-413 b is a sub-earth exoplanet 930 light-years away, orbiting K2-413. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 19.4 hours. It is 0.76 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 19.4 hours.
  • It is 930.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 930 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.76× Earth's radius).
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0099% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.36× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,535 K (1,262 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 921.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.012 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
