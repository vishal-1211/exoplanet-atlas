K2-223 c

K2-223 c is a super-earth exoplanet 648 light-years away, orbiting K2-223. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 4.6 days. It is 1.41 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.6 days, so about 80 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 647.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 648 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.41× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.2× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 2.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 148 kg.
  • Its average density is 8.3 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,191 K (918 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
