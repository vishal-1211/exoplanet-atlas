GJ 514 b

GJ 514 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 25 light-years away, orbiting GJ 514. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 140.4 days. It is 2.13 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 140.4 days, so about 2.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 24.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 25 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.13× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.2× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 80 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 202 K (-71 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits GJ 514, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,728 K.
