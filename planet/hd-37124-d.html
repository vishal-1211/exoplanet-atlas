HD 37124 d

HD 37124 d is a gas giant exoplanet 103 light-years away, orbiting HD 37124. Discovered in 2005 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 5.1 years. It is 14 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.1 years, so a single orbit takes 5.1 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 103.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 103 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14× Earth's radius, or 1.25× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.82% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.7× Jupiter in mass (221.2× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 160 K (-113 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 9.8% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 37124, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,699 K.
