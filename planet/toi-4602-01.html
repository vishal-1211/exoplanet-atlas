TOI-4602.01

TOI-4602.01 is a mini-neptune exoplanet 206 light-years away, orbiting TOI-4602. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 4 days. It is 2.45 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4 days, so about 91.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 205.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 206 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.45× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.53× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be close to Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 64 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,344 K (1,071 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 664 times as much starlight as Earth does.
