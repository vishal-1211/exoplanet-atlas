GJ 676 A e

GJ 676 A e is a mini-neptune exoplanet 52 light-years away, orbiting GJ 676 A. Discovered in 2012 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 35.5 days. It is 2.47 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 35.5 days, so about 10.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 52.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 52 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.47× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.11% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.67× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 77 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 352 K (78 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
