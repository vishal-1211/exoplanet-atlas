BD-08 2823 b

BD-08 2823 b is a gas giant exoplanet 135 light-years away, orbiting BD-08 2823. Discovered in 2009 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 5.6 days. It is 12.48 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.6 days, so about 65.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 134.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 135 years ago.
  • It weighs 9.28× Jupiter in mass (2,948× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 799 K (526 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 65.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.06 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
  • It orbits BD-08 2823, a orange (K) star at 4,816 K.
