KIC 3558849 b

KIC 3558849 b is a gas giant exoplanet 3,965 light-years away, orbiting KIC 3558849. Discovered in 2015 by transit. A year there lasts 3.6 years. It is 6.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.6 years, so a single orbit takes 3.6 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 3,965 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,965 years ago.
  • Its radius is 6.9× Earth's radius, or 0.62× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.39% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 38.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 196 K (-78 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits KIC 3558849, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,175 K.
  • It was one of 155 planets discovered in 2015.
