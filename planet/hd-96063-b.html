HD 96063 b

HD 96063 b is a gas giant exoplanet 457 light-years away, orbiting HD 96063. Discovered in 2011 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 362.5 days. It is 13.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 362.5 days, so about 1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 456.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 457 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.7× Earth's radius, or 1.22× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.27× Jupiter in mass (403.6× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.86 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 501 K (228 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 10.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
