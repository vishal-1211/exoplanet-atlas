GJ 687 b

GJ 687 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 15 light-years away, orbiting GJ 687. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 38.1 days. It is 4.32 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 38.1 days, so about 9.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 14.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 15 years ago. That makes it the 35th closest of the 6,445 known planets.
  • Its radius is 4.32× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.89% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 17.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 264 K (-1 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.163 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits GJ 687, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,413 K.
