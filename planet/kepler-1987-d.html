Kepler-1987 d

Kepler-1987 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,943 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1987. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 5.6 days. It is 2.66 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.6 days, so about 64.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,943 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,943 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.66× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.12% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 7.56× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 744 K (471 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 72.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.057 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
