HD 158996 b

HD 158996 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 925 light-years away, orbiting HD 158996. Discovered in 2018 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.2 years. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.2 years, so a single orbit takes 2.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 925.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 925 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.00050% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 14× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 960 K (687 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 234.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 158996, a orange (K) star at 4,069 K.
