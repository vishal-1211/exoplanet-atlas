HD 63433 d

HD 63433 d is an earth-sized exoplanet 73 light-years away, orbiting HD 63433. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 4.2 days. It is 1.07 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.2 days, so about 86.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 73.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 73 years ago.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.25× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,040 K (767 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 296.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.05 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
