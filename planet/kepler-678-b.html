Kepler-678 b

Kepler-678 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 5,956 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-678. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 7.3 days. It is 5.14 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.3 days, so about 50.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 5,956 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 5,956 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.14× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.27% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 23.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.94 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 833 K (560 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 113.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
