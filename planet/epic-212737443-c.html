EPIC 212737443 c

EPIC 212737443 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,097 light-years away, orbiting EPIC 212737443. Discovered in 2019 by transit. A year there lasts 65.5 days. It is 2.69 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 65.5 days, so about 5.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,097 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,097 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.69× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.13% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 7.7× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 316 K (43 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.28 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
