HD 56414 b

HD 56414 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 888 light-years away, orbiting HD 56414. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 29 days. It is 3.71 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 29 days, so about 12.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 887.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 888 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.71× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 13.3× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,133 K (860 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,614 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.229 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
