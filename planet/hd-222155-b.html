HD 222155 b

HD 222155 b is a gas giant exoplanet 165 light-years away, orbiting HD 222155. Discovered in 2011 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 10.9 years. It is 13.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 10.9 years, so a single orbit takes 10.9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 165.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 165 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.4× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.44% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.83× Jupiter in mass (581.6× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 165 K (-108 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 222155, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,701 K.
  • It was one of 141 planets discovered in 2011.
