GJ 251 c

GJ 251 c is a super-earth exoplanet 18 light-years away, orbiting GJ 251. Discovered in 2025 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 53.6 days. It is 1.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 53.6 days, so about 6.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 18.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 18 years ago. That makes it the 48th closest of the 6,445 known planets.
  • Its radius is 1.8× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.21% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 3.88× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 216 K (-57 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.196 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits where starlight is in a range that could allow liquid water on a rocky planet. That does not mean it is habitable: its atmosphere and surface are unknown.
