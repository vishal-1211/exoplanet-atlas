HIP 54597 b

HIP 54597 b is a gas giant exoplanet 130 light-years away, orbiting HIP 54597. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 9 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9 years, so a single orbit takes 9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 129.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 130 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 3.41% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.4× Jupiter in mass (762.8× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 94 K (-179 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 1.4% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HIP 54597, a orange (K) star at 4,799 K.
