HN Lib b

HN Lib b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 20 light-years away, orbiting HN Lib. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 36.1 days. It is 2.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 36.1 days, so about 10.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 20.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 20 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.2× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.45% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.46× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 234 K (-39 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.142 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits HN Lib, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,347 K.
