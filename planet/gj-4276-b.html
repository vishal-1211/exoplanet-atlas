GJ 4276 b

GJ 4276 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 70 light-years away, orbiting GJ 4276. Discovered in 2019 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 13.4 days. It is 4.22 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 13.4 days, so about 27.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 69.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 70 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.22× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.9% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 16.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 364 K (91 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.082 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
