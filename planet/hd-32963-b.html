HD 32963 b

HD 32963 b is a gas giant exoplanet 124 light-years away, orbiting HD 32963. Discovered in 2015 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 6.5 years. It is 13.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.5 years, so a single orbit takes 6.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 124.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 124 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.4× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.22% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.07× Jupiter in mass (657.9× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 158 K (-116 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 10% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 32963, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,727 K.
