55 Cnc c

55 Cnc c is a gas giant exoplanet 41 light-years away, orbiting 55 Cnc. Discovered in 2004 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 44.4 days. It is 8.51 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 44.4 days, so about 8.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 41 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 41 years ago.
  • Its radius is 8.51× Earth's radius, or 0.76× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.63% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 54.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 499 K (226 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 10.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.247 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
