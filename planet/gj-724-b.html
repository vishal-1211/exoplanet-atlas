GJ 724 b

GJ 724 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 55 light-years away, orbiting GJ 724. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 5.1 days. It is 3.27 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.1 days, so about 71.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 55.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 55 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.27× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.33% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 10.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 611 K (338 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 23.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.047 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
