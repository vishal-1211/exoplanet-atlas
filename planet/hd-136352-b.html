HD 136352 b

HD 136352 b is a super-earth exoplanet 48 light-years away, orbiting HD 136352. Discovered in 2019 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 11.6 days. It is 1.66 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11.6 days, so about 31.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 47.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 48 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.66× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.72× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.7× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 119 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 905 K (632 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 111.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
