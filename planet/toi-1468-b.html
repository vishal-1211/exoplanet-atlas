TOI-1468 b

TOI-1468 b is a super-earth exoplanet 81 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1468. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 1.9 days. It is 1.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.9 days, so about 194.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 80.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 81 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.4× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.12% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 3.04× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.5× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 108 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 684 K (410 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 36.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
