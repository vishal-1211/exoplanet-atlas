HD 72659 b

HD 72659 b is a gas giant exoplanet 169 light-years away, orbiting HD 72659. Discovered in 2002 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 9.7 years. It is 13.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.7 years, so a single orbit takes 9.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 169.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 169 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.2× Earth's radius, or 1.18× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.79% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.99× Jupiter in mass (949.7× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 155 K (-118 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 72659, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,929 K.
  • HD 72659 has 2 known planets.
