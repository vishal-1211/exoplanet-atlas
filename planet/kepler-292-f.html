Kepler-292 f

Kepler-292 f is a mini-neptune exoplanet 3,446 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-292. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 20.8 days. It is 2.35 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 20.8 days, so about 17.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,446 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,446 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.35× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.12× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 563 K (290 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 24.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.141 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
