TOI-815 c

TOI-815 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 195 light-years away, orbiting TOI-815. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 35 days. It is 2.62 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 35 days, so about 10.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 194.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 195 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.62× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 23.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 469 K (196 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.193 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
