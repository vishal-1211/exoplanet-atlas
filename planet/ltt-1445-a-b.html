LTT 1445 A b

LTT 1445 A b is a super-earth exoplanet 22 light-years away, orbiting LTT 1445 A. Discovered in 2019 by transit. A year there lasts 5.4 days. It is 1.34 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.4 days, so about 68.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 22.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 22 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.34× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.21% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.73× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.5× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 106 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 431 K (158 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 5.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
