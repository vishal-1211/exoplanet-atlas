HD 99109 b

HD 99109 b is a gas giant exoplanet 179 light-years away, orbiting HD 99109. Discovered in 2005 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.2 years. It is 14.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.2 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 178.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 179 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.3× Earth's radius, or 1.28× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.79% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 139.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 239 K (-34 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 99109, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,282 K.
  • It was one of 36 planets discovered in 2005.
