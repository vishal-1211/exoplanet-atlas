kap CrB b

kap CrB b is a gas giant exoplanet 98 light-years away, orbiting kap CrB. Discovered in 2007 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.4 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.4 years, so a single orbit takes 3.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 98.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 98 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.17× Jupiter in mass (689.7× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 319 K (46 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 1.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits kap CrB, a orange (K) star at 4,840 K.
