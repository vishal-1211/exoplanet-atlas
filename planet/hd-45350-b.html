HD 45350 b

HD 45350 b is a gas giant exoplanet 153 light-years away, orbiting HD 45350. Discovered in 2004 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.6 years. It is 13.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.6 years, so a single orbit takes 2.6 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 152.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 153 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.5× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.96% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.79× Jupiter in mass (568.9× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 218 K (-55 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.78). It is 8 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits HD 45350, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,566 K.
