AT2021ueyL b

AT2021ueyL b is a gas giant exoplanet 3,392 light-years away, orbiting AT2021ueyL. Discovered in 2025 by microlensing. A year there lasts 11.4 years. It is 13.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11.4 years, so a single orbit takes 11.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 3,392 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,392 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.6× Earth's radius, or 1.21× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 1.34× Jupiter in mass (425.9× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.93 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • It orbits AT2021ueyL, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,680 K.
  • It was one of 246 planets discovered in 2025.
