OGLE-2018-BLG-0596L b

OGLE-2018-BLG-0596L b is a neptune-like exoplanet 18,428 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2018-BLG-0596L. Discovered in 2019 by microlensing. A year there lasts 2 years. It is 3.81 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 18,428 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 18,428 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.81× Earth's radius.
  • It is 13.9× Earth's mass.
  • It was one of 194 planets discovered in 2019.
