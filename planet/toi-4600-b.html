TOI-4600 b

TOI-4600 b is a gas giant exoplanet 705 light-years away, orbiting TOI-4600. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 82.7 days. It is 6.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 82.7 days, so about 4.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 704.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 705 years ago.
  • Its radius is 6.8× Earth's radius, or 0.61× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.59% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.02× Jupiter in mass (959.8× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 347 K (74 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 3.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.349 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
