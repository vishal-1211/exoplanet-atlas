HD 95086 b

HD 95086 b is a gas giant exoplanet 281 light-years away, orbiting HD 95086. Discovered in 2013 by imaging. A year there lasts 328.6 years. It is 12.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 328.6 years, so a single orbit takes 328.6 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 281.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 281 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.9× Earth's radius, or 1.15× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.63% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 5× Jupiter in mass (1,589× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,000 K (727 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives only 0.21% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 56 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
