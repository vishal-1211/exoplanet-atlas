GJ 3929 c

GJ 3929 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 52 light-years away, orbiting GJ 3929. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 15 days. It is 2.26 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 15 days, so about 24.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 51.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 52 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.26× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.42% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.71× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 317 K (44 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 1.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.081 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
