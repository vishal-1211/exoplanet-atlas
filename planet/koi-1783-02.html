KOI-1783.02

KOI-1783.02 is a neptune-like exoplanet 2,824 light-years away, orbiting KOI-1783. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 284.2 days. It is 5.44 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 284.2 days, so about 1.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,824 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,824 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.44× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.19% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 15× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.51 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 321 K (48 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
