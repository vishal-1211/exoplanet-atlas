GJ 676 A d

GJ 676 A d is a super-earth exoplanet 52 light-years away, orbiting GJ 676 A. Discovered in 2012 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.6 days. It is 1.78 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.6 days, so about 101.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 52.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 52 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.78× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 3.81× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 757 K (484 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 59.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.039 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
