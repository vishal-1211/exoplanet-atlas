Kepler-653 c

Kepler-653 c is a sub-earth exoplanet 1,943 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-653. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 21.6 hours. It is 0.78 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 21.6 hours.
  • It is 1,943 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,943 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.78× Earth's radius).
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0036% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.4× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 0.66× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 46 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,078 K (1,805 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 3,743 times as much starlight as Earth does.
