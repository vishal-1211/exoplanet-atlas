Kepler-164 d

Kepler-164 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 2,905 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-164. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 29 days. It is 2.45 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 29 days, so about 12.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,905 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,905 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.45× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.57× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 637 K (364 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 35.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.187 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
