HD 67087 b

HD 67087 b is a gas giant exoplanet 250 light-years away, orbiting HD 67087. Discovered in 2015 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 352.2 days. It is 13.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 352.2 days, so about 1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 249.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 250 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.1× Earth's radius, or 1.17× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.6% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.06× Jupiter in mass (973× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 366 K (93 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 67087, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,330 K.
