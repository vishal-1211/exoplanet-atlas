1RXS J160929.1-210524 b

1RXS J160929.1-210524 b is a gas giant exoplanet 454 light-years away, orbiting 1RXS J160929.1-210524. Discovered in 2008 by imaging. A year there lasts 6,502 years. It is 18.65 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6,502 years, so a single orbit takes 6,502 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 453.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 454 years ago.
  • Its radius is 18.6× Earth's radius, or 1.66× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.69% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 9.44× Jupiter in mass (3,000× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,700 K (1,427 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 330 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
