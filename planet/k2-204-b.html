K2-204 b

K2-204 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,883 light-years away, orbiting K2-204. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 7.1 days. It is 3.17 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.1 days, so about 51.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,883 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,883 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.17× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 10.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,148 K (875 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 290.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.074 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
