K2-63 c

K2-63 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 1,688 light-years away, orbiting K2-63. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 25.5 days. It is 3.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 25.5 days, so about 14.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,688 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,688 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.9× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 14.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 959 K (686 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 139 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.189 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
