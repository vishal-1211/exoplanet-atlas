OGLE-2012-BLG-0724L b

OGLE-2012-BLG-0724L b is a gas giant exoplanet 21,852 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2012-BLG-0724L. Discovered in 2016 by microlensing. A year there lasts 3.8 years. It is 14.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.8 years, so a single orbit takes 3.8 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 21,852 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 21,852 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.3× Earth's radius, or 1.28× Jupiter's.
  • It is 150× Earth's mass.
  • It was one of 1,504 planets discovered in 2016.
