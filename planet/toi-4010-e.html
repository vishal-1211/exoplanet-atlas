TOI-4010 e

TOI-4010 e is a gas giant exoplanet 579 light-years away, orbiting TOI-4010. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.1 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.1 years, so a single orbit takes 2.1 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 578.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 579 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.18× Jupiter in mass (692× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 174 K (-99 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits TOI-4010, a orange (K) star at 4,960 K.
  • TOI-4010 has 4 known planets.
