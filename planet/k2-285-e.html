K2-285 e

K2-285 e is a super-earth exoplanet 505 light-years away, orbiting K2-285. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 14.8 days. It is 1.95 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 14.8 days, so about 24.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 505.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 505 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.95× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 10.7× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 2.8× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 197 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 501 K (228 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 10.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
