HD 3167 d

HD 3167 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 154 light-years away, orbiting HD 3167. Discovered in 2017 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 8.4 days. It is 2.61 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.4 days, so about 43.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 154.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 154 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.61× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 7.3× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 865 K (592 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 88.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.077 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
