HD 136118 b

HD 136118 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 168 light-years away, orbiting HD 136118. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.3 years. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.3 years, so a single orbit takes 3.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 167.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 168 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.45% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 13.1× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 250 K (-24 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 136118, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,097 K.
  • It was one of 367 planets discovered in 2022.
