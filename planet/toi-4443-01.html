TOI-4443.01

TOI-4443.01 is a super-earth exoplanet 183 light-years away, orbiting TOI-4443. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 1.8 days. It is 1.72 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.8 days, so about 197.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 182.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 183 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.72× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 3.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,639 K (1,366 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,194 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.03 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
