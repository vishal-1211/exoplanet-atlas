HD 20003 c

HD 20003 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 136 light-years away, orbiting HD 20003. Discovered in 2019 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 33.9 days. It is 3.89 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 33.9 days, so about 10.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 136.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 136 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.89× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 14.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 574 K (301 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 18.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.196 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
