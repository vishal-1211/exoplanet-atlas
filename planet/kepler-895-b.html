Kepler-895 b

Kepler-895 b is a super-earth exoplanet 1,762 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-895. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 2.8 days. It is 1.54 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.8 days, so about 130.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,762 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,762 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.54× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.99× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 771 K (498 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 106.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.033 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
