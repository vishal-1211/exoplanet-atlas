TIC 117642575 b

TIC 117642575 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 616 light-years away, orbiting TIC 117642575. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 4.9 days. It is 2.83 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.9 days, so about 73.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 615.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 616 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.83× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.38× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 1,192 K (918 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 336 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.058 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
