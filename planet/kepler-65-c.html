Kepler-65 c

Kepler-65 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 991 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-65. Discovered in 2012 by transit. A year there lasts 5.9 days. It is 2.58 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.9 days, so about 62.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 990.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 991 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.58× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,246 K (973 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 610 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.068 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
