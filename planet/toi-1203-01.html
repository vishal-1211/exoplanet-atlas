TOI-1203.01

TOI-1203.01 is a mini-neptune exoplanet 212 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1203. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 25.5 days. It is 2.92 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 25.5 days, so about 14.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 212 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 212 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.92× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 7.39× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 744 K (470 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 51 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.163 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
