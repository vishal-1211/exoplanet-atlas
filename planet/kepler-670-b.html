Kepler-670 b

Kepler-670 b is a gas giant exoplanet 3,065 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-670. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 2.8 days. It is 13.18 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.8 days, so about 129.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,065 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,065 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.2× Earth's radius, or 1.18× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.49% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,219 K (946 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 528.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.038 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
