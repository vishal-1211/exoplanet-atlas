HD 196067 b

HD 196067 b is a gas giant exoplanet 130 light-years away, orbiting HD 196067. Discovered in 2012 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 9.3 years. It is 12.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.3 years, so a single orbit takes 9.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 130.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 130 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.4× Earth's radius, or 1.11× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.44% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 11.4× Jupiter in mass (3,623× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 176 K (-97 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.56). It is 4 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits HD 196067, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,072 K.
