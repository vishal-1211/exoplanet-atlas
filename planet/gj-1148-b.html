GJ 1148 b

GJ 1148 b is a gas giant exoplanet 36 light-years away, orbiting GJ 1148. Discovered in 2010 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 41.4 days. It is 11.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 41.4 days, so about 8.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 35.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 36 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.9× Earth's radius, or 1.06× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 9.24% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 96.7× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.32 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 233 K (-40 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.166 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
