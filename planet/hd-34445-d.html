HD 34445 d

HD 34445 d is a gas giant exoplanet 150 light-years away, orbiting HD 34445. Discovered in 2017 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 117.9 days. It is 6.07 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 117.9 days, so about 3.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 150.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 150 years ago.
  • Its radius is 6.07× Earth's radius, or 0.54× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.16% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 30.7× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 476 K (203 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 8.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 34445, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,836 K.
