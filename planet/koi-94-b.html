KOI-94 b

KOI-94 b is a super-earth exoplanet 1,556 light-years away, orbiting KOI-94. Discovered in 2013 by transit. A year there lasts 3.7 days. It is 1.71 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.7 days, so about 97.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,556 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,556 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.71× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 10.5× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 3.6× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 251 kg.
  • Its average density is 11.6 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,486 K (1,213 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
