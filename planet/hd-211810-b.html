HD 211810 b

HD 211810 b is a gas giant exoplanet 211 light-years away, orbiting HD 211810. Discovered in 2018 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 4.3 years. It is 14 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.3 years, so a single orbit takes 4.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 210.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 211 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14× Earth's radius, or 1.25× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.29% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.67× Jupiter in mass (212.9× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.43 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 178 K (-95 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.68). It is 5 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
