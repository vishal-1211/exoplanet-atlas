NGTS-11 b

NGTS-11 b is a gas giant exoplanet 621 light-years away, orbiting NGTS-11. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 35.5 days. It is 9.16 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 35.5 days, so about 10.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 621.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 621 years ago.
  • Its radius is 9.16× Earth's radius, or 0.82× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 109.3× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.78 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 435 K (162 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 9.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
