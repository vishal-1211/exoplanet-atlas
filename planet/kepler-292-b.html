Kepler-292 b

Kepler-292 b is a super-earth exoplanet 3,446 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-292. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 2.6 days. It is 1.32 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.6 days, so about 141.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,446 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,446 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.32× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.3× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.3× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 92 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,130 K (857 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 394.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
