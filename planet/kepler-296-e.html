Kepler-296 e

Kepler-296 e is a super-earth exoplanet 545 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-296. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 34.1 days. It is 1.53 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 34.1 days, so about 10.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 544.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 545 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.53× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.09% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.96× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 337 K (64 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits at 0.169 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits where starlight is in a range that could allow liquid water on a rocky planet. That does not mean it is habitable: its atmosphere and surface are unknown.
