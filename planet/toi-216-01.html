TOI-216.01

TOI-216.01 is a gas giant exoplanet 580 light-years away, orbiting TOI-216. Discovered in 2019 by transit. A year there lasts 34.5 days. It is 10.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 34.5 days, so about 10.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 580.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 580 years ago.
  • Its radius is 10.1× Earth's radius, or 0.9× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.53% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.56× Jupiter in mass (178× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.95 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 497 K (224 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 10.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
