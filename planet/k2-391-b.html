K2-391 b

K2-391 b is a super-earth exoplanet 1,657 light-years away, orbiting K2-391. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 4.6 days. It is 1.37 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.6 days, so about 79 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,657 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,657 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.37× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.45× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.3× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 91 kg.
  • Its estimated temperature is 904 K (631 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 111.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
