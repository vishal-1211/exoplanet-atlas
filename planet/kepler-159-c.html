Kepler-159 c

Kepler-159 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,219 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-159. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 43.6 days. It is 3.41 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 43.6 days, so about 8.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,219 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,219 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.41× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.22% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 275 K (2 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 4.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.218 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
