HD 145377 b

HD 145377 b is a gas giant exoplanet 174 light-years away, orbiting HD 145377. Discovered in 2008 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 104 days. It is 12.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 104 days, so about 3.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 174.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 174 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.8× Earth's radius, or 1.14× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.25% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 6.02× Jupiter in mass (1,913× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 443 K (170 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 7.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 145377, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,014 K.
