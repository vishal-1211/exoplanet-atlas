EPIC 211945201 b

EPIC 211945201 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 593 light-years away, orbiting EPIC 211945201. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 19.5 days. It is 5.76 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 19.5 days, so about 18.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 592.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 593 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.76× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.14% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.32 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 817 K (544 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 101 times as much starlight as Earth does.
