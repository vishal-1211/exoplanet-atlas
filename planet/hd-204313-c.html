HD 204313 c

HD 204313 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 156 light-years away, orbiting HD 204313. Discovered in 2015 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 35 days. It is 4.31 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 35 days, so about 10.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 155.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 156 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.31× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.13% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 17.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 635 K (362 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 28.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.211 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
