KOI-319 c

KOI-319 c is a super-jupiter exoplanet 2,153 light-years away, orbiting KOI-319. Discovered in 2014 by transit timing variations. A year there lasts 80.1 days. It is 11.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 80.1 days, so about 4.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,153 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,153 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.9× Earth's radius, or 1.06× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.28% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 27.7× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its estimated temperature is 645 K (371 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 28.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits KOI-319, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,880 K.
