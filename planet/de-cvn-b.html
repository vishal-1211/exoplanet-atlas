DE CVn b

DE CVn b is a gas giant exoplanet 100 light-years away, orbiting DE CVn. Discovered in 2018 by eclipse timing variations. A year there lasts 11.2 years. It is 12.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11.2 years, so a single orbit takes 11.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 99.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 100 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.4× Earth's radius, or 1.11× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 7.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 12× Jupiter in mass (3,823× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 47 K (-226 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.08% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits DE CVn, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,579 K.
