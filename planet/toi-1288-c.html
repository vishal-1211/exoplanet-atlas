TOI-1288 c

TOI-1288 c is a gas giant exoplanet 375 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1288. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.1 years. It is 11.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.1 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 374.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 375 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.1× Earth's radius, or 0.99× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.13% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 85.7× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.35 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 246 K (-27 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits TOI-1288, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,388 K.
