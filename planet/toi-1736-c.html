TOI-1736 c

TOI-1736 c is a gas giant exoplanet 290 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1736. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.6 years. It is 12.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.6 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 290.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 290 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.5× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.64% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 8.81× Jupiter in mass (2,800× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 249 K (-24 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits TOI-1736, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,636 K.
  • TOI-1736 has 2 known planets.
