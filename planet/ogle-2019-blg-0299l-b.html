OGLE-2019-BLG-0299L b

OGLE-2019-BLG-0299L b is a gas giant exoplanet 19,015 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2019-BLG-0299L. Discovered in 2021 by microlensing. A year there lasts 6.1 years. It is 12.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.1 years, so a single orbit takes 6.1 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 19,015 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 19,015 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.7× Earth's radius, or 1.13× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 6.22× Jupiter in mass (1,977× Earth).
  • It was one of 564 planets discovered in 2021.
