HD 1605 c

HD 1605 c is a gas giant exoplanet 290 light-years away, orbiting HD 1605. Discovered in 2015 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 5.9 years. It is 13 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.9 years, so a single orbit takes 5.9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 289.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 290 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13× Earth's radius, or 1.16× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.12% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.62× Jupiter in mass (1,151× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 234 K (-39 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 1605, a orange (K) star at 4,915 K.
  • HD 1605 has 2 known planets.
