KOI-134 b

KOI-134 b is a gas giant exoplanet 3,781 light-years away, orbiting KOI-134. Discovered in 2025 by transit. A year there lasts 67.1 days. It is 12.04 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 67.1 days, so about 5.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,781 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,781 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12× Earth's radius, or 1.07× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.44% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.09× Jupiter in mass (346.4× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 659 K (386 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 45 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.363 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
