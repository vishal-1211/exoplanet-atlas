Kepler-97 c

Kepler-97 c is a gas giant exoplanet 1,308 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-97. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.2 years. It is 13.72 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.2 years, so a single orbit takes 2.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 1,308 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,308 years ago.
  • It weighs 1.08× Jupiter in mass (344× Earth).
  • Its estimated temperature is 216 K (-58 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits Kepler-97, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,779 K.
  • Kepler-97 has 2 known planets.
  • It was one of 872 planets discovered in 2014.
