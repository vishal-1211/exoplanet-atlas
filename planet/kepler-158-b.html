Kepler-158 b

Kepler-158 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,028 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-158. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 16.7 days. It is 2.12 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 16.7 days, so about 21.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,028 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,028 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.12× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.14× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 500 K (227 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 13.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.111 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
