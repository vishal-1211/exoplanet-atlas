HD 7199 b

HD 7199 b is a gas giant exoplanet 118 light-years away, orbiting HD 7199. Discovered in 2011 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.7 years. It is 12.37 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.7 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 117.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 118 years ago.
  • It weighs 11.4× Jupiter in mass (3,639× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 219 K (-54 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 7199, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,371 K.
  • It was one of 141 planets discovered in 2011.
