OGLE-2011-BLG-0173L b

OGLE-2011-BLG-0173L b is a gas giant exoplanet 19,896 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2011-BLG-0173L. Discovered in 2018 by microlensing. A year there lasts 35.3 years. It is 9.01 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 35.3 years, so a single orbit takes 35.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 19,896 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 19,896 years ago.
  • Its radius is 9.01× Earth's radius, or 0.8× Jupiter's.
  • It is 60× Earth's mass.
  • It was one of 308 planets discovered in 2018.
