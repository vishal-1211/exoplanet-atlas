KELT-8 b

KELT-8 b is a gas giant exoplanet 644 light-years away, orbiting KELT-8. Discovered in 2015 by transit. A year there lasts 3.2 days. It is 18.16 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.2 days, so about 112.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 644.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 644 years ago.
  • Its radius is 18.2× Earth's radius, or 1.62× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.3% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.66× Jupiter in mass (209.8× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.19 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,675 K (1,402 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 983.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
