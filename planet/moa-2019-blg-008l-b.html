MOA-2019-BLG-008L b

MOA-2019-BLG-008L b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 8,806 light-years away, orbiting MOA-2019-BLG-008L. Discovered in 2022 by microlensing. It is 12.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • It is 8,806 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 8,806 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.2× Earth's radius, or 1.09× Jupiter's.
  • At 18× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • It was one of 367 planets discovered in 2022.
