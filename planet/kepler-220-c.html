Kepler-220 c

Kepler-220 c is a super-earth exoplanet 558 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-220. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 9 days. It is 1.57 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9 days, so about 40.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 558.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 558 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.57× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 3.09× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 586 K (313 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 37.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.076 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
