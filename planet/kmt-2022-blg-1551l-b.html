KMT-2022-BLG-1551L b

KMT-2022-BLG-1551L b is a gas giant exoplanet 22,505 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2022-BLG-1551L. Discovered in 2026 by microlensing. A year there lasts 4.7 years. It is 13.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.7 years, so a single orbit takes 4.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 22,505 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 22,505 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.2× Earth's radius, or 1.18× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 2.96× Jupiter in mass (940.8× Earth).
  • It was one of 355 planets discovered in 2026.
