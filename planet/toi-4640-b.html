TOI-4640 b

TOI-4640 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 799 light-years away, orbiting TOI-4640. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 2.7 days. It is 2.84 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.7 days, so about 136 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 799.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 799 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.84× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.46× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,379 K (1,106 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 854.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.039 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
