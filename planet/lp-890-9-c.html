LP 890-9 c

LP 890-9 c is a super-earth exoplanet 106 light-years away, orbiting LP 890-9. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 8.5 days. It is 1.37 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.5 days, so about 43.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 105.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 106 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.37× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.65% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 25.3× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 13.5× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 948 kg.
  • Its average density is 54.6 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 272 K (-1 °C), below the freezing point of water.
