TOI-544 c

TOI-544 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 134 light-years away, orbiting TOI-544. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 50.1 days. It is 4.92 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 50.1 days, so about 7.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 134.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 134 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.92× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.52% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 21.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.99 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its estimated temperature is 332 K (59 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
