GJ 1148 c

GJ 1148 c is a gas giant exoplanet 36 light-years away, orbiting GJ 1148. Discovered in 2017 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.5 years. It is 9.71 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.5 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 35.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 36 years ago.
  • Its radius is 9.71× Earth's radius, or 0.87× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 6.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 68.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 99 K (-174 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 1.6% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits GJ 1148, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,287 K.
