HAT-P-11 b

HAT-P-11 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 123 light-years away, orbiting HAT-P-11. Discovered in 2010 by transit. A year there lasts 4.9 days. It is 4.36 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.9 days, so about 74.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 123.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 123 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.36× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.34% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 25× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 838 K (565 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 100.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.053 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
