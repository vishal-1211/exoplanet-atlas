TYC 1422-614-1 b

TYC 1422-614-1 b is a gas giant exoplanet 2,193 light-years away, orbiting TYC 1422-614-1. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 198.4 days. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 198.4 days, so about 1.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,193 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,193 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.5× Jupiter in mass (794.5× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 730 K (457 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 47 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits TYC 1422-614-1, a orange (K) star at 4,806 K.
