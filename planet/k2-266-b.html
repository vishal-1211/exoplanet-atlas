K2-266 b

K2-266 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 253 light-years away, orbiting K2-266. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 15.8 hours. It is 3.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 15.8 hours.
  • It is 253 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 253 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.3× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.18% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11.3× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,515 K (1,242 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 879.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.013 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
