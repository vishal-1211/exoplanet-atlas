OGLE-2019-BLG-0468L c

OGLE-2019-BLG-0468L c is a gas giant exoplanet 14,351 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2019-BLG-0468L. Discovered in 2022 by microlensing. A year there lasts 4.8 years. It is 12.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.8 years, so a single orbit takes 4.8 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 14,351 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 14,351 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.5× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 10.2× Jupiter in mass (3,248× Earth).
  • OGLE-2019-BLG-0468L has 2 known planets.
  • It was one of 367 planets discovered in 2022.
