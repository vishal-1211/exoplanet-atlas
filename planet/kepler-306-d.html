Kepler-306 d

Kepler-306 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 2,530 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-306. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 17.3 days. It is 2.47 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 17.3 days, so about 21.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,530 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,530 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.47× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.66× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 535 K (262 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 22 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.12 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
