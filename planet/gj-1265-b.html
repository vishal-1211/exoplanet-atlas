GJ 1265 b

GJ 1265 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 33 light-years away, orbiting GJ 1265. Discovered in 2018 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.7 days. It is 2.63 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.7 days, so about 100 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 33.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 33 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.63× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.57% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 7.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 424 K (151 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 5.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.026 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
