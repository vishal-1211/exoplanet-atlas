TOI-2141 d

TOI-2141 d is a neptune-like exoplanet 253 light-years away, orbiting TOI-2141. Discovered in 2025 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 60.5 days. It is 3.97 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 60.5 days, so about 6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 253.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 253 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.97× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 14.9× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 491 K (218 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 9.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.291 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
