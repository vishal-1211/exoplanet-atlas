HATS-30 b

HATS-30 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,116 light-years away, orbiting HATS-30. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 3.2 days. It is 13.17 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.2 days, so about 115.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,116 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,116 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.2× Earth's radius, or 1.18× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.29% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.71× Jupiter in mass (224.4× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.54 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,414 K (1,141 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 718.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
