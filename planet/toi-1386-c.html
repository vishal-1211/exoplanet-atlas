TOI-1386 c

TOI-1386 c is a gas giant exoplanet 479 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1386. Discovered in 2024 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 232 days. It is 10.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 232 days, so about 1.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 479 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 479 years ago.
  • Its radius is 10.3× Earth's radius, or 0.92× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.85% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 75.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 327 K (54 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 1.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits TOI-1386, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,735 K.
