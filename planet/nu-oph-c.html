nu Oph c

nu Oph c is a super-jupiter exoplanet 151 light-years away, orbiting nu Oph. Discovered in 2012 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 8.7 years. It is 12 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.7 years, so a single orbit takes 8.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 150.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 151 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12× Earth's radius, or 1.07× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0081% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 24.7× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 341 K (68 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits nu Oph, a orange (K) star at 4,887 K.
