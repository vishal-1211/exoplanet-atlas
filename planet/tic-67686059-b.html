TIC 67686059 b

TIC 67686059 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 813 light-years away, orbiting TIC 67686059. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 3.5 days. It is 2.59 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.5 days, so about 103.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 812.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 813 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.59× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 7.23× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 1,071 K (798 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 219.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.043 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
