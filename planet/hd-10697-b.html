HD 10697 b

HD 10697 b is a gas giant exoplanet 108 light-years away, orbiting HD 10697. Discovered in 1999 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.9 years. It is 12.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.9 years, so a single orbit takes 2.9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 108 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 108 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.8× Earth's radius, or 1.14× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.4% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 5.74× Jupiter in mass (1,825× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 259 K (-14 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 10697, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,641 K.
  • It was one of 13 planets discovered in 1999.
