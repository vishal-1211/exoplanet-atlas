Kepler-169 b

Kepler-169 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 1,326 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-169. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 3.3 days. It is 1.13 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.3 days, so about 112.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,326 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,326 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.13× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.51× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 962 K (689 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 195.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.04 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
