Kepler-411 c

Kepler-411 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 501 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-411. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 7.8 days. It is 4.42 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.8 days, so about 46.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 501.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 501 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.42× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.24% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 26.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 838 K (565 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 56 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.074 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
