Kepler-41 b

Kepler-41 b is a gas giant exoplanet 3,565 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-41. Discovered in 2011 by transit. A year there lasts 1.9 days. It is 14.46 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.9 days, so about 196.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,565 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,565 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.5× Earth's radius, or 1.29× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.56× Jupiter in mass (178× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.32 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,790 K (1,517 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 2,037 times as much starlight as Earth does.
