Kepler-431 c

Kepler-431 c is a sub-earth exoplanet 1,587 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-431. Discovered in 2015 by transit. A year there lasts 8.7 days. It is 0.67 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.7 days, so about 42 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,587 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,587 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.67× Earth's radius).
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0031% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.23× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 951 K (678 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 415.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.085 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
