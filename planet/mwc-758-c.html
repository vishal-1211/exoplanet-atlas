MWC 758 c

MWC 758 c is a not identified exoplanet 520 light-years away, orbiting MWC 758. Discovered in 2023 by imaging. A year there lasts 816.5 years.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 816.5 years, so a single orbit takes 816.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 520.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 520 years ago.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 500 K (227 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives only 0.1% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 100 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
  • It orbits MWC 758, a yellow-white (F) star at 7,314 K.
  • It was one of 323 planets discovered in 2023.
