TOI-5788 c

TOI-5788 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 318 light-years away, orbiting TOI-5788. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 16.2 days. It is 2.27 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 16.2 days, so about 22.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 318.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 318 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.27× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.4× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.2× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 87 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 667 K (394 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 49.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
