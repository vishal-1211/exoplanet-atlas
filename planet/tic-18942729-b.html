TIC 18942729 b

TIC 18942729 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 376 light-years away, orbiting TIC 18942729. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 24 hours. It is 2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 24 hours.
  • It is 375.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 376 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.66× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.2× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 82 kg.
  • Its estimated temperature is 1,467 K (1,194 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 771.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
