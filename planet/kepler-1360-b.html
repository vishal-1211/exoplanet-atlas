Kepler-1360 b

Kepler-1360 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 4,945 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1360. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 40.5 days. It is 3.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 40.5 days, so about 9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 4,945 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 4,945 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.4× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 933 K (660 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 82.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.287 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
