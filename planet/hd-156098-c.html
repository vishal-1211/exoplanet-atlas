HD 156098 c

HD 156098 c is a gas giant exoplanet 157 light-years away, orbiting HD 156098. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 21.5 years. It is 12.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 21.5 years, so a single orbit takes 21.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 157.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 157 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.8× Earth's radius, or 1.14× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.18% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 5.23× Jupiter in mass (1,662× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 177 K (-96 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 156098, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,419 K.
  • HD 156098 has 2 known planets.
