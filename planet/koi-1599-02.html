KOI-1599.02

KOI-1599.02 is a super-earth exoplanet 3,691 light-years away, orbiting KOI-1599. Discovered in 2019 by transit. A year there lasts 13.6 days. It is 1.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 13.6 days, so about 26.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,691 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,691 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.9× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 9× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 2.5× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 175 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 755 K (482 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 76.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
