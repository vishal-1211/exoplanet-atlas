OGLE-TR-56 b

OGLE-TR-56 b is a gas giant exoplanet 922 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-TR-56. Discovered in 2002 by transit. A year there lasts 1.2 days. It is 15.28 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.2 days, so about 301.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 922.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 922 years ago.
  • Its radius is 15.3× Earth's radius, or 1.36× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.39× Jupiter in mass (441.8× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.68 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,212 K (1,939 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 3,942 times as much starlight as Earth does.
