Kepler-434 b

Kepler-434 b is a gas giant exoplanet 3,987 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-434. Discovered in 2015 by transit. A year there lasts 12.9 days. It is 12.67 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 12.9 days, so about 28.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,987 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,987 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.7× Earth's radius, or 1.13× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.71% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.86× Jupiter in mass (909× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,000 K (727 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 158.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.114 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
