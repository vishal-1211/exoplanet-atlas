GJ 317 c

GJ 317 c is a gas giant exoplanet 50 light-years away, orbiting GJ 317. Discovered in 2016 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 17.8 years. It is 13.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 17.8 years, so a single orbit takes 17.8 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 49.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 50 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.6× Earth's radius, or 1.21× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 9.55% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.43× Jupiter in mass (454.5× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 48 K (-225 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.08% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits GJ 317, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,496 K.
