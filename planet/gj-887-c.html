GJ 887 c

GJ 887 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 11 light-years away, orbiting GJ 887. Discovered in 2020 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 21.8 days. It is 2.43 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 21.8 days, so about 16.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 10.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 11 years ago. That makes it the 8th closest of the 6,445 known planets.
  • Its radius is 2.43× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.23% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.5× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 77 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 320 K (47 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
