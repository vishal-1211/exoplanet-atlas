HD 99492 b

HD 99492 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 59 light-years away, orbiting HD 99492. Discovered in 2004 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 17.1 days. It is 5.44 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 17.1 days, so about 21.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 59.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 59 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.44× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.37% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 25.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.87 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 622 K (349 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 23.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
