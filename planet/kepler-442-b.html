Kepler-442 b

Kepler-442 b is a super-earth exoplanet 1,194 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-442. Discovered in 2015 by transit. A year there lasts 112.3 days. It is 1.34 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 112.3 days, so about 3.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,194 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,194 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.34× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.36× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.3× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 92 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 241 K (-32 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits where starlight is in a range that could allow liquid water on a rocky planet. That does not mean it is habitable: its atmosphere and surface are unknown.
