61 Vir b

61 Vir b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 28 light-years away, orbiting 61 Vir. Discovered in 2009 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 4.2 days. It is 2.11 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.2 days, so about 86.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 27.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 28 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.11× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,178 K (905 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 318.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.05 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
