UCAC4 328-061594 b

UCAC4 328-061594 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 130 light-years away, orbiting UCAC4 328-061594. Discovered in 2024 by imaging. A year there lasts 2,400,805 years. It is 12.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2,400,805 years, so a single orbit takes 2,400,805 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 130.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 130 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.1× Earth's radius, or 1.08× Jupiter's.
  • At 21× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • It orbits at 19,000 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
  • It was one of 260 planets discovered in 2024.
