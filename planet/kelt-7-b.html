KELT-7 b

KELT-7 b is a gas giant exoplanet 446 light-years away, orbiting KELT-7. Discovered in 2015 by transit. A year there lasts 2.7 days. It is 17.93 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.7 days, so about 133.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 445.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 446 years ago.
  • Its radius is 17.9× Earth's radius, or 1.6× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.82% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.39× Jupiter in mass (441.8× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.42 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,048 K (1,775 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 1,775 times as much starlight as Earth does.
