TOI-6002 b

TOI-6002 b is a super-earth exoplanet 104 light-years away, orbiting TOI-6002. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 10.9 days. It is 1.65 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 10.9 days, so about 33.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 104.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 104 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.65× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.39% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 3.36× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 321 K (48 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 1.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.057 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
