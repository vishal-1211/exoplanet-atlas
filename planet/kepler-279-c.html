Kepler-279 c

Kepler-279 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 3,384 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-279. Discovered in 2013 by transit. A year there lasts 35.7 days. It is 4.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 35.7 days, so about 10.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,384 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,384 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.3× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.14% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 49.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 774 K (501 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 60.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.224 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
