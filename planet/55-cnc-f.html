55 Cnc f

55 Cnc f is a gas giant exoplanet 41 light-years away, orbiting 55 Cnc. Discovered in 2007 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 260.6 days. It is 7.59 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 260.6 days, so about 1.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 41 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 41 years ago.
  • Its radius is 7.59× Earth's radius, or 0.68× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.5% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 44.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.56 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 277 K (4 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits 55 Cnc, a orange (K) star at 5,198 K.
