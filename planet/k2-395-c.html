K2-395 c

K2-395 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 935 light-years away, orbiting K2-395. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 8.5 days. It is 3.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.5 days, so about 43 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 935.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 935 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.4× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.16% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 755 K (482 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 54.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.08 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
