GJ 876 b

GJ 876 b is a gas giant exoplanet 15 light-years away, orbiting GJ 876. Discovered in 1998 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 61.1 days. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 61.1 days, so about 6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 15.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 15 years ago. That makes it the 37th closest of the 6,445 known planets.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 16.5% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.28× Jupiter in mass (723.2× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 191 K (-83 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.208 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits GJ 876, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,294 K.
