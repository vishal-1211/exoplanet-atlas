LHS 3154 b

LHS 3154 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 51 light-years away, orbiting LHS 3154. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.7 days. It is 3.69 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.7 days, so about 98.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 51.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 51 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.69× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 5.78% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 13.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 344 K (71 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 3.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.023 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
