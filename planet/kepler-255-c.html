Kepler-255 c

Kepler-255 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 3,433 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-255. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 9.9 days. It is 2.99 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.9 days, so about 36.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,433 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,433 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.99× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.09% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 9.22× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 807 K (534 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 83.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.092 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
