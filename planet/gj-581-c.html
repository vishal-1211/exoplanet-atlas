GJ 581 c

GJ 581 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 21 light-years away, orbiting GJ 581. Discovered in 2007 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 12.9 days. It is 2.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 12.9 days, so about 28.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 20.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 21 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.5× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.57% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.81× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 346 K (73 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.072 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
