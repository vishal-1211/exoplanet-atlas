TOI-431 c

TOI-431 c is a super-earth exoplanet 106 light-years away, orbiting TOI-431. Discovered in 2021 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 4.8 days. It is 1.49 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.8 days, so about 75.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 106.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 106 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.49× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.83× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 867 K (594 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 100.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.052 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
