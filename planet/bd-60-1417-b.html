BD+60 1417 b

BD+60 1417 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 147 light-years away, orbiting BD+60 1417. Discovered in 2021 by imaging. A year there lasts 67,756 years. It is 14.68 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 67,756 years, so a single orbit takes 67,756 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 146.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 147 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.7× Earth's radius, or 1.31× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.85% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 15× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,303 K (1,030 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 1,662 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
