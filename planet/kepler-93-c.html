Kepler-93 c

Kepler-93 c is a gas giant exoplanet 313 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-93. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 4 years. It is 13.12 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4 years, so a single orbit takes 4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 312.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 313 years ago.
  • It weighs 3× Jupiter in mass (954× Earth).
  • Its estimated temperature is 168 K (-105 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits Kepler-93, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,669 K.
  • Kepler-93 has 2 known planets.
  • It was one of 872 planets discovered in 2014.
