TIC 38828280 b

TIC 38828280 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 934 light-years away, orbiting TIC 38828280. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 4.9 days. It is 2.63 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.9 days, so about 75.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 934.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 934 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.63× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.11% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 7.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 910 K (637 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 114.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.053 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
