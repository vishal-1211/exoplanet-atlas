EPIC 206042996 c

EPIC 206042996 c is an earth-sized exoplanet 1,473 light-years away, orbiting EPIC 206042996. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 8.5 hours. It is 1.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 8.5 hours.
  • It is 1,473 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,473 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.2× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.87× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 1,558 K (1,285 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 981 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.0081 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
