WASP-117 b

WASP-117 b is a gas giant exoplanet 515 light-years away, orbiting WASP-117. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 10 days. It is 11.88 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 10 days, so about 36.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 515.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 515 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.9× Earth's radius, or 1.06× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.8% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 95.3× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,001 K (728 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 706.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.095 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
