K2-229 b

K2-229 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 335 light-years away, orbiting K2-229. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 14 hours. It is 1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 14 hours.
  • It is 334.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 335 years ago.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.55× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 2.5× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 179 kg.
  • Its average density is 14.1 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,818 K (1,545 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
