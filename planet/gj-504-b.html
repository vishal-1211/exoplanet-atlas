GJ 504 b

GJ 504 b is a gas giant exoplanet 57 light-years away, orbiting GJ 504. Discovered in 2013 by imaging. A year there lasts 259.7 years. It is 12.11 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 259.7 years, so a single orbit takes 259.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 57.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 57 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.1× Earth's radius, or 1.08× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.77% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 4× Jupiter in mass (1,271× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 510 K (237 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives only 0.11% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 44 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
