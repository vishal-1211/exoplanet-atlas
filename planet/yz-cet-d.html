YZ Cet d

YZ Cet d is an earth-sized exoplanet 12 light-years away, orbiting YZ Cet. Discovered in 2017 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 4.7 days. It is 1.03 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.7 days, so about 78.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 12.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 12 years ago. That makes it the 23rd closest of the 6,445 known planets.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.36% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.09× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 357 K (84 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.029 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
