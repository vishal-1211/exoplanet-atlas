AF Lep b

AF Lep b is a gas giant exoplanet 88 light-years away, orbiting AF Lep. Discovered in 2023 by imaging. A year there lasts 24.4 years. It is 13 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 24.4 years, so a single orbit takes 24.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 87.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 88 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13× Earth's radius, or 1.16× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.91% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.61× Jupiter in mass (1,147× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,400 K (1,127 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives only 2.4% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits AF Lep, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,140 K.
