HD 190228 b

HD 190228 b is a gas giant exoplanet 205 light-years away, orbiting HD 190228. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.1 years. It is 12.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.1 years, so a single orbit takes 3.1 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 204.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 205 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.9× Earth's radius, or 1.15× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.21% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 5.03× Jupiter in mass (1,599× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 268 K (-5 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.56). It is 4 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits HD 190228, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,360 K.
