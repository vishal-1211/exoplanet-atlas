WISE J033605.05-014350.4 b

WISE J033605.05-014350.4 b is a gas giant exoplanet, orbiting WISE J033605.05-014350.4. Discovered in 2023 by imaging. A year there lasts 7 years. It is 12.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7 years, so a single orbit takes 7 times longer than Earth's.
  • Its radius is 12.6× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 8.25× Jupiter in mass (2,622× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 325 K (52 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits WISE J033605.05-014350.4, a ultracool dwarf star at 415 K.
  • It was one of 323 planets discovered in 2023.
