HD 83443 c

HD 83443 c is a gas giant exoplanet 133 light-years away, orbiting HD 83443. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 22.6 years. It is 13.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 22.6 years, so a single orbit takes 22.6 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 133.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 133 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.6× Earth's radius, or 1.21× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.76% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.5× Jupiter in mass (476.7× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 90 K (-183 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 1.2% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.76). It is 7 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
