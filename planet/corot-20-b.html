CoRoT-20 b

CoRoT-20 b is a gas giant exoplanet 2,753 light-years away, orbiting CoRoT-20. Discovered in 2011 by transit. A year there lasts 9.2 days. It is 9.42 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.2 days, so about 39.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,753 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,753 years ago.
  • Its radius is 9.42× Earth's radius, or 0.84× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.71% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 4.3× Jupiter in mass (1,367× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,024 K (751 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 180.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.09 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
