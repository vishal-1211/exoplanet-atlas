HD 95338 b

HD 95338 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 121 light-years away, orbiting HD 95338. Discovered in 2020 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 55.1 days. It is 4.27 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 55.1 days, so about 6.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 120.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 121 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.27× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.2% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 42.9× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 443 K (170 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 7.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.262 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
