HD 24040 b

HD 24040 b is a gas giant exoplanet 152 light-years away, orbiting HD 24040. Discovered in 2006 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 10 years. It is 12.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 10 years, so a single orbit takes 10 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 152.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 152 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.9× Earth's radius, or 1.15× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.83% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 5× Jupiter in mass (1,589× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 144 K (-129 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 7.8% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 24040, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,802 K.
