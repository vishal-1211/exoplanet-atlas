ups And c

ups And c is a super-jupiter exoplanet 44 light-years away, orbiting ups And. Discovered in 1999 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 241.3 days. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 241.3 days, so about 1.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 43.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 44 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.52% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 14× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 408 K (134 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 4.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits ups And, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,157 K.
