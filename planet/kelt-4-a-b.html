KELT-4 A b

KELT-4 A b is a gas giant exoplanet 711 light-years away, orbiting KELT-4 A. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 3 days. It is 19.04 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3 days, so about 122.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 711.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 711 years ago.
  • Its radius is 19× Earth's radius, or 1.7× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.18% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.9× Jupiter in mass (286.7× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.23 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,823 K (1,550 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 1,840 times as much starlight as Earth does.
