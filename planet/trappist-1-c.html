TRAPPIST-1 c

TRAPPIST-1 c is an earth-sized exoplanet 41 light-years away, orbiting TRAPPIST-1. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 2.4 days. It is 1.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One of seven roughly Earth-sized planets orbiting the tiny, cool star TRAPPIST-1, about 40 light-years away. At least three of them orbit in or near its habitable zone.
  • One year here lasts 2.4 days, so about 150.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 40.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 41 years ago.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.71% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.31× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 76 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 340 K (67 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
