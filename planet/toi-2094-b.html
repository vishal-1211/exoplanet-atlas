TOI-2094 b

TOI-2094 b is a super-earth exoplanet 163 light-years away, orbiting TOI-2094. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 18.8 days. It is 1.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 18.8 days, so about 19.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 163.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 163 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.9× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.21% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.27× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 276 K (3 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits at 0.136 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits where starlight is in a range that could allow liquid water on a rocky planet. That does not mean it is habitable: its atmosphere and surface are unknown.
