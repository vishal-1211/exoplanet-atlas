TOI-5806 b

TOI-5806 b is a gas giant exoplanet 389 light-years away, orbiting TOI-5806. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 3.2 days. It is 15.13 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.2 days, so about 114.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 388.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 389 years ago.
  • Its radius is 15.1× Earth's radius, or 1.35× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.27× Jupiter in mass (721.5× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,621 K (1,348 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,660 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.048 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
