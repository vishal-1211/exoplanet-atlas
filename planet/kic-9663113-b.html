KIC 9663113 b

KIC 9663113 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 5,201 light-years away, orbiting KIC 9663113. Discovered in 2015 by transit. A year there lasts 1.6 years. It is 4.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.6 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 5,201 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 5,201 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.6× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.17% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 19.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 264 K (-9 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives 3.4 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits KIC 9663113, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,065 K.
