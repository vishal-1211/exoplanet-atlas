K2-121 b

K2-121 b is a gas giant exoplanet 553 light-years away, orbiting K2-121. Discovered in 2017 by transit. A year there lasts 5.2 days. It is 7.16 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.2 days, so about 70.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 552.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 553 years ago.
  • Its radius is 7.16× Earth's radius, or 0.64× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.94% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 51× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.77 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 716 K (443 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 62.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
