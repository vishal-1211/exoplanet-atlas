HIP 41378 b

HIP 41378 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 347 light-years away, orbiting HIP 41378. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 15.6 days. It is 2.64 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 15.6 days, so about 23.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 346.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 347 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.64× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.9× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 959 K (686 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 140 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.13 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
