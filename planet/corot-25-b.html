CoRoT-25 b

CoRoT-25 b is a gas giant exoplanet 3,599 light-years away, orbiting CoRoT-25. Discovered in 2013 by transit. A year there lasts 4.9 days. It is 12.11 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.9 days, so about 75.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 3,599 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,599 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.1× Earth's radius, or 1.08× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.87% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 85.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.27 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,330 K (1,057 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 414.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
