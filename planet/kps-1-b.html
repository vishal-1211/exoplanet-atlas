KPS-1 b

KPS-1 b is a gas giant exoplanet 857 light-years away, orbiting KPS-1. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 1.7 days. It is 11.55 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.7 days, so about 214.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 856.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 857 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.5× Earth's radius, or 1.03× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.36% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.09× Jupiter in mass (346.4× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,459 K (1,186 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 729 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.027 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
