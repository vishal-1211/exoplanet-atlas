bet Cnc b

bet Cnc b is a gas giant exoplanet 290 light-years away, orbiting bet Cnc. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.7 years. It is 12.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.7 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 290.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 290 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.6× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.00060% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 7.8× Jupiter in mass (2,479× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,040 K (767 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 274.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits bet Cnc, a orange (K) star at 4,092 K.
