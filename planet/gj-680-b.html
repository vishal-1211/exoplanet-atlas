GJ 680 b

GJ 680 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 32 light-years away, orbiting GJ 680. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 47.3 years. It is 12 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 47.3 years, so a single orbit takes 47.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 31.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 32 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12× Earth's radius, or 1.07× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 5.72% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 25.1× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 37 K (-237 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.03% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits GJ 680, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,563 K.
