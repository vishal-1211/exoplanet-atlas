HD 25912 c

HD 25912 c is a super-jupiter exoplanet 160 light-years away, orbiting HD 25912. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 72 years. It is 12.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 72 years, so a single orbit takes 72 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 160.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 160 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.2× Earth's radius, or 1.09× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.26% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 16.5× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 67 K (-206 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.34% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 25912, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,921 K.
