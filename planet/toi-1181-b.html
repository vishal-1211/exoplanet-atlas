TOI-1181 b

TOI-1181 b is a gas giant exoplanet 988 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1181. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 2.1 days. It is 17.03 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.1 days, so about 173.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 987.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 988 years ago.
  • Its radius is 17× Earth's radius, or 1.52× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.63% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.18× Jupiter in mass (374.7× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.42 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,942 K (1,669 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 14,140 times as much starlight as Earth does.
