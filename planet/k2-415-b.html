K2-415 b

K2-415 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 71 light-years away, orbiting K2-415. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 4 days. It is 1.02 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4 days, so about 90.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 71.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 71 years ago.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.22% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 7.5× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 7.3× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 510 kg.
  • Its average density is 39.5 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 412 K (139 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
