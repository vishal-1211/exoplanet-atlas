GJ 27.1 b

GJ 27.1 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 77 light-years away, orbiting GJ 27.1. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 15.8 days. It is 3.66 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 15.8 days, so about 23.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 77 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 77 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.66× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.42% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 13× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 387 K (114 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 2.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.101 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
