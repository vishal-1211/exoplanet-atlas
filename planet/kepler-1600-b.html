Kepler-1600 b

Kepler-1600 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 3,362 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1600. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 386.4 days. It is 3.13 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 386.4 days, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 3,362 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 3,362 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.13× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.12% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 9.96× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 209 K (-64 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits Kepler-1600, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,214 K.
  • Kepler-1600 has 2 known planets.
