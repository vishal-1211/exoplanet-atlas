Kepler-1855 b

Kepler-1855 b is an earth-sized exoplanet, orbiting Kepler-1855. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 17.5 hours. It is 1.22 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 17.5 hours.
  • Its radius is 1.22× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.96× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.3× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 93 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 849 K (576 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 87 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.011 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
