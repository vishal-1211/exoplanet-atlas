K2-141 b

K2-141 b is a super-earth exoplanet 202 light-years away, orbiting K2-141. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 6.7 hours. It is 1.51 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 6.7 hours.
  • It is 201.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 202 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.51× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.97× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 2.2× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 153 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,103 K (1,830 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 3,260 times as much starlight as Earth does.
