HD 4732 c

HD 4732 c is a gas giant exoplanet 179 light-years away, orbiting HD 4732. Discovered in 2012 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 8.7 years. It is 13.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.7 years, so a single orbit takes 8.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 178.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 179 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.1× Earth's radius, or 1.17× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.36× Jupiter in mass (1,069× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 244 K (-29 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits HD 4732, a orange (K) star at 4,898 K.
  • HD 4732 has 2 known planets.
