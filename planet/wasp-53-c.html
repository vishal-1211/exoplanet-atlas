WASP-53 c

WASP-53 c is a super-jupiter exoplanet 657 light-years away, orbiting WASP-53. Discovered in 2017 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 7.8 years. It is 12.17 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.8 years, so a single orbit takes 7.8 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 656.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 657 years ago.
  • At 16.3× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 110 K (-163 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 2.5% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.84). It is 11 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits WASP-53, a orange (K) star at 4,953 K.
  • WASP-53 has 2 known planets.
