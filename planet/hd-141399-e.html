HD 141399 e

HD 141399 e is a gas giant exoplanet 121 light-years away, orbiting HD 141399. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 13.7 years. It is 14.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 13.7 years, so a single orbit takes 13.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 120.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 121 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.1× Earth's radius, or 1.26× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.87% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.66× Jupiter in mass (209.8× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 142 K (-131 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 6.4% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 141399, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,600 K.
