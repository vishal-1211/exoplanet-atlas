KMT-2023-BLG-0466L b

KMT-2023-BLG-0466L b is a gas giant exoplanet 22,179 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2023-BLG-0466L. Discovered in 2026 by microlensing. A year there lasts 6.6 years. It is 13.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.6 years, so a single orbit takes 6.6 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 22,179 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 22,179 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.1× Earth's radius, or 1.17× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 3.25× Jupiter in mass (1,033× Earth).
  • It was one of 355 planets discovered in 2026.
