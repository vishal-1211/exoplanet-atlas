OGLE-2007-BLG-349L AB c

OGLE-2007-BLG-349L AB c is a gas giant exoplanet 9,002 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2007-BLG-349L A. Discovered in 2016 by microlensing. A year there lasts 7 years. It is 10.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7 years, so a single orbit takes 7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 9,002 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 9,002 years ago.
  • Its radius is 10.7× Earth's radius, or 0.95× Jupiter's.
  • It is 80× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.36 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • OGLE-2007-BLG-349L A is part of a system with 2 stars.
  • It was one of 1,504 planets discovered in 2016.
