2MASS J22362452+4751425 b

2MASS J22362452+4751425 b is a gas giant exoplanet 227 light-years away, orbiting 2MASS J22362452+4751425. Discovered in 2016 by imaging. A year there lasts 4,503 years. It is 12.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4,503 years, so a single orbit takes 4,503 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 226.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 227 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 3.09% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 12.5× Jupiter in mass (3,973× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 10 K (-263 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 230 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
