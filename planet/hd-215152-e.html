HD 215152 e

HD 215152 e is a gas giant exoplanet 70 light-years away, orbiting HD 215152. Discovered in 2018 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 25.2 days. It is 13.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 25.2 days, so about 14.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 70.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 70 years ago.
  • It weighs 3.07× Jupiter in mass (975.4× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 518 K (245 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 12.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.154 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits HD 215152, a orange (K) star at 4,935 K.
  • HD 215152 has 4 known planets.
