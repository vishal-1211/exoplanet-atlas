HD 164922 d

HD 164922 d is a super-earth exoplanet 72 light-years away, orbiting HD 164922. Discovered in 2020 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 12.5 days. It is 1.83 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 12.5 days, so about 29.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 71.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 72 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.83× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 788 K (515 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 66.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.103 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
