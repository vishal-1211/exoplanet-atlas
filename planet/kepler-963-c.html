Kepler-963 c

Kepler-963 c is a sub-earth exoplanet 2,516 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-963. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 22.1 hours. It is 0.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 22.1 hours.
  • It is 2,516 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,516 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.6× Earth's radius).
  • It is the 19th smallest planet in the catalogue by radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0035% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 0.16× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 0.43× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 30 kg.
  • Its estimated temperature is 1,824 K (1,551 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
