OGLE-2018-BLG-1185L b

OGLE-2018-BLG-1185L b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 24,136 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2018-BLG-1185L. Discovered in 2021 by microlensing. A year there lasts 3.1 years. It is 2.83 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.1 years, so a single orbit takes 3.1 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 24,136 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 24,136 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.83× Earth's radius.
  • It is 8.4× Earth's mass.
  • It was one of 564 planets discovered in 2021.
