Kepler-90 i

Kepler-90 i is a super-earth exoplanet 2,767 light-years away, orbiting KOI-351. Discovered in 2017 by transit. A year there lasts 14.4 days. It is 1.32 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 14.4 days, so about 25.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,767 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,767 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.32× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0088% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.3× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 709 K (436 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 128.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.12 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
