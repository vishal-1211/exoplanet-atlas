Kepler-68 c

Kepler-68 c is an earth-sized exoplanet 470 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-68. Discovered in 2013 by transit. A year there lasts 9.6 days. It is 0.98 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.6 days, so about 38 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 470.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 470 years ago.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0051% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.3× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.4× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 95 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,052 K (779 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 204 times as much starlight as Earth does.
