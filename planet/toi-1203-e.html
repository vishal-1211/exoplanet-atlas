TOI-1203 e

TOI-1203 e is a gas giant exoplanet 212 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1203. Discovered in 2025 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 204.6 days. It is 7.31 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 204.6 days, so about 1.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 212 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 212 years ago.
  • Its radius is 7.31× Earth's radius, or 0.65× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.32% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 42.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 372 K (99 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 3.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits TOI-1203, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,737 K.
