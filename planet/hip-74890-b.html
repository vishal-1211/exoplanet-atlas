HIP 74890 b

HIP 74890 b is a gas giant exoplanet 257 light-years away, orbiting HIP 74890. Discovered in 2016 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.3 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.3 years, so a single orbit takes 2.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 256.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 257 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.04% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.4× Jupiter in mass (762.8× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 388 K (115 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 3.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HIP 74890, a orange (K) star at 4,850 K.
