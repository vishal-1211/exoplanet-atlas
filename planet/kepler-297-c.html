Kepler-297 c

Kepler-297 c is a gas giant exoplanet 2,257 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-297. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 74.9 days. It is 6.54 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 74.9 days, so about 4.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,257 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,257 years ago.
  • Its radius is 6.54× Earth's radius, or 0.58× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.43% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 42.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.83 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 409 K (136 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 6.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
