GJ 229 A c

GJ 229 A c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 19 light-years away, orbiting GJ 229. Discovered in 2020 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 121.9 days. It is 2.87 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 121.9 days, so about 3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 18.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 19 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.87× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.33% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.58× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 188 K (-85 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.384 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits GJ 229, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,564 K.
