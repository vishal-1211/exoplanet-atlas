TYC 8998-760-1 c

TYC 8998-760-1 c is a gas giant exoplanet 309 light-years away, orbiting TYC 8998-760-1. Discovered in 2020 by imaging. A year there lasts 5,724 years. It is 12.33 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5,724 years, so a single orbit takes 5,724 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 308.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 309 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.23% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 6× Jupiter in mass (1,907× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,240 K (967 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 320 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
