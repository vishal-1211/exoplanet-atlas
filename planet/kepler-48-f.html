Kepler-48 f

Kepler-48 f is a gas giant exoplanet 1,000 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-48. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 14.3 years. It is 13.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 14.3 years, so a single orbit takes 14.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 1,000 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,000 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.8× Earth's radius, or 1.23× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.29% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.94× Jupiter in mass (298.6× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 96 K (-177 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 1.6% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits Kepler-48, a orange (K) star at 5,190 K.
