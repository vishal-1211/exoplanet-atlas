GJ 900 b

GJ 900 b is a gas giant exoplanet 68 light-years away, orbiting GJ 900 A. Discovered in 2024 by imaging. A year there lasts 1,264,911 years. It is 12.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1,264,911 years, so a single orbit takes 1,264,911 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 67.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 68 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.4× Earth's radius, or 1.11× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.51% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 11× Jupiter in mass (3,496× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1 K (-272 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 12,000 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
