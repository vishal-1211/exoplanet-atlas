HD 187085 b

HD 187085 b is a gas giant exoplanet 150 light-years away, orbiting HD 187085. Discovered in 2006 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 2.8 years. It is 13.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.8 years, so a single orbit takes 2.8 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 149.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 150 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.9× Earth's radius, or 1.24× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.84× Jupiter in mass (265.7× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.55 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 229 K (-44 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives 30.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
