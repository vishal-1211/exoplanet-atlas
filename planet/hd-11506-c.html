HD 11506 c

HD 11506 c is a gas giant exoplanet 167 light-years away, orbiting HD 11506. Discovered in 2008 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 223.9 days. It is 14 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 223.9 days, so about 1.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 167.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 167 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14× Earth's radius, or 1.25× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 127.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.26 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 374 K (101 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 3.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
