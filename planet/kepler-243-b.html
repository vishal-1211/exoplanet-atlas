Kepler-243 b

Kepler-243 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 2,263 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-243. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 5.7 days. It is 2.45 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.7 days, so about 63.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,263 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,263 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.45× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.57× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 850 K (577 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 115.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.062 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
