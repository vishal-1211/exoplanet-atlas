HD 104067 b

HD 104067 b is a gas giant exoplanet 66 light-years away, orbiting HD 104067. Discovered in 2011 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 55.9 days. It is 9.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 55.9 days, so about 6.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 66.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 66 years ago.
  • Its radius is 9.2× Earth's radius, or 0.82× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.17% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 62.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 408 K (135 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 4.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.267 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
