Gliese 48 b

Gliese 48 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 27 light-years away, orbiting Gliese 48. Discovered in 2026 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 39.6 days. It is 2.77 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 39.6 days, so about 9.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 26.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 27 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.77× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.3% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.11× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 271 K (-2 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.175 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits Gliese 48, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,450 K.
