OGLE-2018-BLG-1269L b

OGLE-2018-BLG-1269L b is a gas giant exoplanet 8,187 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2018-BLG-1269L. Discovered in 2020 by microlensing. A year there lasts 9.1 years. It is 14.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.1 years, so a single orbit takes 9.1 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 8,187 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 8,187 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.1× Earth's radius, or 1.26× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 0.66× Jupiter in mass (210× Earth).
  • It was one of 234 planets discovered in 2020.
