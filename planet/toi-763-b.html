TOI-763 b

TOI-763 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 310 light-years away, orbiting TOI-763. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 5.6 days. It is 2.28 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.6 days, so about 65.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 310.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 310 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.28× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 9.79× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.9× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 132 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,038 K (765 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 182.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
