Kepler-129 d

Kepler-129 d is a gas giant exoplanet 1,333 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-129. Discovered in 2021 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 7.2 years. It is 12.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.2 years, so a single orbit takes 7.2 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 1,333 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,333 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.6× Earth's radius, or 1.12× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.49% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 8.3× Jupiter in mass (2,638× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 179 K (-94 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits Kepler-129, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,770 K.
  • Kepler-129 has 3 known planets.
