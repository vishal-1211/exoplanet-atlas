HAT-P-8 b

HAT-P-8 b is a gas giant exoplanet 690 light-years away, orbiting HAT-P-8. Discovered in 2008 by transit. A year there lasts 3.1 days. It is 15.69 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.1 days, so about 118.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 690 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 690 years ago.
  • Its radius is 15.7× Earth's radius, or 1.4× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.84% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.28× Jupiter in mass (406.8× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.58 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,713 K (1,440 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,520 times as much starlight as Earth does.
