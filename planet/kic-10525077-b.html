KIC 10525077 b

KIC 10525077 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 4,766 light-years away, orbiting KIC 10525077. Discovered in 2015 by transit. A year there lasts 2.3 years. It is 5.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.3 years, so a single orbit takes 2.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 4,766 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 4,766 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.5× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.25% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 25.9× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 222 K (-51 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits KIC 10525077, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,091 K.
  • It was one of 155 planets discovered in 2015.
