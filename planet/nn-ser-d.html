NN Ser d

NN Ser d is a gas giant exoplanet 1,682 light-years away, orbiting NN Ser. Discovered in 2010 by eclipse timing variations. A year there lasts 7.9 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.9 years, so a single orbit takes 7.9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 1,682 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,682 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 2.28× Jupiter in mass (724.6× Earth).
  • It orbits NN Ser, a blue giant-type (O) star at 57,000 K.
  • NN Ser has 2 known planets.
  • NN Ser is part of a system with 2 stars.
  • It was one of 93 planets discovered in 2010.
