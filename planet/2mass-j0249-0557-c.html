2MASS J0249-0557 c

2MASS J0249-0557 c is a gas giant exoplanet 215 light-years away, orbiting 2MASS J0249-0557 A. Discovered in 2018 by imaging. A year there lasts 401,488 years. It is 17.37 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 401,488 years, so a single orbit takes 401,488 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 215.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 215 years ago.
  • Its radius is 17.4× Earth's radius, or 1.55× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 25.4% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 11.4× Jupiter in mass (3,617× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,723 K (1,450 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 1,950 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
