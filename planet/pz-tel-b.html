PZ Tel b

PZ Tel b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 154 light-years away, orbiting PZ Tel. Discovered in 2023 by imaging. A year there lasts 120.5 years. It is 11.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 120.5 years, so a single orbit takes 120.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 153.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 154 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.9× Earth's radius, or 1.06× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.75% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 27× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 54 K (-219 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.14% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.52). It is 3 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
