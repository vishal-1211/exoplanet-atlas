K2-273 b

K2-273 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,005 light-years away, orbiting K2-273. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 11.7 days. It is 3.05 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11.7 days, so about 31.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,005 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,005 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.05× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.11% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 9.53× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 849 K (576 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 83.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.073 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
