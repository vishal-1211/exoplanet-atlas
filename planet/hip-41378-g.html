HIP 41378 g

HIP 41378 g is a mini-neptune exoplanet 347 light-years away, orbiting HIP 41378. Discovered in 2025 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 63.2 days. It is 2.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 63.2 days, so about 5.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 346.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 347 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.5× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.81× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 605 K (332 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 22.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.332 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
