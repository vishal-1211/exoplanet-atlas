Kepler-215 d

Kepler-215 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,585 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-215. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 30.9 days. It is 2.39 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 30.9 days, so about 11.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,585 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,585 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.39× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 23.6× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 4.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 289 kg.
  • Its average density is 9.5 g/cm³, denser than Earth. It may have an unusually large metal core.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 527 K (254 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
