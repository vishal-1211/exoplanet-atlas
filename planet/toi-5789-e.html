TOI-5789 e

TOI-5789 e is a mini-neptune exoplanet 67 light-years away, orbiting TOI-5789. Discovered in 2026 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 63 days. It is 3.43 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 63 days, so about 5.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 66.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 67 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.43× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.14% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 11.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 424 K (151 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 5.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.29 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
