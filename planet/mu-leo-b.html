mu Leo b

mu Leo b is a gas giant exoplanet 106 light-years away, orbiting mu Leo. Discovered in 2014 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 357.8 days. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 357.8 days, so about 1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 106.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 106 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.4× Jupiter in mass (762.8× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 704 K (431 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 51.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits mu Leo, a orange (K) star at 4,538 K.
