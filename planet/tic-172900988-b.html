TIC 172900988 b

TIC 172900988 b is a gas giant exoplanet 819 light-years away, orbiting TIC 172900988 Aa. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 200.5 days. It is 11.25 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 200.5 days, so about 1.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 818.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 819 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.3× Earth's radius, or 1× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.55% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.96× Jupiter in mass (942× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 361 K (88 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 5.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits TIC 172900988 Aa, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,050 K.
