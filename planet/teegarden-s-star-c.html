Teegarden's Star c

Teegarden's Star c is an earth-sized exoplanet 12 light-years away, orbiting Teegarden's Star. Discovered in 2019 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 11.4 days. It is 1.02 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 11.4 days, so about 32 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 12.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 12 years ago. That makes it the 26th closest of the 6,445 known planets.
  • Its radius is almost exactly Earth's size.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.61% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.05× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 209 K (-64 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.045 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
