HD 290327 b

HD 290327 b is a gas giant exoplanet 184 light-years away, orbiting HD 290327. Discovered in 2009 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 6.7 years. It is 13.3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.7 years, so a single orbit takes 6.7 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 184.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 184 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.3× Earth's radius, or 1.19× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.64% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.43× Jupiter in mass (772.3× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 140 K (-133 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 6.2% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 290327, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,505 K.
