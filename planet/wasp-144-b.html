WASP-144 b

WASP-144 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,160 light-years away, orbiting WASP-144. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 2.3 days. It is 9.53 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.3 days, so about 160.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,160 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,160 years ago.
  • Its radius is 9.53× Earth's radius, or 0.85× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.16% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 139.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.89 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,260 K (987 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 775.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
