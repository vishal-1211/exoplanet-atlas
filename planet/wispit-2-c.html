WISPIT 2 c

WISPIT 2 c is a gas giant exoplanet 440 light-years away, orbiting WISPIT 2. Discovered in 2026 by imaging. A year there lasts 50.4 years. It is 17.93 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 50.4 years, so a single orbit takes 50.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 440 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 440 years ago.
  • Its radius is 17.9× Earth's radius, or 1.6× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.6% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 10× Jupiter in mass (3,178× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,050 K (1,777 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives only 0.34% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits WISPIT 2, a orange (K) star at 4,530 K.
