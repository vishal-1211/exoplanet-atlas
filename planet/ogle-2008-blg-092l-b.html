OGLE-2008-BLG-092L b

OGLE-2008-BLG-092L b is a gas giant exoplanet 26,419 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2008-BLG-092L. Discovered in 2014 by microlensing. A year there lasts 68.9 years. It is 8.74 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 68.9 years, so a single orbit takes 68.9 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 26,419 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 26,419 years ago.
  • Its radius is 8.74× Earth's radius, or 0.78× Jupiter's.
  • It is 57× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.47 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • OGLE-2008-BLG-092L is part of a system with 2 stars.
  • It was one of 872 planets discovered in 2014.
