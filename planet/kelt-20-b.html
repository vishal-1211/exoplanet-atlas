KELT-20 b

KELT-20 b is a gas giant exoplanet 445 light-years away, orbiting KELT-20. Discovered in 2017 by transit. A year there lasts 3.5 days. It is 19.51 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.5 days, so about 105.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 445 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 445 years ago.
  • Its radius is 19.5× Earth's radius, or 1.74× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.3% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.38× Jupiter in mass (1,075× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.8 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,262 K (1,989 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 4,363 times as much starlight as Earth does.
