GJ 179 b

GJ 179 b is a gas giant exoplanet 40 light-years away, orbiting GJ 179. Discovered in 2009 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 6.3 years. It is 13.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6.3 years, so a single orbit takes 6.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 40.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 40 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.8× Earth's radius, or 1.23× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 11.1% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.95× Jupiter in mass (301.9× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.63 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 65 K (-209 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 0.28% of the starlight Earth gets.
