GJ 667 C c

GJ 667 C c is a super-earth exoplanet 24 light-years away, orbiting GJ 667 C. Discovered in 2013 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 28.1 days. It is 1.77 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 28.1 days, so about 13 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 23.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 24 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.77× Earth's radius.
  • It is 3.8× Earth's mass.
  • It orbits at 0.125 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits where starlight is in a range that could allow liquid water on a rocky planet. That does not mean it is habitable: its atmosphere and surface are unknown.
  • It orbits GJ 667 C, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,350 K.
  • GJ 667 C has 5 known planets.
