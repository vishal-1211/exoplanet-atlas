eps CrB b

eps CrB b is a gas giant exoplanet 228 light-years away, orbiting eps CrB. Discovered in 2012 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 1.1 years. It is 12.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.1 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 227.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 228 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.7× Earth's radius, or 1.13× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0031% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 6.7× Jupiter in mass (2,129× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 854 K (581 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 101.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits eps CrB, a orange (K) star at 4,406 K.
