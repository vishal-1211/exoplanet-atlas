HD 219828 b

HD 219828 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 238 light-years away, orbiting HD 219828. Discovered in 2007 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.8 days. It is 4.59 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.8 days, so about 95.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 238.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 238 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.59× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 19.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,603 K (1,330 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,095 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.049 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
