K2-329 b

K2-329 b is a gas giant exoplanet 761 light-years away, orbiting K2-329. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 12.5 days. It is 8.68 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 12.5 days, so about 29.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 760.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 761 years ago.
  • Its radius is 8.68× Earth's radius, or 0.77× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.93% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 82.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.7 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 650 K (377 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 45.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
