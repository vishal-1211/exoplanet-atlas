HD 181433 b

HD 181433 b is a gas giant exoplanet 88 light-years away, orbiting HD 181433. Discovered in 2008 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 9.4 days. It is 12.79 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 9.4 days, so about 39 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 87.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 88 years ago.
  • It weighs 5.38× Jupiter in mass (1,709× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 733 K (459 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 51 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.083 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • A planet this close to its star is probably tidally locked, with one side always facing the star, like the Moon facing Earth.
  • It orbits HD 181433, a orange (K) star at 4,918 K.
