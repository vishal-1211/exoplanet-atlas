Kepler-849 b

Kepler-849 b is a gas giant exoplanet 2,681 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-849. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 394.6 days. It is 8.08 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 394.6 days, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 2,681 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,681 years ago.
  • Its radius is 8.08× Earth's radius, or 0.72× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.16% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.94× Jupiter in mass (298.8× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 363 K (90 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits Kepler-849, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,950 K.
