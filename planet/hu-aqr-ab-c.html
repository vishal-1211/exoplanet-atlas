HU Aqr AB c

HU Aqr AB c is a gas giant exoplanet 627 light-years away, orbiting HU Aqr. Discovered in 2011 by eclipse timing variations. A year there lasts 12 years. It is 12.9 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 12 years, so a single orbit takes 12 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 626.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 627 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.9× Earth's radius, or 1.15× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 4.5× Jupiter in mass (1,430× Earth).
  • Its orbit is a stretched oval (eccentricity 0.51). It is 3 times farther from its star at the farthest point than at the closest, so its seasons are extreme.
  • It orbits HU Aqr, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,952 K.
  • HU Aqr has 2 known planets.
  • HU Aqr is part of a system with 2 stars.
