Kepler-229 c

Kepler-229 c is a neptune-like exoplanet 2,695 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-229. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 16.1 days. It is 4.92 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 16.1 days, so about 22.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,695 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,695 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.92× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.38% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 21.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 600 K (327 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 21.6 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.117 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
