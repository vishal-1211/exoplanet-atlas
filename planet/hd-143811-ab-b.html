HD 143811 AB b

HD 143811 AB b is a gas giant exoplanet 446 light-years away, orbiting HD 143811 A. Discovered in 2025 by imaging. A year there lasts 320.3 years. It is 15.69 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 320.3 years, so a single orbit takes 320.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 445.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 446 years ago.
  • Its radius is 15.7× Earth's radius, or 1.4× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.73% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 6.1× Jupiter in mass (1,939× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,042 K (769 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives only 0.11% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 63 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
