HIP 79431 b

HIP 79431 b is a gas giant exoplanet 47 light-years away, orbiting HIP 79431. Discovered in 2009 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 111.7 days. It is 13.4 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 111.7 days, so about 3.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 47.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 47 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.4× Earth's radius, or 1.2× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 6.57% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.1× Jupiter in mass (667.4× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 177 K (-96 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.36 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits HIP 79431, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,191 K.
