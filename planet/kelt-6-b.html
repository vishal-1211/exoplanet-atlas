KELT-6 b

KELT-6 b is a gas giant exoplanet 785 light-years away, orbiting KELT-6. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 7.8 days. It is 14.57 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.8 days, so about 46.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 785.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 785 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14.6× Earth's radius, or 1.3× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.59% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.52× Jupiter in mass (165.3× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.29 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,313 K (1,040 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 718.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
