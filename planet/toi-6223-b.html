TOI-6223 b

TOI-6223 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 434 light-years away, orbiting TOI-6223. Discovered in 2025 by transit. A year there lasts 3.9 days. It is 5.07 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.9 days, so about 94.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 434.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 434 years ago.
  • Its radius is 5.07× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.65% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 22.6× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 714 K (441 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 41.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.04 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
