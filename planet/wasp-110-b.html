WASP-110 b

WASP-110 b is a gas giant exoplanet 897 light-years away, orbiting WASP-110. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 3.8 days. It is 13.19 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.8 days, so about 96.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 896.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 897 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.2× Earth's radius, or 1.18× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 154.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.37 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,158 K (885 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 279 times as much starlight as Earth does.
