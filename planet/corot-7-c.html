CoRoT-7 c

CoRoT-7 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 522 light-years away, orbiting CoRoT-7. Discovered in 2009 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3.7 days. It is 2.83 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.7 days, so about 98.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 521.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 522 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.83× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.09% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 8.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,099 K (825 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 242.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.046 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
