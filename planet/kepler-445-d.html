Kepler-445 d

Kepler-445 d is a super-earth exoplanet 415 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-445. Discovered in 2015 by transit. A year there lasts 8.2 days. It is 1.25 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.2 days, so about 44.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 414.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 415 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.25× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.3% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.1× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 305 K (32 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 4.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.044 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
