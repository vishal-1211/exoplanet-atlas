HD 180617 b

HD 180617 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 19 light-years away, orbiting HD 180617. Discovered in 2018 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 105.9 days. It is 3.53 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 105.9 days, so about 3.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 19.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 19 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.53× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.45% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 12.2× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 202 K (-71 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.343 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits HD 180617, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,534 K.
