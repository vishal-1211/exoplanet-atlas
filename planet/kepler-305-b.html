Kepler-305 b

Kepler-305 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 2,833 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-305. Discovered in 2013 by transit. A year there lasts 5.5 days. It is 3.6 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.5 days, so about 66.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,833 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,833 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.6× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.17% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 10.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 836 K (563 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 112.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.057 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
