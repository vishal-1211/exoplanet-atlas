TOI-2267 c

TOI-2267 c is an earth-sized exoplanet 74 light-years away, orbiting TOI-2267 A. Discovered in 2025 by transit. A year there lasts 3.5 days. It is 1.14 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3.5 days, so about 104.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 73.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 74 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.14× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.25% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.55× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.2× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 83 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 374 K (101 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 4.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
