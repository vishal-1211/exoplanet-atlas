G 261-6 b

G 261-6 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 35 light-years away, orbiting G 261-6. Discovered in 2025 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 5.5 days. It is 1.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.5 days, so about 67 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 34.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 35 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.1× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.43% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 1.37× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 79 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 322 K (49 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 1.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
