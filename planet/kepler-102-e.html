Kepler-102 e

Kepler-102 e is a mini-neptune exoplanet 352 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-102. Discovered in 2013 by transit. A year there lasts 16.1 days. It is 2.17 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 16.1 days, so about 22.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 351.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 352 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.17× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.08% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.7× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be close to Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 70 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 590 K (317 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 20.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
