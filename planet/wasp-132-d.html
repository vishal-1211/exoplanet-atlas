WASP-132 d

WASP-132 d is a gas giant exoplanet 401 light-years away, orbiting WASP-132. Discovered in 2024 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 5 years. It is 12.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5 years, so a single orbit takes 5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 400.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 401 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.8× Earth's radius, or 1.14× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.39% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 5.16× Jupiter in mass (1,640× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 107 K (-166 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 3.6% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits WASP-132, a orange (K) star at 4,686 K.
