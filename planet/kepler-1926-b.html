Kepler-1926 b

Kepler-1926 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 2,177 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1926. Discovered in 2021 by transit. A year there lasts 42.9 days. It is 2.31 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 42.9 days, so about 8.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 2,177 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 2,177 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.31× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.06% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.97× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 78 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 451 K (178 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 9.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
