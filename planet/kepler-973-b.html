Kepler-973 b

Kepler-973 b is a super-earth exoplanet 1,071 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-973. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 49.6 days. It is 1.96 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 49.6 days, so about 7.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,071 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,071 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.96× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.05% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.5× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.2× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 82 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 402 K (129 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 7.9 times as much starlight as Earth does.
