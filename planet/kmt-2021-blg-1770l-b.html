KMT-2021-BLG-1770L b

KMT-2021-BLG-1770L b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 22,570 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2021-BLG-1770L. Discovered in 2023 by microlensing. A year there lasts 2.4 years. It is 12.1 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.4 years, so a single orbit takes 2.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 22,570 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 22,570 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.1× Earth's radius, or 1.08× Jupiter's.
  • At 21× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • It was one of 323 planets discovered in 2023.
