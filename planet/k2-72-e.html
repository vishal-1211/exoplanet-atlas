K2-72 e

K2-72 e is a super-earth exoplanet 217 light-years away, orbiting K2-72. Discovered in 2016 by transit. A year there lasts 24.2 days. It is 1.29 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 24.2 days, so about 15.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 216.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 217 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.29× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.13% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.21× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.3× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 93 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 286 K (13 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits at 0.106 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
