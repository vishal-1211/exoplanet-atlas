KMT-2021-BLG-0736L b

KMT-2021-BLG-0736L b is a neptune-like exoplanet 22,831 light-years away, orbiting KMT-2021-BLG-0736L. Discovered in 2025 by microlensing. A year there lasts 10.3 years. It is 4.86 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 10.3 years, so a single orbit takes 10.3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 22,831 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 22,831 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.86× Earth's radius.
  • It is 21× Earth's mass.
  • It was one of 246 planets discovered in 2025.
