HD 191939 d

HD 191939 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 175 light-years away, orbiting HD 191939. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 38.4 days. It is 3 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 38.4 days, so about 9.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 174.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 175 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.09% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its average density is 0.57 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 540 K (267 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 14.3 times as much starlight as Earth does.
