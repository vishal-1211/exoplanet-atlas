K2-240 b

K2-240 b is a mini-neptune exoplanet 238 light-years away, orbiting K2-240. Discovered in 2018 by transit. A year there lasts 6 days. It is 2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 6 days, so about 60.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 237.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 238 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.12% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.66× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 586 K (313 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 20.1 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.051 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
