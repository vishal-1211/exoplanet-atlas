HD 142 b

HD 142 b is a gas giant exoplanet 85 light-years away, orbiting HD 142. Discovered in 2001 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 351.4 days. It is 12.7 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 351.4 days, so about 1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 85.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 85 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.7× Earth's radius, or 1.13× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.63% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 7.1× Jupiter in mass (2,257× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 358 K (85 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 2.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 142, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,245 K.
