47 UMa b

47 UMa b is a gas giant exoplanet 45 light-years away, orbiting 47 UMa. Discovered in 1996 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 3 years. It is 13.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 3 years, so a single orbit takes 3 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 45 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 45 years ago.
  • Its radius is 13.2× Earth's radius, or 1.18× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.99% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 2.53× Jupiter in mass (804.1× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 215 K (-58 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits 47 UMa, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,872 K.
  • 47 UMa has 3 known planets.
