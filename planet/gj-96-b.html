GJ 96 b

GJ 96 b is a neptune-like exoplanet 39 light-years away, orbiting GJ 96. Discovered in 2018 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 73.9 days. It is 4.67 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 73.9 days, so about 4.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 38.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 39 years ago.
  • Its radius is 4.67× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.54% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 19.7× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 258 K (-15 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It orbits at 0.291 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
  • It orbits GJ 96, a red dwarf (M) star at 3,785 K.
