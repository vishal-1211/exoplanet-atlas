HD 17156 b

HD 17156 b is a gas giant exoplanet 255 light-years away, orbiting HD 17156. Discovered in 2007 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 21.2 days. It is 12.26 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 21.2 days, so about 17.2 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 254.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 255 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.3× Earth's radius, or 1.09× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.55% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 3.26× Jupiter in mass (1,036× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 888 K (615 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 68.8 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.163 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
