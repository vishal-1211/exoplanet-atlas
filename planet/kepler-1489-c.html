Kepler-1489 c

Kepler-1489 c is a sub-earth exoplanet 4,185 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-1489. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 16.3 hours. It is 0.51 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 16.3 hours.
  • It is 4,185 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 4,185 years ago.
  • Its radius is smaller than Earth (0.51× Earth's radius).
  • It is the 9th smallest planet in the catalogue by radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.0024% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • Its mass is only 0.09× Earth's, lighter than Mars.
  • Its estimated temperature is 2,142 K (1,869 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 3,506 times as much starlight as Earth does.
