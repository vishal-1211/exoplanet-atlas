TOI-5177 b

TOI-5177 b is a super-earth exoplanet 505 light-years away, orbiting TOI-5177. Discovered in 2026 by transit. A year there lasts 2.2 days. It is 1.93 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.2 days, so about 165.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 505.4 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 505 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.93× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.01% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 4.38× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.2× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 82 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,185 K (1,912 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 3,786 times as much starlight as Earth does.
