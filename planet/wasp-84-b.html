WASP-84 b

WASP-84 b is a gas giant exoplanet 328 light-years away, orbiting WASP-84. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 8.5 days. It is 10.72 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 8.5 days, so about 42.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 328.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 328 years ago.
  • Its radius is 10.7× Earth's radius, or 0.96× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.63% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 0.69× Jupiter in mass (220× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.98 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 732 K (459 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 34.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
