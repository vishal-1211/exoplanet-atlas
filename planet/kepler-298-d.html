Kepler-298 d

Kepler-298 d is a mini-neptune exoplanet 1,690 light-years away, orbiting Kepler-298. Discovered in 2014 by transit. A year there lasts 77.5 days. It is 2.5 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 77.5 days, so about 4.7 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,690 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,690 years ago.
  • Its radius is 2.5× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.15% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 6.8× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 277 K (4 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It receives 1.7 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.305 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
