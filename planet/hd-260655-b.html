HD 260655 b

HD 260655 b is an earth-sized exoplanet 33 light-years away, orbiting HD 260655. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 2.8 days. It is 1.24 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.8 days, so about 131.9 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 32.6 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 33 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.24× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 2.14× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.4× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 97 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 709 K (436 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 42.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
