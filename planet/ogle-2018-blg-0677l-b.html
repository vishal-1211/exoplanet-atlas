OGLE-2018-BLG-0677L b

OGLE-2018-BLG-0677L b is a super-earth exoplanet 24,723 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2018-BLG-0677L. Discovered in 2020 by microlensing. A year there lasts 1.4 years. It is 1.82 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 1.4 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 24,723 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 24,723 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.82× Earth's radius.
  • It is 3.96× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 1.2× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 84 kg.
  • It was one of 234 planets discovered in 2020.
