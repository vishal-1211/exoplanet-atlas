HD 191939 g

HD 191939 g is a neptune-like exoplanet 175 light-years away, orbiting HD 191939. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 284 days. It is 3.74 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 284 days, so about 1.3 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 174.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 175 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.74× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.13% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 13.5× Earth's mass.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 278 K (5 °C), between the freezing and boiling points of water on Earth. Whether it actually has liquid water depends on an atmosphere we do not know about.
  • It orbits HD 191939, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,348 K.
  • HD 191939 has 6 known planets.
