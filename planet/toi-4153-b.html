TOI-4153 b

TOI-4153 b is a gas giant exoplanet 1,382 light-years away, orbiting TOI-4153. Discovered in 2024 by transit. A year there lasts 4.6 days. It is 16.12 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.6 days, so about 79.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 1,382 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 1,382 years ago.
  • Its radius is 16.1× Earth's radius, or 1.44× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.85% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.15× Jupiter in mass (365.5× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.48 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,669 K (1,396 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,212 times as much starlight as Earth does.
