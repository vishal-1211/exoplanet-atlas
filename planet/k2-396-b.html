K2-396 b

K2-396 b is a super-earth exoplanet 896 light-years away, orbiting K2-396. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 16.2 hours. It is 1.58 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 16.2 hours.
  • It is 896.5 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 896 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.58× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 3.12× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 1,990 K (1,717 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 2,614 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.015 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
