OGLE-2015-BLG-0051L b

OGLE-2015-BLG-0051L b is a gas giant exoplanet 26,745 light-years away, orbiting OGLE-2015-BLG-0051L. Discovered in 2016 by microlensing. A year there lasts 2 years. It is 14 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2 years, similar to an Earth year.
  • It is 26,745 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 26,745 years ago.
  • Its radius is 14× Earth's radius, or 1.25× Jupiter's.
  • It weighs 0.72× Jupiter in mass (230× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.46 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • It was one of 1,504 planets discovered in 2016.
