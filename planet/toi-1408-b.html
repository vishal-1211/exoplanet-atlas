TOI-1408 b

TOI-1408 b is a gas giant exoplanet 455 light-years away, orbiting TOI-1408. Discovered in 2023 by transit. A year there lasts 4.4 days. It is 25 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.4 days, so about 82.5 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 454.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 455 years ago.
  • Its radius is 25× Earth's radius, or 2.23× Jupiter's.
  • It is the 6th largest planet in the catalogue by radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.24% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.87× Jupiter in mass (593× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.21 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,293 K (1,020 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
