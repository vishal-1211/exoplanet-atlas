WASP-189 b

WASP-189 b is a gas giant exoplanet 325 light-years away, orbiting WASP-189. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 2.7 days. It is 18.15 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 2.7 days, so about 134.1 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 325.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 325 years ago.
  • Its radius is 18.1× Earth's radius, or 1.62× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.5% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.99× Jupiter in mass (632.5× Earth).
  • Its average density is 0.58 g/cm³, lower than water. If there were an ocean big enough, it would float.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 3,353 K (3,080 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 8,084 times as much starlight as Earth does.
