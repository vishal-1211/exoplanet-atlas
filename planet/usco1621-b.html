USco1621 b

USco1621 b is a super-jupiter exoplanet 450 light-years away, orbiting USco1621 A. Discovered in 2019 by imaging. A year there lasts 257,595 years. It is 12.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 257,595 years, so a single orbit takes 257,595 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 449.8 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 450 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.2× Earth's radius, or 1.09× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.48% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 16× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,270 K (1,997 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives only 0% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits at 2,880 AU, farther from its star than Neptune is from the Sun (30 AU).
