HD 80883 b

HD 80883 b is a gas giant exoplanet 113 light-years away, orbiting HD 80883. Discovered in 2022 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 17.5 years. It is 12.8 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 17.5 years, so a single orbit takes 17.5 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 112.9 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 113 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.8× Earth's radius, or 1.14× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 2.07% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 5.5× Jupiter in mass (1,748× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 89 K (-184 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 1% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 80883, a orange (K) star at 5,178 K.
