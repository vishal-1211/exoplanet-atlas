HAT-P-2 b

HAT-P-2 b is a gas giant exoplanet 417 light-years away, orbiting HAT-P-2. Discovered in 2007 by transit. A year there lasts 5.6 days. It is 10.66 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 5.6 days, so about 64.8 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 416.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 417 years ago.
  • Its radius is 10.7× Earth's radius, or 0.95× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.49% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 9.02× Jupiter in mass (2,867× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 1,540 K (1,267 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives 588.5 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.068 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
