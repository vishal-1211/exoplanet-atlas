HD 73256 c

HD 73256 c is a super-jupiter exoplanet 120 light-years away, orbiting HD 73256. Discovered in 2023 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 7.4 years. It is 12.2 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 7.4 years, so a single orbit takes 7.4 times longer than Earth's.
  • It is 119.7 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 120 years ago.
  • Its radius is 12.2× Earth's radius, or 1.09× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 1.4% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • At 16× Jupiter's mass, it is heavy enough that it may really be a brown dwarf (a failed star).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 134 K (-139 °C), below the freezing point of water.
  • It receives only 5.1% of the starlight Earth gets.
  • It orbits HD 73256, a Sun-like yellow (G) star at 5,570 K.
