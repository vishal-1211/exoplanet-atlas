HD 80653 b

HD 80653 b is a super-earth exoplanet 358 light-years away, orbiting HD 80653. Discovered in 2020 by transit. A year there lasts 17.3 hours. It is 1.62 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • A year here is shorter than an Earth day. It lasts just 17.3 hours.
  • It is 358.3 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 358 years ago.
  • Its radius is 1.62× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.02% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 5.61× Earth's mass.
  • If it has a solid surface, gravity there would be about 2.1× Earth's, so a 70 kg person would feel like 149 kg.
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 2,438 K (2,165 °C), hot enough to melt iron (1,811 K).
  • It receives about 6,130 times as much starlight as Earth does.
