HD 114082 b

HD 114082 b is a gas giant exoplanet 311 light-years away, orbiting HD 114082. Discovered in 2022 by transit. A year there lasts 225.6 days. It is 11.72 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 225.6 days, so about 1.6 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 311.1 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 311 years ago.
  • Its radius is 11.7× Earth's radius, or 1.05× Jupiter's.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.52% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It weighs 1.6× Jupiter in mass (508.5× Earth).
  • Its equilibrium temperature is 950 K (677 °C), above the boiling point of water at sea level on Earth.
  • It receives 192.2 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits HD 114082, a yellow-white (F) star at 6,610 K.
