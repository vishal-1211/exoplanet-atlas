HD 196067 c

HD 196067 c is a mini-neptune exoplanet 130 light-years away, orbiting HD 196067. Discovered in 2025 by radial velocity. A year there lasts 4.6 days. It is 3.21 times the size of Earth.

Interesting facts

  • One year here lasts 4.6 days, so about 79.4 of its years fit into a single Earth year.
  • It is 130.2 light-years away. The light we see from its star left 130 years ago.
  • Its radius is 3.21× Earth's radius.
  • Seen edge-on, it would block about 0.03% of its star's light as it passes in front.
  • It is 10.4× Earth's mass.
  • Its estimated temperature is 1,605 K (1,332 °C), hotter than most lava on Earth.
  • It receives about 1,107 times as much starlight as Earth does.
  • It orbits at 0.057 AU, closer to its star than Mercury is to the Sun (0.39 AU).
