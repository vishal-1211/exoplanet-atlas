About this data and the pictures
  • The pictures are artist's impressions. Almost no planet here has been photographed. Each image is generated from the planet's size, temperature and star, and coloured to match. Real colours, clouds and continents are unknown.
  • Temperatures are equilibrium temperatures: what the planet would be if it only absorbed and re-radiated starlight. Real temperatures depend on atmospheres. When NASA gives none, we estimate one from the star and orbit and say so.
  • Masses found by the radial-velocity method can be minimum values. Only planets with a measured size and mass get a density.
  • Habitable-zone starlight means the starlight is in a range that could allow liquid water on a rocky planet. It says nothing about whether water or life exists.
  • The background is a map of the whole sky. Each dot is a real star that has at least one known planet, placed at its real position.
  • Facts are generated from the numbers, plus short hand-written notes for famous planets. Not identified means astronomers have not measured it yet.
